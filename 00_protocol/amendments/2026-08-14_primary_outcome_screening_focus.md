# Amendment: primary-outcome-focused full-text eligibility

Date effective: 2026-08-14

Requested by: review lead in the active screening session

## Change

For full-text eligibility, an otherwise eligible randomized perioperative TEAS or EA report must provide extractable comparative results for at least one of the following:

1. Cumulative systemic postoperative opioid consumption from the end of surgery to approximately 24 hours, with the opioid, route and quantity reported sufficiently for conversion to intravenous morphine milligram equivalents, or with the necessary information reasonably obtainable from the report or authors.
2. Postoperative pain intensity at approximately 24 hours, at rest and/or during movement, using an interpretable pain scale.

PONV, rescue analgesia without an identifiable systemic opioid quantity, quality of recovery, gastrointestinal recovery, length of stay, persistent opioid use, adverse events and other secondary perioperative outcomes do not independently satisfy outcome eligibility after this amendment.

## Operational rule

- Retain when either primary outcome is reported at an exact 24-hour time point or at a clearly designated postoperative-day-1/approximately-24-hour assessment.
- Exclude as `Wrong outcomes` only when report-level evidence positively establishes that neither primary outcome is available.
- Do not infer absence from the title alone. If the report is unavailable or the timing/metric remains unclear, mark for human review rather than definitively excluding.
- All population, design, anaesthesia, intervention, timing, comparator and separability criteria in the locked protocol remain unchanged.

## Reason

The review lead directed that screening focus on the review's two primary outcomes: 24-hour systemic postoperative opioid consumption and pain around 24 hours.

## Consequence

Include votes cast under the broader original outcome scope require reassessment. Decisions made before this date remain part of the audit trail and must not be overwritten in the locked protocol summary.
