# Amendment: English-language full-text eligibility

Date effective: 2026-08-20

Requested by: review lead following PROSPERO clarification

## Change

Electronic database searches remain unrestricted by publication language. Eligibility for the systematic review is now restricted to studies with a full-text report published in English.

Reports published only in languages other than English are excluded during full-text study selection, with `Publication language` recorded as the primary exclusion reason.

## PROSPERO wording

### Context

Only studies with a full-text report published in English are eligible.

### Search language restrictions

> There are no language restrictions.

This describes the electronic database searches, which continue to be run without language filters.

### Other relevant information about searching and screening

> No publication-date or language restrictions will be applied to the electronic database searches. This is intended to maximise retrieval and avoid language-related loss of potentially relevant records at the search stage. However, eligibility for the systematic review will be restricted to studies with a full-text report published in English. Reports published only in languages other than English will therefore be excluded during study selection and the reason for exclusion will be recorded as publication language.

## Operational consequence

- Do not apply language filters to database searches.
- During full-text screening, exclude reports whose full text is published only in a language other than English.
- Record the Covidence exclusion reason as `Publication language`.
- An English title or abstract does not make a non-English full-text report eligible.
- All other eligibility criteria, including the primary-outcome-focused amendment effective 2026-08-14, remain unchanged.

## Reason

This amendment aligns the operational screening criteria with the clarified PROSPERO registration language while preserving a language-unrestricted search strategy.
