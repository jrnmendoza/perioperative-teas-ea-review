# Locked protocol scope

Status: LOCKED operational summary  
Locked on: 2026-07-21  
Authority: `00_protocol/source/PROSPERO TEAS EA.pdf` (10-page PROSPERO preview supplied by the review team)

This file is a faithful operational summary of the authoritative protocol. It is not an amendment. Any later change must be prospective, dated, justified, and recorded under `00_protocol/amendments/` without altering this file.

## Population and setting

- Adults aged 18 years or older undergoing an operative surgical procedure under general anaesthesia.
- General anaesthesia may be used alone or with balanced regional or neuraxial anaesthesia or analgesia.
- Any surgical specialty; elective or urgent; inpatient, day-surgery, ambulatory surgical, or other perioperative hospital setting.
- Mixed-age studies are eligible only when adult data can be extracted separately or at least 90% of participants are aged 18 years or older.
- Exclude studies involving only children or adolescents; procedures exclusively under local, regional, or neuraxial anaesthesia without general anaesthesia; non-surgical procedures; dental procedures without general anaesthesia; chronic-pain populations without an acute perioperative context; and interventions delivered only after recovery from the acute postoperative period.

## Study design

- Randomized controlled trials only.
- Eligible designs: individually randomized parallel-group trials; cluster-randomized trials when design-adjusted results are available or calculable; and crossover trials only when an eligible perioperative period is validly separable and carryover is implausible.
- Exclude quasi-randomized and non-randomized studies, observational studies, case series, case reports, qualitative studies, reviews, and protocols without results.
- Conference abstracts lacking enough information for eligibility, risk-of-bias assessment, and outcome extraction may be listed as awaiting classification rather than included.

## Eligible interventions

1. **TEAS:** non-invasive electrical stimulation delivered through surface electrodes positioned over recognized or explicitly defined body acupuncture points, without needle penetration.
2. **EA:** electrical stimulation delivered through one or more pairs of acupuncture needles inserted at recognized or explicitly defined body acupuncture points.

Eligible terminology can include transcutaneous electrical/electric acupoint stimulation, transcutaneous acupoint electrical stimulation, electrical acupoint stimulation, electroacupoint stimulation, acupuncture-like TENS delivered at acupuncture points, electroacupuncture, electric acupuncture, or electrically stimulated acupuncture when the intervention meets the definitions above.

- At least one eligible stimulation session must occur before the end of the first 24 postoperative hours.
- Delivery may be preoperative, intraoperative, postoperative, or span multiple perioperative phases.
- TEAS or EA may be used alone or as an adjunct to balanced usual multimodal perioperative care.
- Incomplete reporting of protocol characteristics does not itself exclude a trial, but limits the analyses to which it can contribute.
- TEAS and EA must remain separate modalities in all downstream analyses and must not be pooled together.

## Explicitly excluded interventions

- Manual acupuncture without electrical stimulation.
- Auricular acupuncture, battlefield acupuncture, auricular acupressure, and electroauriculotherapy.
- Press-tack or intradermal needles.
- Wrist-ankle acupuncture.
- Buccal acupuncture.
- Dry needling, trigger-point needling, acupuncture-point injection, laser acupuncture, moxibustion, cupping, massage, and herbal interventions.
- Ordinary TENS or percutaneous electrical nerve stimulation delivered only at non-acupuncture locations.
- Electrical stimulation used only for neuromuscular monitoring, nerve localization, or physiological measurement.
- Mixed interventions, including combined TEAS and EA arms, when the independent effect of TEAS or EA cannot be separated.

## Comparators and analytic separation

- Principal comparison 1: TEAS versus inactive or credible sham TEAS.
- Principal comparison 2: EA versus credible sham EA.
- Usual perioperative care, standard multimodal analgesia, no stimulation, waiting-list control, and attention control are supportive comparisons and remain separate from sham-controlled evidence.
- Active electrical comparators such as non-acupoint TENS may be included only when clinically interpretable and must be reported separately.
- Exclude comparators containing an active acupuncture-point intervention that prevents estimation of the independent TEAS or EA effect, materially unbalanced perioperative co-interventions without adequate adjustment, and comparators that cannot be classified.

## Outcomes and synthesis boundary

- The protocol's main outcomes are cumulative systemic postoperative opioid consumption from the end of surgery to 24 hours, converted to IV morphine milligram equivalents, and pain at approximately 24 hours, separated into rest and movement where reported.
- Additional eligible outcomes are defined in the source protocol and include later opioid and pain intervals, intraoperative opioid use, PONV, rescue analgesia, quality and function of recovery, gastrointestinal recovery, length of stay, persistent opioid use, and adverse events.
- TEAS and EA analyses are stratified by modality. Principal sham-controlled and supportive non-sham comparisons remain separate.

## Search constraints

- Main databases: PubMed, Embase, CENTRAL, and CINAHL.
- Later supplementary methods: trial/study registers, backward citation searching, forward citation searching, and author/expert contact.
- No publication-date restriction.
- No language restriction is applied to electronic database searches. Under the amendment effective 2026-08-20, eligibility is restricted to reports with full text published in English; non-English-only full texts are excluded during study selection as `Publication language`.
- The electronic search must not require general-anaesthesia terminology; general anaesthesia is verified during study selection.
- The electronic search must not require opioid consumption, analgesia, pain, MME, morphine, PONV, recovery, postoperative outcomes, or another outcome concept.
- Search architecture: separate TEAS and EA concepts, combined with OR; then surgery/perioperative and a validated database-specific randomized-trial component. Preserve separate TEAS-line and EA-line counts.

## Locked interpretation rule

Search-validation classification is not a final eligibility decision. Ambiguous modality, anaesthetic technique, timing, comparator, or report linkage must be resolved during the relevant review stage and documented without changing this locked scope.
