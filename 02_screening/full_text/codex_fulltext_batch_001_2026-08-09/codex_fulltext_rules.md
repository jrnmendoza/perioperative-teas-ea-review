## Decision standard

Full-text exclusion requires positive evidence in the report. Uncertainty, incomplete reporting, or difficulty locating a criterion is not an exclusion. When no definite exclusion is established, retain the report and flag any unresolved issue for human review. Use one primary exclusion reason following the hierarchy in the user-supplied protocol.

Eligible reports contain original participant results from a randomized trial of therapeutic surface-electrode TEAS or needle-based EA in adults undergoing operative surgery under general anaesthesia. Treatment must begin no later than 24 hours postoperatively, the TEAS/EA effect must be separately identifiable, an eligible comparator must be available, and at least one eligible clinical perioperative outcome must be reported. Companion reports are retained when useful.

Every registered decision requires PDF-page evidence. Decisions are made one report at a time and verified by disappearance of the unique report card from the active vote-required view. No conflict page or other-reviewer decision may be opened.
