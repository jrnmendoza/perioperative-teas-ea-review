## Eligibility rules applied

- Electronic searching remains unrestricted by language.
- Eligibility is restricted to reports with full text published in English.
- An English abstract or translated English title does not make a non-English full-text report eligible.
- A report must provide at least one primary review outcome: cumulative systemic postoperative opioid consumption from end of surgery to approximately 24 hours, convertible to MME, or pain at approximately 24 hours/POD1.
- Secondary outcomes alone were excluded as `Wrong outcomes`.
- TEAS and EA were screened as distinct modalities.
- Reports with unresolved details were retained only when no positive exclusion evidence was present and a qualifying primary outcome was available or realistically reported within the eligible window.
