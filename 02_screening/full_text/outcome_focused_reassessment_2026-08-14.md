# Outcome-focused full-text reassessment — 2026-08-14

## Rule applied

For this reassessment, a report is retained at full text only when it contains, or could still contain pending source verification, at least one of the following:

1. cumulative systemic postoperative opioid consumption from the end of surgery to approximately 24 hours, in a form that can be converted to intravenous morphine milligram equivalents; or
2. pain intensity measured at approximately 24 hours (including a clearly defined postoperative day 1 assessment).

Other outcomes alone (for example PONV, gastrointestinal recovery, delirium, cognition, pulmonary outcomes, quality of recovery, or intraoperative anaesthetic/opioid consumption) are not sufficient.

Exclusion was applied only when the available report-level evidence clearly established that neither qualifying outcome was reported. Abstract-only or otherwise incomplete sources were not excluded merely because a qualifying outcome was absent from the title or abstract.
