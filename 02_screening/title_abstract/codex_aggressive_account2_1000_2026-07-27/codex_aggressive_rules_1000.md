# Aggressive account-2 screening rules

This batch follows the user-supplied supervised screening specification dated 2026-07-27.

- Adult operative surgery under general anaesthesia.
- Therapeutic TEAS or needle-based EA at recognised or explicitly defined body acupoints.
- At least one session begins no later than 24 postoperative hours.
- TEAS/EA effect must be independently estimable.
- Eligible comparator and randomized allocation required.
- Primary participant results with at least one clinical perioperative outcome required.
- YES when all or nearly all criteria are supported.
- MAYBE only when exactly one essential criterion is uncertain and eligibility remains realistic.
- NO for one clear exclusion or two or more unsupported essential criteria.
- Protocols, registrations without results, reviews, animal studies, paediatric-only studies, non-surgical care, late rehabilitation, non-GA procedures, excluded interventions, nonrandomized designs, inseparable packages, ineligible active-only comparators, and surrogate-only studies are excluded.
- Postoperative day 1 does not prove treatment began within 24 hours; use MAYBE when timing is the sole uncertainty.
- Remain blinded; do not inspect history, conflicts, comments, tags, or another reviewer’s decisions.
- Click once, verify registration, then append the audit object.
