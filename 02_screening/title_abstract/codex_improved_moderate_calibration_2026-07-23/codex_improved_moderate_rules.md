# Locked improved moderate calibration rules — 23 July 2026

## Role and stopping rule

Codex is an independent machine-assisted second reviewer. It does not inspect another reviewer’s vote, adjudicate conflicts, enter full-text screening, or change prior decisions. Screening stops after exactly 50 successfully registered votes.

## Decision threshold

- **Yes:** eligibility is clear or strongly supported.
- **Maybe:** one important unresolved issue creates a specific, realistic eligible full-text scenario.
- **No:** an explicit exclusion is present or the complete record strongly indicates ineligibility.
- Missing reporting alone is not a reason for No, and not every unreported detail warrants Maybe.

## Core eligibility

Eligible records concern adults undergoing operative surgery under general anaesthesia; TEAS through surface electrodes at recognized or explicitly defined acupuncture points or needle-based EA at such points; at least one eligible session by postoperative hour 24; randomized comparisons; an independently estimable electrical-acupuncture effect; and a clinical perioperative outcome that is present or reasonably plausible.

## Direct exclusions

Protocols and other non-results reports, reviews, case reports, non-human studies, non-operative procedures, non-general anaesthesia, ineligible acupuncture modalities, non-acupoint TENS, late rehabilitation, inseparable bundles, isolated active comparators, and non-randomized studies are excluded using the specified direct reason code.

## Ambiguity rules

Ambiguous translated allocation language is retained as Maybe when the concurrent comparison and remaining criteria are plausible. ERAS balance is assessed rather than inferred. Surrogate-focused eligible trials are Maybe when clinical outcome data could realistically exist; exclusion requires a clearly exclusively mechanistic study. Missing abstracts and non-English records are assessed without automatic inclusion or exclusion.

## Operational controls

Each citation is read, assessed, and logged before one vote is clicked. Registration is confirmed and the actual post-vote timestamp is then recorded. Numerical Covidence-ID gaps are logged without inventing an explanation. Quality control is performed after every 10 votes. No external searching, visible tags/comments, voting history, conflict resolution, imports, exports, deletion, merging, settings changes, or full-text actions are permitted.
