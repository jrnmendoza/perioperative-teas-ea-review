# Screening rules — 1,000-record continuation

This run follows the user-supplied moderate-selectivity rules dated 2026-07-26.

- Scope: adult operative surgery under general anaesthesia; randomized trials of therapeutic TEAS or separable needle-based EA.
- Timing: at least one eligible session must begin at or before 24 postoperative hours.
- Eligible background care may be shared; TEAS or EA must be the only systematically different therapeutic component in an extractable comparison.
- Yes is used when eligibility is clearly or strongly supported.
- Maybe is used only when one important unresolved issue could realistically change eligibility at full text.
- No is used when the available title/abstract evidence establishes or strongly indicates ineligibility.
- Protocols and registrations without results, reviews, animal studies, non-operative populations, non-general-anaesthesia studies, wrong interventions, late treatment, inseparable bundles, ineligible active-only comparators, non-randomized studies, and records with no eligible clinical outcome are excluded using the requested reason codes.
- No language restriction is applied.
- Companion and secondary reports are retained when they may report an eligible randomized trial.
- Another reviewer’s votes, history, and conflict decisions are not inspected.
- Exactly one audit object is appended only after each Covidence vote is visibly confirmed.
