# Locked calibration rules — 23 July 2026

## Role and stopping rule

Codex acts only as an independent machine-assisted second reviewer. It is not the human primary reviewer, final eligibility decision-maker, full-text reviewer, or conflict adjudicator. This pilot must stop after exactly 50 successfully registered title-and-abstract votes.

## Sensitivity rule

- Vote **Yes** when all essential criteria are clearly or strongly supported and no definitive exclusion is present.
- Vote **Maybe** whenever eligibility is plausible but any essential detail is missing or uncertain, including absent/incomplete abstracts, uncertain language, general anaesthesia, randomization, intervention, comparator, timing, outcome, or report linkage.
- Vote **No** only when the title or abstract explicitly establishes one unmistakable exclusion criterion and missing full text could not plausibly change it.

False exclusion is treated as more harmful than unnecessary progression to full text.

## Core eligibility

- Adults undergoing operative surgery under general anaesthesia, including balanced regional or neuraxial techniques.
- TEAS through surface electrodes at recognized or explicitly defined body acupuncture points, or needle-based EA with electrical current delivered through acupuncture needles at such points.
- At least one eligible session before the end of the first 24 postoperative hours.
- Controlled randomized design; potentially eligible companion or secondary randomized-trial reports are retained.
- Any clinically interpretable acute postoperative recovery outcome; 0–24-hour opioid consumption is not mandatory at this stage.
- No language restriction.

## Definitive exclusions

- Children-only, animal, non-surgical, chronic non-perioperative pain, or procedures explicitly performed without general anaesthesia.
- Manual, auricular, battlefield, press-tack, intradermal, wrist–ankle, buccal, dry/trigger-point/injection/laser acupuncture; moxibustion, cupping, massage, herbs; non-acupoint TENS/PENS; measurement/monitoring stimulation.
- Inseparable combined interventions or stimulation initiated only after 24 postoperative hours.
- No controlled comparison or clearly uninterpretable co-intervention imbalance.
- Clearly non-randomized, quasi-randomized, protocol without results, registry entry without results, review/editorial/commentary, case report/series, or other explicitly ineligible publication type.
- Exclusively mechanistic/surrogate outcomes with no eligible clinical perioperative outcome.

## Decision order

1. Human operative surgical population?
2. Could general anaesthesia have been used?
3. TEAS or EA present?
4. Controlled comparison?
5. Could it be randomized?
6. Eligible clinical perioperative outcome present or plausible?
7. Stimulation by the end of postoperative hour 24?

Assessment stops after one definitive exclusion criterion.

## Prohibited actions

- Do not inspect another reviewer’s vote or use View history.
- Do not resolve conflicts.
- Do not open full-text review, extraction, risk of bias, excluded/included studies, or conflict resolution.
- Do not add visible tags or comments.
- Do not search PubMed, journal sites, Google Scholar, trial registries, DOI links, or local PDFs.
- Do not import, export, delete, deduplicate, merge, or manually move references.
- Do not change review settings, reviewer mode, team membership, eligibility criteria, automation, conflict permissions, review name, or imported references.

## Quality control

After every block of 10: confirm the screen-reference count has decreased by 10, the log has 10 new rows, the page remains in title-and-abstract screening, and conflict resolution has not been opened. Stop immediately for any uncertain interface state or registration failure.
