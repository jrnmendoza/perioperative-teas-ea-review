# 3. REGISTERED SCIENTIFIC FRAMEWORK

Before touching current analyses, independently verify these directly from PROSPERO.

The registered framework appears to specify:

## Population

Adults ≥18 years undergoing operative surgery under GENERAL ANAESTHESIA.

General anaesthesia may be combined with regional or neuraxial techniques.

## TEAS

Non-invasive electrical stimulation delivered through surface electrodes over recognised or explicitly defined acupuncture points, without needle penetration.

## EA

Electrical stimulation delivered through acupuncture needles inserted at recognised or explicitly defined acupuncture points.

## Primary clinical outcome

Cumulative SYSTEMIC postoperative opioid consumption:

**end of surgery → 24 postoperative hours**

converted to:

**mg IV morphine equivalents (IV MME)**.

Programmed but undelivered PCA doses do NOT count.

## Critical pain co-outcome

Pain at approximately 24 h:

- rest;
- movement where available.

## Clinical importance

Primary opioid threshold:

**≥10 mg IV MME reduction**

Pain-worsening margin:

**+1.0 point on a 0–10 pain scale**

Sensitivity interpretations:

- 8 mg IV MME;
- 30% relative opioid reduction where calculable.

## Additional opioid outcomes

- 0–48 h;
- 0–72 h;
- longest acute postoperative interval.

## Principal evidence

TEAS versus credible sham TEAS.

EA versus credible sham EA.

## Supportive evidence

TEAS or EA versus:

- usual care;
- no stimulation;
- attention control.

## Active electrical controls

Analysed separately where clinically interpretable.

## Synthesis restriction

**TEAS AND EA WILL NOT BE COMBINED IN A POOLED ESTIMATE.**

Verify all of this directly.

Do not rely on this prompt when the PDF says otherwise.

The PDF wins.
