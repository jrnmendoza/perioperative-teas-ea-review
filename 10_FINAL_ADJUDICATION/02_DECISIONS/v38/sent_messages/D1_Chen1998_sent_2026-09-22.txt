Dear Professor White,

I am writing regarding your trial of transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation at acupoint versus nonacupoint sites in women undergoing total abdominal hysterectomy or myomectomy, published in 1998.

I was unable to locate a current professional email address for Dr Lei Chen, the first author of the paper, so I am contacting you directly.

We are conducting a systematic review of perioperative transcutaneous electrical acupoint stimulation and electroacupuncture for postoperative opioid consumption, registered on PROSPERO as CRD420261452908.

Our primary outcome is all delivered systemic opioid from the end of surgery to 24 hours, expressed in intravenous morphine equivalents, and it must include opioid given outside the patient-controlled analgesia pump.

Your trial is one of very few reporting a sham-controlled comparison with a 24-hour cumulative opioid total, so we would be very grateful for your help with the following:

1. Table 2 reports "supplemental analgesic medication" over 0-24 hours as a number of doses, for example 3 +/- 2 in the sham group, rather than as a total dose.

Which drug or drugs did this represent, and is an arm-level total dose in milligrams available for the first 24 postoperative hours?

2. Were any of those supplemental medications opioids?

If so, could you provide the arm-level total with its standard deviation?

3. Is a combined total of all systemic opioid delivered over 0-24 hours available for each arm, with its own standard deviation?

We cannot calculate this reliably by summing the pump and supplemental components because the individual-level covariance needed for the combined variance is not published.

4. Could you confirm that the 24-hour hydromorphone totals in Table 2 represent drug actually delivered by the pump rather than demanded doses, and confirm the analysed sample size in each arm?

5. Group I is described as sham-TENS with no electrical current at ST36, and Group II as nonacupoint-TENS at the shoulders.

Could you confirm that Group I received no electrical current at any site?

We analyse an inactive sham separately from an active electrical control, so this determines which comparison the trial contributes to.

Aggregate arm-level summaries are all we need; please do not send any participant-level data.

We will record any clarification separately from the published report. We would also be happy to send you our proposed interpretation to check before we use it in the review.
