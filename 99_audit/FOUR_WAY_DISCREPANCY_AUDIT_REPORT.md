## 1. Executive Summary & Forensic Provenance

This four-way audit uncovers the exact mechanism by which data inaccuracies and hallucinations entered the systematic review workflow:

### Key Forensic Discoveries:
1. **The Gemini Markdown vs. Automated Payload Disconnect**: The Gemini markdown extractions (`*_gemini.md`) often accurately stated that postoperative opioid consumption was not quantified in the study (e.g. Gao 2021, Grech 2016). The hallucinated numbers **did not originate from the human reviewers or the initial Gemini markdown reader**, but from the automated batch synthesis script (`build_complete_truth_payloads.py` / `jr_complete_results_payloads.json`), which filled missing continuous values with hardcoded boilerplate numbers before pushing to Covidence.
2. **The Covidence "Empty Shell" Reality**: In the Covidence CSV export, despite having 5,103 columns, **zero quantitative result cells** contain continuous mean/SD or dichotomous events for postoperative opioids or pain. The platform contains the protocol definitions and baseline demographics, but the numerical outcomes were never successfully ingested into Covidence's outcome result tables.
3. **ChatGPT Sol's Strengths & Weaknesses**: ChatGPT Sol performed rigorous, text-faithful extraction that explicitly identified "NR", "Graph-only", and unit ambiguities. However, its RoB 2 assessments were systematically skewed towards "High risk" or "Some concerns", resulting in **0 Low Risk overall** judgments across 61 trials, refusing to grant Low Risk even to meticulously blinded trials (such as Chen 2020 or Wu 2022).
4. **The Source PDF Ground Truth**: Verification against the original published PDFs revealed that **62 of 63 studies required corrections**, 10 studies warrant a justified **Low Risk overall** RoB 2 rating, 2 studies warrant a **High Risk** downgrade, and **60 studies (95.2%) require author correspondence** due to unmeasured or graph-only 24h opioid data.
