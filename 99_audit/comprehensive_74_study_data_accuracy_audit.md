## 3. Primary Endpoint Refinement & Exclusion Audit (2026-09-01)
- **Directive**: Exclude trials that contribute *Neither 24-Hour Cumulative Opioid nor 24-Hour Postoperative Pain Scores* from data extraction and synthesis.
- **Action Executed**: 11 candidate studies moved from Data Extraction back to Full-Text Review in Covidence and voted **Exclude (`Wrong outcomes`)**:
  1. `#643 - Chen 2016` (`1879896981`): PACU emergence & early 2-4h recovery only; no 24-h opioid/pain.
  2. `#985 - Christensen 1993` (`1879897531`): Early acute recovery (0-4h) only; no 24-h opioid/pain.
  3. `#839 - Colak 2010` (`1879897298`): Immediate post-extubation sedation & PACU emergence only.
  4. `#694 - Ertas 2015` (`1879897063`): Immediate PACU discharge pain only; no 24-h assessment.
  5. `#537 - Gao 2018` (`1879896778`): Intraoperative anesthetic sparing & PACU stay only.
  6. `#449 - Song 2020` (`1879896637`): Sleep architecture & early PACU recovery only.
  7. `#505 - Oztas 2019` (`1879896727`): Preoperative anxiety & immediate post-op 0-2h pain only.
  8. `#441 - Szmit 2021` (`1879896624`): Postoperative nausea & early PACU stay only.
  9. `#3157 - Kang 2023` (`1882880803`): Bowel recovery & acute PACU sedation only.
  10. `#4330 - Larson 2010` (`1882881976`): Antiemetic/PONV outcomes & PACU discharge time only.
  11. `#3690 - Zhang 2018` (`1882881336`): GI motility & hospital stay only.
- **Resulting Synthesis Cohort**: 63 retained RCTs, 100% of which contribute directly to 24-hour acute analgesic meta-analyses.
