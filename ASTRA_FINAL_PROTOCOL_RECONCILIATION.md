The registered primary outcome is all delivered systemic postoperative opioid consumption from the end of surgery through 24 hours, in mg IV morphine equivalents. PCA-only exposure is insufficient when additional systemic opioid use is unquantified. Adults must undergo general anaesthesia (with regional techniques permitted). TEAS uses surface electrodes without needle penetration; EA uses inserted needles. Principal comparisons are modality-specific credible sham comparisons. Usual care/no stimulation/attention controls are supportive; active electrical comparators require separate interpretation. TEAS and EA are never pooled inferentially.

Clinical interpretation requires an opioid point estimate showing at least 10 mg IV MME sparing and approximately 24-hour pain evidence whose upper 95% confidence limit is below +1 point on a 0–10 scale. Rest and movement remain separate; 8 mg and 30% relative sparing are sensitivity thresholds. Absence of paired pain evidence cannot establish this joint criterion. The 48- and 72-hour opioid outcomes are additional, not co-primary.
