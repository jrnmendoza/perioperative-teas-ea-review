The full included result inventory was inspected rather than substituting EA/usual-care for EA/sham. Coura2011 reports fentanyl in µg/kg, not absolute µg, with unquantified additional analgesia; no mean-weight multiplication supplies an absolute-dose SD. ## Rescue and conversion rule

A pump total is eligible when the source establishes it as the delivered systemic postoperative opioid total and does not describe additional uncounted opioid use. If extra systemic opioid is permitted but its inclusion or amount cannot be established, use a separately labeled device-exposure sensitivity. Do not impute rescue as zero, infer opioid rescue from mixed-medication counts, or add programmed doses. Author-reported equivalents are acceptable numerically; ambiguous IV/oral basis is carried as a unit-assumption sensitivity. These rules apply consistently regardless of effect direction.
