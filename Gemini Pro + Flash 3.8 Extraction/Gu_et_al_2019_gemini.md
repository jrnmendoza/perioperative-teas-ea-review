## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** The research did not receive any specific grant from funding agencies in the public, commercial, or not-for-profit sectors [cite: 21].
* **Country:** China [cite: 21].
* **Journal:** European Journal of Integrative Medicine [cite: 21].
* **DOI:** 10.1016/j.eujim.2019.01.001 [cite: 21].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** Not reported [cite: 21].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Randomized controlled trial with a pre- and posttest design [cite: 21].
* **Randomization method:** Randomized according to a computer-generated random numbers table [cite: 21].
* **Blinding:** The patients were unaware of the group allocations [cite: 21]. Another investigator collected the data and was blind to the group allocation [cite: 21]. Those who managed the data and statistical analysis were also blinded [cite: 21].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** Long duration TEAS (L-TEAS) group and placebo-control (C-TEAS) group [cite: 21].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 120 patients randomly allocated (C-TEAS: n=60; L-TEAS: n=60) [cite: 21]. 117 patients completed the trial and were analyzed (C-TEAS: n=59; L-TEAS: n=58) [cite: 21].
* **Age:** C-TEAS: 56.67 ± 6.23 years; L-TEAS: 57.59 ± 7.32 years [cite: 21].
* **Sex distribution:** C-TEAS: 29 Male / 30 Female; L-TEAS: 31 Male / 27 Female [cite: 21].
* **BMI:** C-TEAS: 21.84 ± 2.78 kg/m²; L-TEAS: 22.71 ± 2.54 kg/m² [cite: 21].
* **ASA classification:** ASA physical status classification of grade I or II [cite: 21].
* **Comorbidities:** Patients with inflammatory rheumatic disease, lumbar muscle degeneration, major organ system disease, or a history of any gastrointestinal surgeries were excluded [cite: 21]. Patients using analgesic or psychotropic drugs, and patients with central nervous system disease, local skin infection, or upper/lower limb nerve injury were also excluded [cite: 21].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Laparoscopic radical gastrectomy (Proximal, Distal, or Total gastrectomy) [cite: 21].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 21].
* **Induction:** 2.0 mg/kg propofol, 0.5 μg/kg sufentanil, and 0.15 mg/kg cis-atracurium [cite: 21].
* **Maintenance:** Closed-loop automated system of propofol and remifentanil, sustained infusion of cis-atracurium [cite: 21].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Transcutaneous electrical acupoint stimulation (TEAS) [cite: 21].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 58 analyzed [cite: 21].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Bilateral Zusanli (ST36) and Neiguan (PC6) [cite: 21].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** 2/100 Hz with a dense-disperse wave [cite: 21].
* **Treatment intensity:** 5 to 30 mA, adjusted to make patients feel "de qi" sensations [cite: 21].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** Maintained from 30 min before anesthetic induction to 30 min after the operation [cite: 21]. Additionally, TEAS was performed at 8 a.m., 1 p.m., and 6 p.m. within 2 days of the operation [cite: 21].
* **Duration of each treatment:** Maintained throughout the perioperative phase as noted above, and postoperatively for 30 min per session [cite: 21].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** One perioperative session followed by three sessions per day for 2 days postoperatively [cite: 21].
* **Total treatment period:** From preoperative until 2 days postoperatively [cite: 21].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Patient-controlled intravenous analgesia (PCIA) [cite: 21].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Placebo-control (C-TEAS) [cite: 21].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 59 analyzed [cite: 21].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** The output wires of the stimulator were broken, and the intensity was set to 20 mA [cite: 21]. Patients were told TEAS is an acupuncture method without feeling [cite: 21].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** PCIA device containing 100 μg sufentanil, 200 μg dexmedetomidine, 100 mg flurbiprofen axetil, and 0.5 mg palonosetron (total 100 ml) [cite: 21].
* **Dosage parameters:** Bolus dose 0.5 ml, lock-out time 15 min, basal infusion 2 ml/h [cite: 21].
* **Duration:** Extracted from device records at intervals up to 36 hours postoperatively [cite: 21].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Visual analogue scale (VAS), scale of 0-10 [cite: 21].
* **Pain assessment time points:** 4 h (T1), 8 h (T2), 16 h (T3), 24 h (T4), and 36 h (T5) postoperatively [cite: 21].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group:**
    * 4h: L-TEAS = 2.79 ± 0.48; C-TEAS = 3.41 ± 0.62 [cite: 21].
    * 8h: L-TEAS = 2.65 ± 0.42; C-TEAS = 3.17 ± 0.49 [cite: 21].
    * 16h: L-TEAS = 2.45 ± 0.54; C-TEAS = 2.43 ± 0.64 [cite: 21].
    * 24h: L-TEAS = 1.98 ± 0.39; C-TEAS = 2.72 ± 0.73 [cite: 21].
    * 36h: L-TEAS = 2.12 ± 0.35; C-TEAS = 2.25 ± 0.42 [cite: 21].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** VAS scores at 4 h, 8 h, and 24 h were significantly decreased in the L-TEAS group (P < 0.05) [cite: 21].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Sufentanil (via PCIA mixed with other drugs) [cite: 21].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous via PCIA [cite: 21].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** Analgesic consumption (ml) at 4h = 10.02 ± 2.26; 8h = 20.55 ± 4.59; 36h = 72.64 ± 9.74 [cite: 21].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** Analgesic consumption (ml) at 4h = 13.38 ± 2.98; 8h = 24.63 ± 3.94; 36h = 76.01 ± 10.43 [cite: 21].
* **Measurement time point(s):** 4h, 8h, 16h, 24h, and 36h postoperatively [cite: 21].
* **Between-group difference:** Analgesic consumption at 4h, 8h, and 36h was significantly decreased in the L-TEAS group (P < 0.05) [cite: 21].
* **Percentage/relative reduction:** Not explicitly calculated [cite: 21].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Not explicitly reported, though analgesic consumption from the PCIA pump was tracked [cite: 21].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** Incidence of PONV was 12% in the L-TEAS group and 32.2% in the C-TEAS group (P < 0.05) [cite: 21]. Palonosetron was given prophylactically before the end of the operation [cite: 21].
* **Gastrointestinal recovery:** Time to first bowel sounds: L-TEAS = 19.69 ± 7.61 h, C-TEAS = 26.58 ± 7.43 h (P = 0.01) [cite: 21]. Time to first flatus: L-TEAS = 36.58 ± 10.75 h, C-TEAS = 43.56 ± 9.57 h (P = 0.03) [cite: 21]. Time to first defecation: L-TEAS = 71.48 ± 20.62 h, C-TEAS = 77.27 ± 22.67 h (P = 0.04) [cite: 21].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** Ramsay Sedation Scale (RSS) scores were not significantly different between the groups (P > 0.05) [cite: 21]. Patient satisfaction was significantly higher in the L-TEAS group (P < 0.05) [cite: 21].
* **Length of stay:** Not reported [cite: 21].
* **Other postoperative complications:** None explicitly reported [cite: 21].
