## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Supported by the School-level research projects of Southwest Medical University [2023QN113] [cite: 35].
* **Country:** China (Luzhou, Sichuan) [cite: 35].
* **Journal:** American Journal of Translational Research (Am J Transl Res) [cite: 35].
* **DOI:** 10.62347/WNT07078 [cite: 35].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** medicalresearch.org.cn (registration number: MR-51-24-038798) [cite: 35]. Approved by the hospital ethics committee (approval number: KY2024-023) [cite: 35].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Single-center, two-arm, single-blind, prospective randomized trial [cite: 35].
* **Randomization method:** Centralized randomization system ensured allocation concealment [cite: 35]. The randomization sequence was computer-generated by an independent statistician using SPSS 27.0 software, and patients were automatically assigned to their respective groups only after formal enrollment [cite: 35].
* **Blinding:** Single-blind [cite: 35]. Patients were blinded to their treatment allocation [cite: 35]. The acupuncture operators were aware of group assignments due to the nature of the intervention [cite: 35]. Outcome assessors and the statistician responsible for data analysis remained blinded to treatment allocation [cite: 35].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** EA (ST36, ST37, ST39) plus standard care group and Standard care group (Control) [cite: 35].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 101 randomized (EA: n=51; Standard care: n=50); 88 completed the outcome measurements and were analyzed (EA: n=43; Standard care: n=45) [cite: 35].
* **Age:** EA group: 49.1 (6.1) years; Standard care group: 51.0 (6.3) years (mean (SD)) [cite: 35].
* **Sex distribution:** 100% Female (inclusion criteria specified women) [cite: 35].
* **BMI:** EA group: 25.1 (4.2) kg/m²; Standard care group: 24.1 (3.6) kg/m² [cite: 35].
* **ASA classification:** ASA Physical Status Grades I-III [cite: 35]. ASA I/II/III distribution: EA group = 11 (25.6%) / 30 (69.8%) / 2 (4.7%); Standard care group = 14 (31.1%) / 31 (68.9%) / 0 (0%) [cite: 35].
* **Comorbidities:** Included hypertension and diabetes [cite: 35]. Patients were excluded if they had severe cardiac, hepatic, or renal insufficiency; a history of gastrointestinal disorders (tumors, chronic constipation, peptic ulcers); or skin infection/nerve damage near experimental acupoints [cite: 35].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Laparoscopic hysterectomy with or without salpingo-oophorectomy or salpingectomy [cite: 35].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 35].
* **Induction:** Not specifically detailed [cite: 35].
* **Maintenance:** Not specifically detailed [cite: 35].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Postoperative electroacupuncture (EA) [cite: 35].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 43 analyzed [cite: 35].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Bilateral Zusanli (ST36), Shangjuxu (ST37), and Xiajuxu (ST39) (totaling six acupuncture points) [cite: 35].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** 2 Hz continuous stimulation [cite: 35].
* **Treatment intensity:** Adjusted to the maximum level tolerated by each patient, ranging from 0.5 mA to 2.0 mA; initially, disposable stainless-steel needles were inserted 20-40 mm deep and manipulated for 30 seconds to achieve "Deqi" sensation [cite: 35].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** Commenced 3-5 hours postoperatively [cite: 35].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 30 minutes per session [cite: 35].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** Three sessions: 3-5 hours postoperatively, and in the morning (07:00-09:00) and afternoon (17:00-19:00) on the first postoperative day [cite: 35].
* **Total treatment period:** Up to the first postoperative day [cite: 35].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Standard care following ERAS protocols (early food intake and early ambulation); self-controlled analgesia pump in 100% of EA patients [cite: 35].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Standard care alone [cite: 35].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 45 analyzed [cite: 35].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Standard postoperative care was provided without EA or any additional interventions that could affect GI function recovery (e.g., Chinese medicine prescriptions, enemas) [cite: 35]. Followed the ERAS protocols [cite: 35].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Self-controlled analgesia pump was used by 43 (100%) in the EA group and 42 (93.3%) in the Standard care group [cite: 35]. Ketorolac tromethamine (30 mg intramuscularly) was given as rescue analgesia if VAS ≥ 6 or if requested within 24 hours postoperatively [cite: 35].
* **Dosage parameters:** Not explicitly detailed for the PCA pump [cite: 35].
* **Duration:** Not explicitly detailed [cite: 35].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Visual Analogue Scale (VAS) ranging from 0 (no pain) to 10 (severe pain) [cite: 35].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Not specifically distinguished [cite: 35].
* **Pain assessment time points:** Recorded once daily from postoperative days 1 to 5 [cite: 35].
* **Median pain score for each group (median (min-max)):**
    * Postoperative 24 h: EA = 5 (3-7); Standard care = 6 (4-8) [cite: 35].
    * Postoperative 48 h: EA = 3 (1-5); Standard care = 4 (1-7) [cite: 35].
    * Postoperative 72 h: EA = 2 (1-4); Standard care = 3 (1-5) [cite: 35].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** The median postoperative pain scores in the EA group were significantly lower than those in the standard care arm at 24, 48, and 72 hours (P < 0.001) [cite: 35].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Not explicitly specified (self-controlled analgesia pump was used) [cite: 35].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous via PCA pump assumed, but text says "Self-controlled analgesia pump" [cite: 35].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention/control groups:** Actual consumption amounts were not reported [cite: 35].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Use of additional analgesic: EA group = 1 (2.3%); Standard care group = 4 (8.9%) (P = 0.385) [cite: 35].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **Gastrointestinal recovery (Primary Outcome):** 
    * Time to first flatus (mean (SD)): EA = 36.4 (8.0) h; Standard care = 42.2 (8.5) h (HR 1.9, 95% CI 1.2-2.9, P < 0.001) [cite: 35].
    * Time to first defecation (mean (SD)): EA = 46.0 (8.0) h; Standard care = 51.3 (9.4) h (HR 1.9, 95% CI 1.2-3.0, P = 0.010) [cite: 35].
    * I-FEED scores (median (min-max)): Postoperative 24 h: EA = 3 (2-4), Standard = 3 (2-4) (P < 0.001); Postoperative 48 h: EA = 2 (0-2), Standard = 2 (1-3) (P < 0.001); Postoperative 72 h: EA = 1 (0-2), Standard = 2 (1-3) (P < 0.001) [cite: 35].
    * Bowel obstruction: EA = 1 (2.3%); Standard care = 0 (0%) (P = 0.489) [cite: 35].
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** Vomiting: EA = 3 (7.0%); Standard care = 9 (20.0%) (P = 0.142) [cite: 35]. Metoclopramide (10 mg IM) was given if requested or if I-FEED score ≥ 6 within 24 hours [cite: 35].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** QoR-40 scores on the day of discharge (median (min-max)): EA = 196 (192-198); Standard care = 192 (188-198) (P < 0.001) [cite: 35].
* **Length of stay:** Length of hospital stay (mean (SD)): EA = 6.1 (1.7) d; Standard care = 6.2 (1.3) d (P = 0.793) [cite: 35].
* **Other postoperative complications:** There were no serious adverse events or fatalities in either arm [cite: 35]. LMR and PNR on postoperative day 2 showed no significant differences [cite: 35].
