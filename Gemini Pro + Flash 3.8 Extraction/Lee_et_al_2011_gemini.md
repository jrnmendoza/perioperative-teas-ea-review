## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Not explicitly stated; however, the treatment component was supported and undertaken at the China Medical University Hospital (CMUH) [cite: 41].
* **Country:** Australia and Taiwan (Authors are affiliated with Victoria University, Australia, and China Medical University Hospital, Taiwan; the trial was conducted in Taiwan) [cite: 41].
* **Journal:** Evidence-Based Complementary and Alternative Medicine [cite: 41].
* **DOI:** 10.1155/2011/696754 [cite: 41].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** Not reported. Ethics approval was gained from the Human Research Ethics Committee of La Trobe University Australia and China Medical University Hospital Taiwan [cite: 41].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Double-blind, sham, and different intervention controlled clinical experimental design (randomized) [cite: 41].
* **Randomization method:** Subjects were assigned to a treatment group in numbered order as they became available, using a random number table for grouping [cite: 41].
* **Blinding:** Double-blind; neither the subjects nor the research assistant/nurse collecting the results was aware of who was receiving which therapy [cite: 41]. The doctor performing the interventions was also stated to be unaware of the differences between the different interventions [cite: 41]. Group assignments were kept fully blinded from the subjects and data collectors [cite: 41].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** 
    * Group 1: NIL (control group) [cite: 41].
    * Group 2: Sham acupuncture [cite: 41].
    * Group 3: High-frequency stimulation [cite: 41].
    * Group 4: Low-frequency stimulation [cite: 41].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 49 consented; 47 completed and were analyzed (Group 1: n=13; Group 2: n=12; Group 3: n=12; Group 4: n=10) [cite: 41]. Two subjects from Group 4 discontinued due to the inability to carry out the VAS [cite: 41].
* **Age:** Overall mean = 42.02 ± 8.31 years (range 14.00 to 59.00) [cite: 41].
* **Sex distribution:** 100% Female (hysterectomized patients) [cite: 41].
* **BMI:** Overall weight mean = 59.74 ± 17.54 kg (range 40.00 to 158.00) [cite: 41]. BMI not reported [cite: 41].
* **ASA classification:** Not reported [cite: 41].
* **Comorbidities:** Patients were excluded if they had hypertension, diabetes, cachexia, cardiac, respiratory, kidney and nervous system problems, coagulopathy, other bleeding disorders, or a history of opioid use and sensitivity to opioid-related side effects [cite: 41].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Hysterectomy [cite: 41].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 41].
* **Induction:** Propofol 2 mg/kg and atracurium 0.5 mg/kg for induction (no narcotics) [cite: 41].
* **Maintenance:** Sevoflurane with 50% nitrous oxide and oxygen [cite: 41].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Silver Spike Point (SSP) needle-free electroacupuncture (EA) [cite: 41].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** Group 3 (High-frequency): 12 analyzed; Group 4 (Low-frequency): 10 analyzed [cite: 41].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Zusanli (ST36) [cite: 41].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** 
    * Group 3: Continuous wave of 100 Hz [cite: 41].
    * Group 4: Dense-sparse wave 3 Hz/4 sec, 10 Hz/4 sec, and 20 Hz/4 sec [cite: 41].
    * Postoperatively, 1/f Yuragi waves were used [cite: 41].
* **Treatment intensity:** Controlled current, ensuring each subject received the same stimulation and dosages [cite: 41].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** Commenced 30 minutes before general anesthesia; postoperatively, it was administered once initiated when the subject regained consciousness in the recovery room [cite: 41].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 30 minutes per session [cite: 41].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** Two sessions (one preoperative, one postoperative) [cite: 41].
* **Total treatment period:** Single day (perioperative) [cite: 41].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Patient-controlled analgesia (PCA) postoperatively [cite: 41].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Group 1: NIL (General anesthesia only, no SSP stimulation); Group 2: Sham SSP needle-free EA [cite: 41].
* **Number of participants in control group:** Group 1: 13 analyzed; Group 2: 12 analyzed [cite: 41].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** For Group 2, the SSP electrode was placed at a sham acupuncture point near to ST36 (on the same level but above the tuberositas tibia) with no stimulation for 30 minutes [cite: 41].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Patient-controlled analgesia (PCA) providing IV morphine [cite: 41].
* **Dosage parameters:** Boluses of 0.8 mg with a lockout time of 8 minutes [cite: 41].
* **Duration:** Connected after one hour in the recovery room for the subsequent 23 hours (assessed over 24 hours) [cite: 41].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Visual Analogue Scale (VAS) (10 cm line, rank 0-10) [cite: 41].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Not specifically distinguished [cite: 41].
* **Pain assessment time points:** 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 8, 16, and 24 hours postoperatively [cite: 41].
* **Mean pain score for each group (Mean ± SD at 24 hours):**
    * Group 1 (Control) = 3.50 ± 1.27 [cite: 41].
    * Group 2 (Sham) = 2.54 ± 0.86 [cite: 41].
    * Group 3 (High-freq) = 2.68 ± 1.63 [cite: 41].
    * Group 4 (Low-freq) = 1.21 ± 1.31 [cite: 41].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** Significant differences were found between the means at 2, 3, 4, 8, 16, and 24 hours postoperatively (P < .05 and P < .01 at 24 hours) [cite: 41]. Post-hoc tests indicated Group 4 was significantly different from Groups 1, 2, and 3 at 24 hours [cite: 41].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Morphine [cite: 41].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous via PCA [cite: 41].
* **Opioid consumption (PCA doses delivered in mg, 24 hr Mean ± SD):**
    * Group 1 (Control) = 38.63 ± 11.08 mg [cite: 41].
    * Group 2 (Sham) = 34.20 ± 6.40 mg [cite: 41].
    * Group 3 (High-freq) = 31.14 ± 8.15 mg [cite: 41].
    * Group 4 (Low-freq) = 29.29 ± 8.89 mg [cite: 41].
* **Measurement time point(s):** Postoperative room (POR), 1-8 h, 8-16 h, and 16-24 h [cite: 41].
* **Between-group difference:** Differences were found for the total amount of analgesia delivered in mg (P < .10), with post-hoc analysis confirming a significant difference between Groups 1 and 4 [cite: 41]. In the POR specifically, Group 4 (2.30 mg) was significantly lower than Group 2 (5.17 mg) and Group 3 (4.58 mg) (P < .05) [cite: 41].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Assessed via total PCA demands (button presses) [cite: 41].
* **Total PCA demands over 24-hr (Mean ± SD):**
    * Group 1 = 84.54 ± 38.66 [cite: 41].
    * Group 2 = 74.18 ± 39.33 [cite: 41].
    * Group 3 = 52.17 ± 21.87 [cite: 41].
    * Group 4 = 41.60 ± 36.47 [cite: 41].
* **Between-group difference:** Significant differences were found for total PCA doses demanded (P < .05), with post-hoc analysis confirming the difference between Groups 1 and 4 [cite: 41].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **Gastrointestinal recovery:** Time to first bowel movement (hours, Mean ± SD): Group 1 = 37.54 ± 2.93; Group 2 = 38.03 ± 2.71; Group 3 = 35.50 ± 2.64; Group 4 = 33.30 ± 2.43 [cite: 41]. No significant differences were found [cite: 41].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** Time of first ambulation (hours, Mean ± SD): Group 1 = 32.50 ± 2.41; Group 2 = 33.75 ± 3.11; Group 3 = 33.13 ± 3.18; Group 4 = 29.90 ± 2.95 [cite: 41]. No significant differences were found [cite: 41].
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** Subjects were asked before discharge about side effects such as bruising, dizziness, and anxiety, but specific numeric results for these were not reported in the findings section [cite: 41].
* **Length of stay:** Not reported [cite: 41].
