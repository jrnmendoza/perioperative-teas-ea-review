## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Supported by grants from the National Science Council of the Republic of China (NSC90-2314-B002-302 and NSC 88-2314-B039-011) [cite: 45].
* **Country:** Taiwan, ROC [cite: 45].
* **Journal:** Pain [cite: 45].
* **DOI:** PII: S0304-3959(02)00261-0 [cite: 45].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** Not reported [cite: 45]. The study protocol was approved by the Hospital Research Committee [cite: 45].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Randomized controlled trial [cite: 45].
* **Randomization method:** Computer-generated randomization sequence [cite: 45].
* **Blinding:** The observer who recorded visual analog scale (VAS) scores had no knowledge of the patient's group assignment [cite: 45]. Subjects and electrical equipment were placed such that subjects were unable to see any specifics regarding the type of current administered, and technicians maintained a normal persona to ensure patients remained unaware of their grouping category [cite: 45].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** Group I (control), Group II (sham-EA), Group III (low-EA), and Group IV (high-EA) [cite: 45].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 100 enrolled and randomized into four groups of 25 each [cite: 45]. All enrolled patients completed the study period with no withdrawals [cite: 45].
* **Age:** Group I (control): 39 ± 8 years; Group II (sham-EA): 41 ± 12 years; Group III (low-EA): 38 ± 7 years; Group IV (high-EA): 42 ± 13 years [cite: 45].
* **Sex distribution:** 100% Female [cite: 45].
* **BMI:** Weight reported: Group I: 51 ± 14 kg; Group II: 55 ± 10 kg; Group III: 54 ± 11 kg; Group IV: 51 ± 17 kg [cite: 45]. BMI not reported [cite: 45].
* **ASA classification:** ASA physical status I-II [cite: 45].
* **Comorbidities:** Patients with a history of opioid abuse, or significant cardiovascular, pulmonary, renal, hepatic, or neurological disease were excluded [cite: 45].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Lower abdominal surgery (specifically, abdominal hysterectomy) [cite: 45].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 45].
* **Induction:** i.v. thiopental 5 mg/kg and succinylcholine 2 mg/kg for tracheal intubation [cite: 45].
* **Maintenance:** Isoflurane in nitrous oxide 60% with oxygen 40% and an intermittent dose of atracurium (without the use of opioids) [cite: 45].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Preoperative electroacupuncture (EA) [cite: 45].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** Group III (low-EA): 25; Group IV (high-EA): 25 [cite: 45].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Bilateral Zusanli (also known as ST-36) [cite: 45].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** 
    * Group III: 2 Hz [cite: 45].
    * Group IV: 100 Hz [cite: 45].
* **Treatment intensity:** Constant current of 0.5 mA, 1 ms square pulse, at a maximal tolerable intensity (a strong, but not painful sensation) after the patient reported the sensation of 'De-Xi' [cite: 45].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** 20 min before the induction of anesthesia [cite: 45].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 20 min [cite: 45].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** One single preoperative session [cite: 45].
* **Total treatment period:** Single day (preoperative) [cite: 45].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Postoperative patient-controlled analgesia (PCA) of morphine [cite: 45]. Before PCA, either pethidine 1 mg/kg i.m. or PCA was provided for the first request during the first hour [cite: 45].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Control group (Group I) and Sham-EA group (Group II) [cite: 45].
* **Number of participants in control group:** Group I: 25; Group II: 25 [cite: 45].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Group I received neither needle insertion nor electric stimulation [cite: 45]. Group II received needle insertion with the indicator light on but without electrical stimulation (sham-EA) [cite: 45].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** The first request for pain medication was either pethidine 1 mg/kg i.m. during the first hour or recorded by PCA when no request was made during the first hour [cite: 45]. At 1 h postoperation, the PCA system was connected [cite: 45].
* **Dosage parameters:** PCA device intravenously delivered 2 mg morphine as 'on demand' doses with a minimum lockout interval of 10 min [cite: 45]. No other analgesics were administered during the PCA control period [cite: 45].
* **Duration:** Measured and recorded over a 24-hour test period (23 hours connected to PCA) [cite: 45].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** 100 mm visual analog scale (VAS) [cite: 45].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Not specifically distinguished [cite: 45].
* **Pain assessment time points:** 30 min, 1, 1.5, 2, 4, 8, 16, and 24 h postoperatively [cite: 45].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group:** Visualized in Figure 1. Pain scores were not different among the four groups [cite: 45].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** Pain scores were not different among the four groups across the 24 h period [cite: 45].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Morphine [cite: 45].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous via PCA [cite: 45].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** 
    * Total morphine delivered in 24 h (mg): Group III (low-EA) = 21.8 ± 14.7; Group IV (high-EA) = 15.0 ± 10.7 [cite: 45].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** 
    * Total morphine delivered in 24 h (mg): Group I (control) = 38.1 ± 16.0; Group II (sham-EA) = 30.2 ± 14.4 [cite: 45].
* **Measurement time point(s):** 1-8 h, 8-16 h, 16-24 h, and total in 24 h [cite: 45].
* **Between-group difference:** There were highly significant differences in morphine delivered among the four groups [cite: 45]. Group III and IV required significantly less morphine than Group I (P < 0.01) [cite: 45].
* **Percentage/relative reduction:** The total amount of morphine required during the 24 h test period was reduced by 21%, 43%, and 61% in the sham-EA, low-EA, and high-EA groups, respectively, compared to the control group [cite: 45].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Addressed via the number of PCA demands during the 24 h test period [cite: 45].
* **Total PCA demands in 24 h:** Group I = 20.5 ± 9.2; Group II = 16.1 ± 7.4; Group III = 11.7 ± 7.1; Group IV = 7.9 ± 5.9 [cite: 45].
* **Time for the first dose of pethidine after operation (min):** Group I = 10.6 ± 5.9; Group II = 18.0 ± 7.9; Group III = 27.9 ± 12.3; Group IV = 28.1 ± 13.8 [cite: 45].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** 
    * Incidence of nausea (during the first 24 h): Group I = 11 (44%); Group II = 10 (40%); Group III = 4 (16%); Group IV = 6 (24%) [cite: 45].
    * Incidence of vomiting (during the first 24 h): Group I = 0 (0%); Group II = 1 (4%); Group III = 1 (4%); Group IV = 0 (0%) [cite: 45].
    * The incidence of nausea was significantly lower in both the low- and high-EA groups compared to the other two groups (P < 0.05) [cite: 45].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** Incidence of dizziness: Group I = 14 (56%); Group II = 16 (64%); Group III = 7 (28%); Group IV = 10 (40%) [cite: 45]. Dizziness was significantly lower in the low- and high-EA groups compared to control and sham-EA (P < 0.05) [cite: 45].
* **Length of stay:** Not reported [cite: 45].
* **Other postoperative complications:** Incidence of pruritis: Group I = 2 (8%); Group II = 0 (0%); Group III = 1 (4%); Group IV = 1 (4%) [cite: 45]. No patient demonstrated any respiratory depression (respiratory rate < 10/min or SpO2 < 90%) [cite: 45].
