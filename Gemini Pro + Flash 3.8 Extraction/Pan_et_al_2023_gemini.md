## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Supported by the National Foundation of Natural Science of China (No.81573742 and 82104622), the Wenzhou Municipal Science and Technology Bureau (No:Y20180568), and the Yuhuan Municipal Science and Technology Bureau (No:Y202211) [cite: 57].
* **Country:** China (Wenzhou, Hangzhou) [cite: 57].
* **Journal:** Journal of Pain Research [cite: 57].
* **DOI:** 10.2147/JPR.S399249 [cite: 57].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** ChiCTR2100045173 [cite: 57]. Approved by the Ethics Committee of Wenzhou Medical University (Registry URL: YJLCYJ-2020-148) [cite: 57].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Double-blind randomized control-group clinical trial [cite: 57].
* **Randomization method:** Patients were assigned to the groups using random number tables [cite: 57].
* **Blinding:** Double-blind [cite: 57]. A PACU nurse, who was unaware of the group of each patient, assisted patients to evaluate their pain level [cite: 57].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** TEAS group (Group T) and control group (Group C) [cite: 57].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 120 women enrolled; 105 were included in the final overall study cohort (Group T: n=52; Group C: n=53) [cite: 57].
* **Age:** Group T: 42.2 ± 5.5 years; Group C: 43.2 ± 6.3 years (mean ± SD) [cite: 57].
* **Sex distribution:** 100% Female (women undergoing laparoscopic myomectomy) [cite: 57].
* **BMI:** Group T: 23.2 ± 2.8 kg/m²; Group C: 22.2 ± 2.5 kg/m² [cite: 57].
* **ASA classification:** ASA physical status I and II [cite: 57]. Group T (I/II): 34/18; Group C (I/II): 36/17 [cite: 57].
* **Comorbidities:** Patients were excluded if they had incision or surgical scar at acupoint, infection of acupoint, nerve injury in any limb, prior spinal surgery, allergic reaction to drugs, sinus bradycardia, severe neurological disease, severe respiratory disease, smoking, severe cardiovascular disease, addiction to an anaesthetic, or recent history of opioid use [cite: 57].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Laparoscopic myomectomy (laparoscopic hysteromyomectomy) [cite: 57].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 57].
* **Induction:** Intravenous sufentanil (0.5 μg/kg) over two seconds, propofol (2 mg/kg) and cisatracurium (2 mg/kg) followed by endotracheal intubation [cite: 57].
* **Maintenance:** Propofol and remifentanil injected by electronic pump to keep blood pressure stable and BIS maintained between 40 and 60 [cite: 57]. Cisatracurium or sufentanil were added according to the actual situation [cite: 57].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Transcutaneous electrical acupoint stimulation (TEAS) [cite: 57].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 52 analyzed [cite: 57].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Bilateral LI4 (Hegu) and PC6 (Neiguan) before/during surgery; bilateral ST36 (Zusanli) and SP6 (Sanyinjiao) in the PACU [cite: 57].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** Dilatational wave with a frequency of 2/100 Hz [cite: 57].
* **Treatment intensity:** Maximum tolerable for patients receiving electrical stimulation [cite: 57].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** 30 minutes before the operation and lasting until the end of anesthesia; followed by 30 minutes while in PACU [cite: 57].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 30 min pre-induction + duration of anesthesia (mean 81.5 min), and 30 min post-anesthesia in PACU [cite: 57].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** Two continuous sessions (one perioperative, one in PACU) [cite: 57].
* **Total treatment period:** Single day (perioperative) [cite: 57].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Tropisetron 5 mg given immediately at the start of surgery to prevent PONV [cite: 57]. Flurbiprofen 100 mg for postoperative analgesia [cite: 57]. Ropivacaine for local infiltration analgesia around the incision at the end of the procedure [cite: 57].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Control group [cite: 57].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 53 analyzed [cite: 57].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Patients in Group C did not undergo TEAS [cite: 57].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Flurbiprofen and ropivacaine local infiltration [cite: 57].
* **Dosage parameters:** Flurbiprofen 100 mg [cite: 57].
* **Duration:** Administered at the end of the procedure, with pain evaluated at 1 h and 24 h postoperatively [cite: 57].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Numerical Rating Scale (NRS) (0-10) [cite: 57].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Not specifically distinguished [cite: 57].
* **Pain assessment time points:** PACU, 1 h, and 24 h after the operation [cite: 57].
* **Median pain score for each group (median (IQR)):**
    * PACU highest NRS score: Group T = 0 (0,1); Group C = 0 (0,2) [cite: 57].
    * NRS at 1 h: Group T = 2 (1,4); Group C = 3 (2,4) [cite: 57].
    * NRS at 24 h: Group T = 2 (2,3); Group C = 3 (3,4) [cite: 57].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** Group T had a significantly lower maximum NRS score in PACU (P = 0.00), lower NRS at 1 hour (P = 0.04), and lower NRS at 24 hours (P = 0.00) [cite: 57].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Sufentanil (induction) and Remifentanil (maintenance) [cite: 57].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous [cite: 57].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** Induction Sufentanil = 22.6 ± 2.9 μg; Duration Remifentanil = 710.1 ± 276.9 μg (extracted as 10.1 ± 276.9 μg due to OCR error in Table 2) [cite: 57].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** Induction Sufentanil = 21.6 ± 2.7 μg; Duration Remifentanil = 854.0 ± 287.5 μg [cite: 57].
* **Measurement time point(s):** Intraoperative [cite: 57].
* **Between-group difference:** The dosage of remifentanil used in Group T was significantly lower by almost 15% relative to Group C (P = 0.04) [cite: 57]. Sufentanil induction dosage showed no significant difference (P = 0.09) [cite: 57].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Utilization rate of analgesic drugs in 24h: Group T = 19 (37%); Group C = 23 (43%) [cite: 57]. The utilization rate of remedial drugs in PACU: Group T = 10 (19%); Group C = 18 (34%) [cite: 57].
* **Between-group difference:** Analgesic drug use at 24 h and remedial drug use in PACU were less frequent in Group T, but these did not reach statistical significance (P = 0.47 and P = 0.09, respectively) [cite: 57].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** 
    * Vomiting at 1 h: Group T = 2 (4%); Group C = 3 (5%) (P = 0.66) [cite: 57].
    * Vomiting at 24 h: Group T = 9 (17%); Group C = 19 (35%) (P = 0.03) [cite: 57]. Group T showed less frequent vomiting within 24 h than Group C [cite: 57].
* **Gastrointestinal recovery:** 
    * First postoperative anal exhaust time (min): Group T = 1020.0 (675.8, 1278.0); Group C = 1210.0 (896.5, 1427.0) (P = 0.01) [cite: 57].
    * First solid food tolerance time (min): Group T = 1207.5 (998.5, 1414.5); Group C = 1328.0 (1133.5, 1521.5) (P = 0.02) [cite: 57].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** QoR-40 total score at 24 hours after operation was significantly higher in Group T (182.7 ± 6.9) compared to Group C (176.4 ± 7.1) (P = 0.00) [cite: 57].
* **Length of stay:** Postoperative hospitalization time (Days): Group T = 4.1 ± 2.3; Group C = 4.1 ± 1.5 (P = 0.88) [cite: 57].
* **Other intraoperative outcomes:** Group T patients had a significantly lower incidence of propofol injection pain (42% vs 81%, P = 0.00) and lower intraoperative hypotension (19% vs 45%, P = 0.00) compared to Group C [cite: 57].
