# Cochrane Risk of Bias 2 (RoB 2) Assessment

## Study & Result Information
- **Study Citation:** Li S, Hu C, Zhu J, et al. Effects of Preoperative Electroacupuncture on Remifentanil-Induced Post-Infusion Hyperalgesia in Patients Undergoing Thyroidectomy: A Double-Blind Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Pain Research. 2022;15:1581–1590.
- **Source Document in Google Drive:** `getfile.php.pdf`
- **Trial Registration:** Chinese Clinical Trial Registry ChiCTR2100050235
- **Population:** 80 adult patients (aged 18–65, ASA I–II) scheduled for elective open thyroidectomy under remifentanil-based general anesthesia (May to October 2021).
- **Intervention:** Preoperative EA at bilateral Hegu (LI4) and Neiguan (PC6) applied 30 minutes before anesthesia induction (2/100 Hz dense-disperse wave, current 1–3 mA adjusted to deqi sensation; n = 40 analyzed).
- **Comparator:** Sham EA with identical acupuncture needles inserted 1 cm lateral to LI4 and PC6 with mock stimulation (0 mA; n = 39 analyzed).
- **Assessed Outcome:** Mechanical Pain Threshold Surrounding the Incision
- **Assessed Timepoint:** 24 hours postoperatively
- **Numerical Result:**
  - Mechanical pain threshold at 24 h postoperatively was significantly higher in the EA group than in the Sham group (mean ± SD: 4.88 ± 0.62 g vs. 3.42 ± 0.58 g, P < 0.001), indicating attenuation of remifentanil-induced hyperalgesia. Total postoperative sufentanil consumption at 24 h was also significantly lower in EA group (16.4 ± 3.2 µg vs. 23.8 ± 4.1 µg, P < 0.001).

### Step 1: Study Design Verification
- **Experimental Design:** Prospective, randomized, double-blind, sham-controlled parallel-group trial (1:1 ratio).
- **Unit of Randomization:** Individual patient.

---

### Step 2: Signaling Questions & Domain Evaluations

#### Domain 1: Risk of bias arising from the randomization process
- **1.1 Random sequence generation:** Yes (Y)
  - **Evidence:** "Participants were randomly allocated... using a random number table generated by SPSS 25.0 software." (p. 1582, col. 2).
- **1.2 Allocation concealment:** Yes (Y)
  - **Evidence:** "Group assignments were enclosed in sequentially numbered, opaque, sealed envelopes opened by an acupuncturist prior to induction." (p. 1582, col. 2).
- **1.3 Baseline balance:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** Age, sex, BMI, baseline mechanical pain threshold, and remifentanil infusion doses were balanced (P > 0.05; Table 1).
- **Domain 1 Judgment:** **Low Risk of Bias**

#### Domain 2: Risk of bias due to deviations from the intended interventions
- **2.1 Participant awareness:** Probably Yes (PY)
  - **Evidence:** Preoperative awake application of electrical current (1–3 mA deqi) vs. 0 mA sham in awake patients creates detectable sensory disparity prior to induction.
- **2.2 Carers awareness:** Probably No (PN)
  - **Evidence:** Anesthesiologists administering maintenance anesthesia, surgeons, and ward nurses were blinded.
- **2.3 Contextual deviations:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** Standardized remifentanil infusion and postoperative rescue analgesia protocol.
- **2.5 Appropriate analysis:** Probably Yes (PY)
  - **Evidence:** 80 randomized; 1 patient in sham group excluded due to surgical conversion to radical neck dissection; 79 analyzed (40 EA vs. 39 Sham).
- **Domain 2 Judgment:** **Some concerns Risk of Bias**

#### Domain 3: Risk of bias due to missing outcome data
- **3.1 Data completeness:** Yes (Y)
  - **Evidence:** 79 of 80 patients (98.75%) completed follow-up with complete mechanical threshold testing; balanced exclusion (1/40 in sham).
- **Domain 3 Judgment:** **Low Risk of Bias**

#### Domain 4: Risk of bias in measurement of the outcome
- **4.1 Inappropriate measurement:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** Standard electronic von Frey aesthesiometer (validated measurement tool for mechanical hyperalgesia).
- **4.2 Differ between groups:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** Identical von Frey testing protocol applied 1 cm from the thyroid incision.
- **4.3 Assessors aware:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** "The outcome evaluator measuring mechanical thresholds was unaware of group assignments." (p. 1583, col. 1).
- **Domain 4 Judgment:** **Low Risk of Bias**

#### Domain 5: Risk of bias in selection of the reported result
- **5.1 Pre-specified analysis plan:** Probably No (PN)
  - **Evidence:** Registered in Chinese Clinical Trial Registry (ChiCTR2100050235) on August 24, 2021; study enrollment started May 2021 (retrospective registration).
- **5.2 Result selected:** Probably No (PN)
  - **Evidence:** Mechanical threshold and pain scores reported at all prespecified time points (2, 6, 12, 24, 48 h).
- **5.3 Multiple analyses:** Probably No (PN)
  - **Evidence:** Primary outcome reported as standard continuous measure.
- **Domain 5 Judgment:** **Some concerns Risk of Bias**
