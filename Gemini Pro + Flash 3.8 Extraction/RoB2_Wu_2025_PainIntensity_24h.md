# Cochrane Risk of Bias 2 (RoB 2) Assessment

## Study & Result Information
- **Study Citation:** Wu S, Chen Z, Liu X, et al. Transcutaneous Electrical Acupoint Stimulation (TEAS) Facilitates Postoperative Recovery in Day Surgery: A Randomized Controlled Trial. Pain and Therapy. 2025;14(1):145–158.
- **Source Document in Google Drive:** `103940.pdf`
- **Trial Registration:** Chinese Clinical Trial Registry ChiCTR2200066600
- **Population:** 100 day-surgery patients (aged 18–65 years, ASA I–II) undergoing laparoscopic cholecystectomy or inguinal hernia repair under general anesthesia.
- **Intervention:** TEAS applied to bilateral Hegu (LI4), Neiguan (PC6), and Zusanli (ST36) upon PACU arrival for 30 minutes (dense-disperse frequency 2/100 Hz, current 6–15 mA adjusted to strong comfortable sensation; n = 50 analyzed).
- **Comparator:** Sham TEAS with identical electrodes and lead wires attached to the same acupoints with 0 mA electrical current (n = 50 analyzed).
- **Assessed Outcome:** Postoperative Pain Intensity at Rest (VAS 0–10)
- **Assessed Timepoint:** 24 hours postoperatively
- **Numerical Result:**
  - VAS pain score at rest at 24 hours postoperatively was significantly lower in the TEAS group compared with the Sham group (mean ± SD: 1.8 ± 0.7 vs. 2.9 ± 0.9, P < 0.001). Quality of recovery (QoR-15 score at 24 h: 136.2 ± 6.4 vs. 128.4 ± 7.1, P < 0.001) and time to discharge readiness were also significantly improved.

### Step 1: Study Design Verification
- **Experimental Design:** Prospective, randomized, double-blind, sham-controlled parallel-group trial (1:1 ratio).
- **Unit of Randomization:** Individual patient.

---

### Step 2: Signaling Questions & Domain Evaluations

#### Domain 1: Risk of bias arising from the randomization process
- **1.1 Random sequence generation:** Yes (Y)
  - **Evidence:** "Randomization was generated by an independent statistician using SAS 9.4 software." (p. 147, col. 2).
- **1.2 Allocation concealment:** Yes (Y)
  - **Evidence:** "Allocations were placed in sequentially numbered, opaque, sealed envelopes that remained unopened until the patient arrived in the PACU." (p. 147, col. 2).
- **1.3 Baseline balance:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** Age, sex, BMI, surgical procedure, duration of anesthesia, and intraoperative opioid doses were balanced (P > 0.05; Table 1).
- **Domain 1 Judgment:** **Low Risk of Bias**

#### Domain 2: Risk of bias due to deviations from the intended interventions
- **2.1 Participant awareness:** Probably No (PN)
  - **Evidence:** Initiated in PACU during emergence/early recovery with identical sham device; blinding index indicated successful patient blinding.
- **2.2 Carers awareness:** Probably No (PN)
  - **Evidence:** "Attending anesthesiologists, surgeons, and ward nurses were unaware of group assignment." (p. 148, col. 1).
- **2.3 Contextual deviations:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** Standardized day surgery protocol and rescue analgesia (oral celecoxib / tramadol) administered identically.
- **2.5 Appropriate analysis:** Yes (Y)
  - **Evidence:** 100 randomized (50 TEAS, 50 Sham); all 100 analyzed in primary analysis (100% ITT).
- **Domain 2 Judgment:** **Low Risk of Bias**

#### Domain 3: Risk of bias due to missing outcome data
- **3.1 Data completeness:** Yes (Y)
  - **Evidence:** Complete 24-hour follow-up obtained for all 100 participants (100% follow-up completion).
- **Domain 3 Judgment:** **Low Risk of Bias**

#### Domain 4: Risk of bias in measurement of the outcome
- **4.1 Inappropriate measurement:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** Validated 10-cm Visual Analog Scale (VAS 0–10).
- **4.2 Differ between groups:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** Identical evaluation protocol at predetermined time points (PACU discharge, 6, 12, 24 h).
- **4.3 Assessors aware:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** "Trained research personnel who performed postoperative evaluations were blinded to group allocation." (p. 148, col. 1).
- **Domain 4 Judgment:** **Low Risk of Bias**

#### Domain 5: Risk of bias in selection of the reported result
- **5.1 Pre-specified analysis plan:** Yes (Y)
  - **Evidence:** Prospectively registered in Chinese Clinical Trial Registry (ChiCTR2200066600) on December 11, 2022.
- **5.2 Result selected:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** Pain scores at rest and movement and QoR-15 scores reported across all prespecified time points.
- **5.3 Multiple analyses:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** Standard pre-specified continuous analysis.
- **Domain 5 Judgment:** **Low Risk of Bias**
