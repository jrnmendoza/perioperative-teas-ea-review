# Cochrane Risk of Bias 2 (RoB 2) Assessment

## Study & Result Information
- **Study Citation:** Zhan W, Tian W, Wang J, et al. Transcutaneous electrical acupoint stimulation combined with transversus abdominis plane block for postoperative analgesia after laparoscopic colorectal cancer surgery: A randomized controlled trial. European Journal of Integrative Medicine. 2020;35:101067.
- **Source Document in Google Drive:** `covidence_1389_full_article.pdf`
- **Trial Registration:** None reported
- **Population:** 90 patients (aged 18–75 years, ASA I–II) undergoing laparoscopic radical resection of colorectal cancer under general anesthesia.
- **Intervention:** TEAS at bilateral Zusanli (ST36) and Hegu (LI4) combined with ultrasound-guided bilateral TAP block (ropivacaine 0.375%), with TEAS initiated 30 min before anesthesia induction and continued until end of surgery (dense-disperse frequency 2/100 Hz, current 10–20 mA; n = 30 analyzed).
- **Comparator:** TAP block alone with sham TEAS (identical electrodes placed with 0 mA current; n = 30 analyzed). A third control group received general anesthesia alone with sham TAP and sham TEAS (n = 30 analyzed).
- **Assessed Outcome:** Cumulative Sufentanil Consumption
- **Assessed Timepoint:** 24 hours postoperatively
- **Numerical Result:**
  - Cumulative sufentanil consumption at 24 hours postoperatively was significantly lower in the TEAS + TAP group compared with the TAP alone group and Control group (mean ± SD: 38.2 ± 5.6 µg vs. 47.5 ± 6.2 µg vs. 58.4 ± 7.1 µg, P < 0.001). Rest and coughing VAS pain scores and time to first flatus were also significantly improved in TEAS + TAP group.

### Step 1: Study Design Verification
- **Experimental Design:** Prospective, randomized, single-blind, sham-controlled 3-arm parallel-group clinical trial (1:1:1 ratio).
- **Unit of Randomization:** Individual patient.

---

### Step 2: Signaling Questions & Domain Evaluations

#### Domain 1: Risk of bias arising from the randomization process
- **1.1 Random sequence generation:** Yes (Y)
  - **Evidence:** "Random numbers were generated by a computer-generated random list." (p. 2, col. 2).
- **1.2 Allocation concealment:** Yes (Y)
  - **Evidence:** "The allocation sequence was concealed in sealed opaque envelopes until intervention delivery." (p. 2, col. 2).
- **1.3 Baseline balance:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** Age, sex, BMI, ASA physical status, tumor location, and duration of surgery were balanced across groups (P > 0.05; Table 1).
- **Domain 1 Judgment:** **Low Risk of Bias**

#### Domain 2: Risk of bias due to deviations from the intended interventions
- **2.1 Participant awareness:** Probably Yes (PY)
  - **Evidence:** Pre-induction awake stimulation with active tingling sensation (10–20 mA) vs. turned-off sham stimulator in awake patients.
- **2.2 Carers awareness:** Probably No (PN)
  - **Evidence:** "Treating surgeons, ward nurses, and postoperative care teams were blinded to group assignments." (p. 2, col. 2).
- **2.3 Contextual deviations:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** Standardized general anesthesia regimen and standardized PCIA sufentanil rescue analgesia protocol.
- **2.5 Appropriate analysis:** Yes (Y)
  - **Evidence:** 90 randomized (30 per arm); all 90 completed and analyzed in assigned groups (100% ITT).
- **Domain 2 Judgment:** **Some concerns Risk of Bias**

#### Domain 3: Risk of bias due to missing outcome data
- **3.1 Data completeness:** Yes (Y)
  - **Evidence:** Complete 24-hour follow-up data available for all 90 randomized participants (100% follow-up completion).
- **Domain 3 Judgment:** **Low Risk of Bias**

#### Domain 4: Risk of bias in measurement of the outcome
- **4.1 Inappropriate measurement:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** Electronic PCIA pump microprocessor records of cumulative sufentanil consumption (µg).
- **4.2 Differ between groups:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** Identical PCIA programming across all three groups.
- **4.3 Assessors aware:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** "The postoperative outcome assessor and statistical analyst were strictly blinded to group allocation." (p. 2, col. 2).
- **Domain 4 Judgment:** **Low Risk of Bias**

#### Domain 5: Risk of bias in selection of the reported result
- **5.1 Pre-specified analysis plan:** No Information (NI)
  - **Evidence:** No trial registration number or protocol publication reported in the published manuscript.
- **5.2 Result selected:** Probably No (PN)
  - **Evidence:** Sufentanil consumption, VAS scores at rest and coughing, and time to gastrointestinal recovery reported.
- **5.3 Multiple analyses:** Probably No (PN)
  - **Evidence:** Standard pre-specified ANOVA and pairwise comparisons.
- **Domain 5 Judgment:** **Some concerns Risk of Bias**
