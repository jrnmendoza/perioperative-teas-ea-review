## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Funded by Beijing Hope Run Special Fund of Cancer Foundation of China (LC2021L05), the Special Research Fund for Central Universities, Peking Union Medical College (3332021033) and Beijing Hope Run Special Fund of Cancer Foundation of China (LC2022A17) [cite: 43].
* **Country:** China [cite: 43].
* **Journal:** Breast Cancer [cite: 43].
* **DOI:** 10.1007/s12282-024-01606-1 [cite: 43].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** Chictr.org.cn registry system (ChiCTR2100053752) [cite: 43]. Ethical approval provided by the Ethical Committee of National Cancer Center/Cancer Hospital, Beijing, China (approval number: 21/391-3062) [cite: 43].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Prospective, double-blinded, randomized controlled trial [cite: 43].
* **Randomization method:** A random allocation sequence was generated by using computer-generated random numbers [cite: 43]. A physician independent of the study inserted treatment strategies into sequentially numbered envelopes [cite: 43].
* **Blinding:** Double-blinded [cite: 43]. The investigator administering the interventions was not blinded [cite: 43]. Outcomes were assessed by trained investigators who were blinded to the allocation [cite: 43]. Patients, anesthesiologists, surgeons, and ward staff were unaware of the allocation [cite: 43]. Sham TEAS used dummy electrode wires with the same power indicator and sound [cite: 43].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** Patients were first assigned to a severe nausea and vomiting during pregnancy (SNVP) group or a mild-moderate nausea and vomiting during pregnancy (MNVP) group based on history [cite: 43]. Then randomized to ondansetron plus dexamethasone (OD) or Ondan + Dex + TEAS (ODT) [cite: 43]. Subgroups: SNVP-OD, SNVP-ODT, MNVP-OD, MNVP-ODT [cite: 43].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 169 assessed; 140 enrolled (SNVP: n=70; MNVP: n=70); 138 completed and analyzed (SNVP-OD: n=35; SNVP-ODT: n=33; MNVP-OD: n=35; MNVP-ODT: n=35) [cite: 43].
* **Age:** SNVP-OD: 49.9 ± 9.5 years; SNVP-ODT: 47.4 ± 8.2 years; MNVP-OD: 49.3 ± 10.0 years; MNVP-ODT: 49.0 ± 8.5 years [cite: 43].
* **Sex distribution:** 100% Female (undergoing breast cancer surgery) [cite: 43].
* **BMI:** SNVP-OD: 24.6 ± 3.6 kg/m²; SNVP-ODT: 23.7 ± 2.7 kg/m²; MNVP-OD: 24.5 ± 4.0 kg/m²; MNVP-ODT: 24.4 ± 3.6 kg/m² [cite: 43].
* **ASA classification:** ASA physical status I or II [cite: 43].
* **Comorbidities:** Included patients with history of at least one full-term pregnancy and unilateral breast cancer [cite: 43]. Excluded patients with history of motion sickness, diabetes, GI diseases, peptic ulcers, endocrinological disorders, liver diseases, recent corticosteroid/antiemetic use, drug abuse, or current pregnancy/breastfeeding [cite: 43].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Breast cancer surgery [cite: 43].
* **Specific procedures:** Radical mastectomy + axillary dissection, radical mastectomy + SNLB, segmental mastectomy + SNLB, segmental mastectomy + axillary dissection, and partial mastectomy [cite: 43].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 43].
* **Induction:** Propofol 1-2 mg/kg, sufentanil 0.3-0.6 μg/kg, and cis-atracurium 0.2-0.4 mg/kg [cite: 43].
* **Maintenance:** 1.5-2.5% sevoflurane constantly inhaled [cite: 43]. Sufentanil 0.1 μg/kg added intraoperatively as required [cite: 43].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Transcutaneous electrical acupoint stimulation (TEAS) [cite: 43].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** SNVP-ODT: 33; MNVP-ODT: 35 [cite: 43].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Bilateral Neiguan (PC6) and Hegu (LI4) [cite: 43].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** 2/100 Hz [cite: 43].
* **Treatment intensity:** 10 mA [cite: 43].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** Before anesthetic induction [cite: 43].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 30 minutes [cite: 43].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** One single preoperative session [cite: 43].
* **Total treatment period:** Single day (preoperative) [cite: 43].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** 5 mg dexamethasone during induction, and 4 mg ondansetron at the end of surgery (OD) [cite: 43].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Sham stimulation + OD [cite: 43].
* **Number of participants in control group:** SNVP-OD: 35; MNVP-OD: 35 [cite: 43].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Sham stimulation at the same points using sham electrode wires (cut output wires resulting in no current), but with the same operating power indicator and sound [cite: 43]. Received same OD regimen [cite: 43].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Flurbiprofen axetil [cite: 43].
* **Dosage parameters:** 100 mg flurbiprofen axetil administered at the end of surgery, and as rescue if VAS pain score ≥ 3 [cite: 43].
* **Duration:** Tracked up to 36 hours postoperatively [cite: 43].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Visual analog scale (VAS) (0-10) [cite: 43].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Not specifically distinguished [cite: 43].
* **Pain assessment time points:** 2, 6, 12, 24, and 36 h after operation [cite: 43].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group:** Not explicitly reported; pain was assessed to determine if rescue analgesia was needed [cite: 43].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** Not reported [cite: 43].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Sufentanil [cite: 43].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous [cite: 43].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** SNVP-ODT: 21.2 ± 3.1 μg; MNVP-ODT: 21.0 ± 3.4 μg [cite: 43].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** SNVP-OD: 21.9 ± 3.2 μg; MNVP-OD: 20.9 ± 3.1 μg [cite: 43].
* **Measurement time point(s):** Intraoperative [cite: 43].
* **Between-group difference:** Sufentanil consumption did not differ significantly between SNVP subgroups (P = 0.402) or MNVP subgroups (P = 0.854) [cite: 43].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Flurbiprofen axetil (100 mg) was administered if pain VAS score was ≥ 3 [cite: 43]. The specific proportion requiring rescue was not reported [cite: 43].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting (Primary Outcome):** 
    * The incidence of PONV in the SNVP group was significantly higher than that in the MNVP group at 2, 6, 12, and 24 h [cite: 43].
    * SNVP-ODT vs SNVP-OD PONV incidence: 6-12 h (21.2% vs 45.7%, P = 0.03); 12-24 h (6.1% vs 31.4%, P = 0.008) [cite: 43].
    * MNVP-ODT vs MNVP-OD PONV incidence: 0-2 h (5.7% vs 25.7%, P = 0.022); 2-6 h (5.7% vs 22.9%, P = 0.04) [cite: 43].
    * Within 6 h, there were fewer vomiting cases in the SNVP-ODT group compared to SNVP-OD (7/33 vs. 19/35, P = 0.005) [cite: 43].
* **GDF-15 (Secondary Outcome):** 
    * Postoperative mean serum levels of GDF-15 on Day 1 were significantly higher than on day 0 in all patients (P < 0.001) [cite: 43].
    * Preoperative GDF-15 serum levels were significantly higher in patients with SNVP compared to MNVP (P = 0.004) [cite: 43].
    * Higher preoperative GDF-15 levels correlated with a higher incidence of PONV (P = 0.043) [cite: 43].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** Not reported.
* **Length of stay:** Not reported [cite: 43].
* **Other postoperative complications:** Two patients were excluded due to postoperative fever [cite: 43].
