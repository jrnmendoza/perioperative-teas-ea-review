## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Supported by the Collaborative Fund of Chongqing Municipal Health and Health Committee and the Chongqing Science and Technology Bureau of China (grant 2019ZY3407, 2017HBRC001, CSTC2019jscx-msxmX0214) [cite: 41].
* **Country:** China [cite: 41].
* **Journal:** Obesity Surgery [cite: 41].
* **DOI:** 10.1007/s11695-020-05205-9 [cite: 41].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** Chinese Clinical Trial Registry (ChiCTR2000037785) [cite: 41]. Approved by the institutional ethics committee of the first affiliated hospital of Chongqing Medical University [cite: 41].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Prospective, randomized, controlled trial [cite: 41].
* **Randomization method:** Computerized random number generator. Group assignment was exposed from a sealed envelope by an acupuncturist when patients arrived at the operating room [cite: 41].
* **Blinding:** Single-blind [cite: 41]. The patients were blinded to the group assignment [cite: 41]. An anesthetic resident performing follow-up and data collection was blinded to group assignments [cite: 41]. The anesthetists and the acupuncturist were not blinded [cite: 41].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** TEAS combined with dexamethasone and tropisetron (TEAS group) and dexamethasone and tropisetron alone (Control group) [cite: 41].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 67 screened; 62 randomized and completed the study (TEAS group: n=31; Control group: n=31) [cite: 41].
* **Age:** TEAS group: 27.5 ± 8.0 years; Control group: 27.3 ± 8.3 years (mean (SD)) [cite: 41].
* **Sex distribution:** 100% Female [cite: 41].
* **BMI:** TEAS group: 39.1 ± 6.5 kg/m²; Control group: 39.9 ± 5.8 kg/m² [cite: 41].
* **ASA classification:** ASA physical status class II or III (text says II or IV, but Table 1 says II, III, IV) [cite: 41]. Table 1 data: TEAS group (II: 16, III: 14, IV: 1); Control group (II: 17, III: 13, IV: 1) [cite: 41].
* **Comorbidities:** Included patients undergoing elective laparoscopic sleeve gastrectomy [cite: 41]. Excluded patients with contraindications for TEAS, cardiac pacemaker/defibrillator, communication difficulties, psychiatric/neurological disease, pre-use of antiemetics/opioids/glucocorticoids, or gastroesophageal reflux [cite: 41].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Laparoscopic sleeve gastrectomy (LSG) [cite: 41].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 41].
* **Induction:** Midazolam (2 mg), propofol (1-2 mg/kg), sufentanil (0.5 μg/kg), and rocuronium (0.9 mg/kg) [cite: 41].
* **Maintenance:** Desflurane in air and intravenous infusion of dexmedetomidine (0.5 μg/kg/h) combined with remifentanil (0.1-0.2 μg/kg/h) [cite: 41].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Transcutaneous electrical acupoint stimulation (TEAS) [cite: 41].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 31 analyzed [cite: 41].
* **Acupuncture points used:** PC6 and ST36 [cite: 41].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** Dense disperse frequency of 2/10 Hz [cite: 41].
* **Treatment intensity:** Adjusted according to individual requirements, starting at 1 mA and increasing gradually to obtain the maximum tolerance for a slight twitching of local muscles [cite: 41].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** Preoperative, intraoperative, and postoperative (2 h and 6 h after surgery) [cite: 41].
* **Duration of each treatment:** Started 30 min before induction and maintained during operation. Postoperative sessions not detailed for specific duration [cite: 41].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** Three distinct phases: perioperative (started 30 min pre-induction and maintained intraoperatively), and two postoperative sessions (at 2 h and 6 h after surgery) [cite: 41].
* **Total treatment period:** Single day (perioperative up to 6 h postoperatively) [cite: 41].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Prophylactic dexamethasone (10 mg IV) during anesthesia induction and tropisetron (4 mg IV) at the start of skin closure [cite: 41]. Postoperative patient-controlled analgesia (sufentanil) [cite: 41].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Control group (dexamethasone and tropisetron) with placebo TEAS [cite: 41].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 31 analyzed [cite: 41].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Gel electrodes were applied and connected to an acupuncture instrument without stimulation [cite: 41]. Received the same prophylactic antiemetics [cite: 41].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Patient-controlled analgesia (PCA) device with sufentanil [cite: 41]. Intravenous parecoxib (40 mg) and incision infiltration of 0.5% ropivacaine (10 mL) administered at the end of surgery [cite: 41].
* **Dosage parameters:** Sufentanil (1.5 μg/h background infusion, 1 μg bolus, 20 min lockout time) [cite: 41].
* **Duration:** Measured postoperatively [cite: 41].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Visual analogue scale (VAS) [cite: 41].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** At rest and during movement [cite: 41].
* **Pain assessment time points:** 2, 6, 12, 24, 36, and 48 h after surgery [cite: 41].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group:** Specific scores not reported [cite: 41].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** We observed no significant difference in pain VAS scores at rest (P = 0.404) and during movement (P = 0.423) within 48 h after surgery [cite: 41].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Sufentanil and Remifentanil [cite: 41]. Morphine equivalents evaluated [cite: 41].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous [cite: 41].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** Intraoperative = 1.8 ± 0.4 mg morphine equivalents/kg; Postoperative = 0.6 ± 0.2 mg morphine equivalents/kg [cite: 41].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** Intraoperative = 1.8 ± 0.4 mg morphine equivalents/kg; Postoperative = 0.6 ± 0.1 mg morphine equivalents/kg [cite: 41].
* **Measurement time point(s):** Intraoperative and Postoperative [cite: 41].
* **Between-group difference:** There was no difference in postoperative opioid consumption between the groups based on comparisons of morphine equivalents [cite: 41].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Three patients in the TEAS group and four patients in the control group experienced moderate pain in the first 12 h after surgery, which was relieved with increased background infusion of the PCA device [cite: 41].
* **Between-group difference:** Not reported [cite: 41].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting (Primary Outcome):** 
    * PONV incidence within 48 h: TEAS group = 13/31 (41.9%); Control group = 24/31 (77.4%) (P = 0.004) [cite: 41].
    * Clinically important PONV: TEAS group = 5 (16.1%); Control group = 15 (48.4%) (P = 0.007) [cite: 41].
    * Antiemetic rescue medication required: TEAS group = 9 (29.0%); Control group = 18 (58.1%) (P = 0.021) [cite: 41].
* **Gastrointestinal recovery:** Time to flatus (h): TEAS group = 23.6 ± 7.7; Control group = 32.3 ± 13.2 (P = 0.003) [cite: 41]. Time to oral diet (h): TEAS = 47.6 (42, 75); Control = 46 (42, 51) (P = 0.810) [cite: 41].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** Time to mobility (days): TEAS = 27.5 (11.9); Control = 29.6 (13.7) (P = 0.522) [cite: 41]. Note: Mobility values appear unusually high for 'days', may be hours. Time to extubation and time in recovery room did not significantly differ [cite: 41].
* **Length of stay:** Postoperative hospital stays (days): TEAS = 3 (3,4); Control = 3 (3,4) (P = 0.981) [cite: 41].
* **Hospitalization costs:** Postoperative hospital costs (¥, thousand): TEAS = 45.8 ± 8.0; Control = 46.2 ± 5.9 (P = 0.652) [cite: 41].
* **Other postoperative complications:** One patient in the TEAS group experienced itching under the electrodes. Two patients in the control group experienced complications (central vomiting, stitch leakage) [cite: 41].
