# Data Extraction: covidence_1930_full_article.pdf (Yang et al., 2024)

## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Funded by Institutional Foundation of Jiangsu province hospital of Chinese Medicine (Y18021) [cite: 43].
* **Country:** China (Nanjing) [cite: 43].
* **Journal:** EXPLORE [cite: 43].
* **DOI:** 10.1016/j.explore.2023.11.004 [cite: 43].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** Chinese Clinical Trial Registry (ChiCTR1900024840) [cite: 43]. Approved by Ethics Committee of Jiangsu Province Hospital of Chinese Medicine [cite: 43].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Single-center, prospective, randomized open-label trial [cite: 43].
* **Randomization method:** Computer-generated codes and sequentially numbered, opaque envelopes [cite: 43].
* **Blinding:** Open-label for patients and acupuncturist [cite: 43]. Anesthetists, assessors, the data collector as well as statisticians were blinded to treatment allocation [cite: 43].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** Electroacupuncture (EA) group and usual care (UC) group [cite: 43].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 184 assessed; 180 randomized (EA: n=90, UC: n=90); 90 per group included in the intention-to-treat analyses (3 patients withdrew but were retained in final analysis) [cite: 43].
* **Age:** EA group: 52.7 ± 11.0 years; UC group: 55.8 ± 11.0 years (mean ± SD) [cite: 43].
* **Sex distribution:** EA group: 24 Male (26.7%) / 66 Female (73.3%); UC group: 34 Male (37.8%) / 56 Female (62.2%) [cite: 43].
* **BMI:** Weight: EA group = 62.8 ± 10.0 kg; UC group = 61.8 ± 10.0 kg [cite: 43]. BMI not explicitly reported [cite: 43].
* **ASA classification:** American Society of Anesthesiologists grade I-II [cite: 43].
* **Comorbidities:** Included patients scheduled for elective video-assisted thoracoscopic lobectomy or segmental resection surgery [cite: 43]. Excluded severe systemic disease, cognitive dysfunction, psychological disorder, history of esophageal/gastric surgery, history of EA treatment/allergy, pregnancy/breastfeeding, pacemakers, severe infection, chronic opioid use, or failure of PVB [cite: 43]. 

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Thoracoscopic lung surgery (video-assisted thoracoscopic lobectomy or segmental resection surgery) [cite: 43].
* **Specific procedures:** Lobectomy (EA: 29; UC: 40); Segmentectomy (EA: 61; UC: 50) [cite: 43].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 43].
* **Induction:** Propofol 2 mg/kg, sufentanil 0.4 μg/kg, and vecuronium 0.1 mg/kg [cite: 43].
* **Maintenance:** Target-controlled infusions of propofol and remifentanil according to hemodynamics and BIS [cite: 43].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Electroacupuncture (EA) [cite: 43].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 90 analyzed (ITT) [cite: 43].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Bilateral PC6 (Neiguan), ST36 (Zusanli), and ST37 (Shangjuxu) [cite: 43].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** 15 Hz frequency [cite: 43].
* **Treatment intensity:** Adjusted to trigger slight vibration of the needle handles according to individual tolerance [cite: 43].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** 24 h prior to surgery, 4 h and 24 h after surgery [cite: 43].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 30 minutes [cite: 43].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** Three sessions in total (one preoperative, two postoperative) [cite: 43].
* **Total treatment period:** Up to 24 hours postoperatively [cite: 43].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Ultrasound guidance paravertebral nerve block with 20 ml of 0.375% ropivacaine [cite: 43]. Ondansetron 4 mg and dexamethasone 5 mg before skin closure for PONV [cite: 43]. Intravenous parecoxib 40 mg at the end of surgery [cite: 43]. Routine oral celecoxib 200 mg every 12 h and postoperative intravenous patient-controlled analgesia (PCA) [cite: 43]. 

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Usual care (UC) group [cite: 43].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 90 analyzed (ITT) [cite: 43].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** No acupuncture treatment was performed [cite: 43]. Routine treatments included nursing care, analgesics, early ambulation, and oral feeding [cite: 43].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Postoperative intravenous PCA (morphine 1 mg/kg, ketamine 1 mg/kg, tropisetron 10 mg), oral celecoxib 200 mg every 12 h [cite: 43].
* **Dosage parameters:** PCA basal rate of 2 ml/h with a bolus dose of 0.5 ml and a lockout time of 15 min [cite: 43]. Additional bucinnazine 100 mg intramuscularly if VAS > 4 [cite: 43].
* **Duration:** PCA used postoperatively; oral celecoxib given every 12 h [cite: 43].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Visual analogue scale (VAS, 0-10) [cite: 43].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Recorded at rest and on movement [cite: 43].
* **Pain assessment time points:** 0-24 h, 24-48 h, and 48-72 h after surgery [cite: 43].
* **Median pain score for each group (Median (IQR)):**
    * 0-24 h at rest: EA = 2 (2-3); UC = 2 (2-3) (P = 0.844) [cite: 43].
    * 0-24 h at movement: EA = 3 (3-4); UC = 3 (2-3) (P = 0.159) [cite: 43].
    * 24-48 h at rest: EA = 2 (2-2.5); UC = 2 (2-2) (P = 0.390) [cite: 43].
    * 24-48 h at movement: EA = 3 (2-3); UC = 3 (2-3) (P = 0.652) [cite: 43].
    * 48-72 h at rest: EA = 2 (2-3); UC = 2 (2-2) (P = 0.353) [cite: 43].
    * 48-72 h at movement: EA = 3 (2-3); UC = 3 (2-3) (P = 0.314) [cite: 43].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** There were no significant differences in the post-operative pain at rest or on movement between groups (P > 0.05) [cite: 43].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Sufentanil and Remifentanil (intraoperative); Morphine (postoperative PCA) [cite: 43].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous [cite: 43].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** Intraoperative Sufentanil = 32.5 (30-35) μg; Intraoperative Remifentanil = 0.5 (0.3-0.6) mg [cite: 43]. Morphine use on POD 1 = 43.7 ± 4.7 mg; Morphine use on POD 1-3 = 127.0 ± 12.0 mg [cite: 43].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** Intraoperative Sufentanil = 32 (30-35) μg; Intraoperative Remifentanil = 0.5 (0.4-0.7) mg [cite: 43]. Morphine use on POD 1 = 44.0 ± 4.9 mg; Morphine use on POD 1-3 = 127.5 ± 12.5 mg [cite: 43].
* **Measurement time point(s):** Intraoperative, POD 1, and POD 1-3 [cite: 43].
* **Between-group difference:** There was no difference in intraoperative opioids or postoperative morphine use between the two groups (P > 0.05) [cite: 43].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Not explicitly stated, though it was noted that additional bucinnazine 100 mg was given for VAS > 4 [cite: 43].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **Gastrointestinal recovery (Primary Outcome):** 
    * Time to first flatus (h): EA = 15.4 ± 3.2; UC = 17.0 ± 3.7 (P = 0.004) [cite: 43].
    * Time to first defecation (h): EA = 75.9 ± 7.9; UC = 79.7 ± 8.1 (P = 0.002) [cite: 43].
    * Incidence of abdominal distension (AD): EA = 31 (34.4%); UC = 46 (51.1%) (P = 0.024) [cite: 43]. Rescue medication use for GI issues was less in EA group (53.3% vs 71.1%, P=0.014) [cite: 43].
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** 
    * Incidence of Nausea 0-24 h: EA = 24 (26.7%); UC = 40 (44.4%) (P = 0.013) [cite: 43].
    * Incidence of Vomiting 0-24 h: EA = 12 (13.3%); UC = 25 (27.8%) (P = 0.016) [cite: 43].
    * Incidence of PONV at 24-48 h and 48-72 h showed no significant differences between groups [cite: 43].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** Time to ambulation (h): EA = 6 (5-7); UC = 6 (5-7) (P = 0.208) [cite: 43].
* **Length of stay:** Length of hospital stay (d): EA = 6 (5-7); UC = 6 (5-7) (P = 0.202) [cite: 43].
* **Other postoperative complications:** Not explicitly reported, though it was noted that one patient discontinued EA postoperatively due to discomfort [cite: 43].
