# Data Extraction: covidence_308_full_article.pdf (Zhang et al., 2023)

## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China (No. 82074153) and the "131" talents funding from the Tianjin Health Bureau [cite: 51].
* **Country:** China (Tianjin) [cite: 51].
* **Journal:** World Journal of Surgery [cite: 51].
* **DOI:** 10.1007/s00268-023-06924-7 [cite: 51].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** clinicaltrials.gov (NCT03631160) [cite: 51]. Approved by the local institutional review board [cite: 51].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Prospective, double-blind randomized clinical trial [cite: 51].
* **Randomization method:** Randomly assigned in a 1:1 ratio using block randomization [cite: 51]. The randomized numbers were enclosed in sequentially numbered, sealed, opaque envelopes [cite: 51].
* **Blinding:** Double-blind [cite: 51]. Participants, outcome assessors, staff responsible for postoperative care, and statisticians were blinded to the treatment allocation [cite: 51]. The acupuncturist providing the treatment was not blinded [cite: 51].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** TEAS group and Sham group [cite: 51].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 3,977 assessed for eligibility; 1,948 randomized (TEAS: n=975; Sham: n=973); 1,838 completed and were analyzed (TEAS: n=922; Sham: n=916) [cite: 51].
* **Age:** TEAS group: 58.9 ± 8.6 years; Sham group: 59.1 ± 8.4 years (mean ± SD) [cite: 51].
* **Sex distribution:** TEAS group: 360 Male (39.0%) / 562 Female (61.0%); Sham group: 383 Male (41.8%) / 533 Female (58.2%) [cite: 51].
* **BMI:** TEAS group: 25.2 ± 3.3 kg/m²; Sham group: 25.2 ± 3.4 kg/m² [cite: 51].
* **ASA classification:** Patients with ASA grade ≥ IV were excluded [cite: 51]. Specific breakdown of ASA I-III not explicitly detailed [cite: 51].
* **Comorbidities:** Included patients aged 40-75 years scheduled for elective laparoscopic cholecystectomy [cite: 51]. Excluded patients with preventive or intraoperative indwelling catheter, urinary tract obstruction/infections, critical condition, severe renal impairment, severe liver and kidney dysfunction, contraindications to TEAS, or previous acupuncture treatment experience [cite: 51].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Elective laparoscopic cholecystectomy [cite: 51].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 51].
* **Induction:** Etomidate (0.2-0.6 mg/kg) and sufentanil (0.2-0.5 μg/kg) [cite: 51].
* **Maintenance:** Remifentanil in combination with propofol infusion [cite: 51].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Transcutaneous electrical acupoint stimulation (TEAS) [cite: 51].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 922 analyzed [cite: 51].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Ciliao (BL32), Sanyinjiao (SP6), Zhongji (CV3), and Guanyuan (CV4) [cite: 51].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** 2-Hz sparse wave [cite: 51].
* **Treatment intensity:** Gradually increased until the strongest needle-like tingling sensation that patients could tolerate was reached [cite: 51].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** Started 30 min before the induction of anesthesia and lasted until 45 min after the surgery [cite: 51].
* **Duration of each treatment:** Maintained continuously from 30 min pre-induction through 45 min postoperatively [cite: 51].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** One single continuous perioperative session [cite: 51].
* **Total treatment period:** Single day (perioperative) [cite: 51].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Patient-controlled intravenous analgesia (PCIA) with sufentanil combined with flurbiprofen for 24-48 h postoperatively [cite: 51]. Ringer's lactate solution infused at a rate of 10 mL/kg/h [cite: 51].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Sham TEAS [cite: 51].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 916 analyzed [cite: 51].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Sham stimulus applied by cutting off the output wires; no current output was delivered [cite: 51]. The apparatus had the same working power indicator and sound as those with normal electrode lines [cite: 51].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Patient-controlled intravenous analgesia (PCIA) with sufentanil combined with flurbiprofen [cite: 51].
* **Dosage parameters:** Details on exact PCIA lockouts/boluses not specified, but cumulative opioid consumption was evaluated in morphine equivalents [cite: 51].
* **Duration:** Administered for 24-48 h postoperatively as necessary [cite: 51].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Numerical Rating Scale (NRS) [cite: 51].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Not specifically distinguished [cite: 51].
* **Pain assessment time points:** Postoperative period (exact timepoint of maximum/average not explicitly defined in the summary table) [cite: 51].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group (Median [IQR]):** 
    * TEAS group: 4 [3-6] [cite: 51].
    * Sham group: 5 [3-6] [cite: 51].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** The TEAS group experienced significantly less pain than the sham group (P = 0.007) [cite: 51].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Sufentanil and Remifentanil (converted to intravenous morphine equivalents) [cite: 51].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous [cite: 51].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group (Median [IQR]):** 
    * Intraoperative: 30.0 (25.0-35.0) mg [cite: 51].
    * Postoperative (within 48 h): 110.0 (80.0-110.0) mg [cite: 51].
* **Opioid consumption for control group (Median [IQR]):** 
    * Intraoperative: 30.0 (25.0-35.0) mg [cite: 51].
    * Postoperative (within 48 h): 110.0 (90.0-110.0) mg [cite: 51].
* **Measurement time point(s):** Intraoperative and within 48 h postoperatively [cite: 51].
* **Between-group difference:** Not explicitly stated as statistically significant; medians and IQRs were highly similar between groups [cite: 51].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** All patients received PCIA as necessary [cite: 51]. Specific proportions requiring additional rescue were not reported [cite: 51].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **Spontaneous Voiding Recovery (Primary Outcome):** 
    * Time to first spontaneous voiding (Median [IQR]): TEAS = 5.6 (3.7-8.0) h; Sham = 7.1 (4.7-9.7) h (P < 0.001) [cite: 51].
    * Incidence of Postoperative Urinary Retention (POUR): TEAS = 75 (8.1%); Sham = 104 (11.4%) (P = 0.02) [cite: 51].
    * Voiding difficulty: TEAS = 261 (28.3%); Sham = 413 (45.1%) (P < 0.001) [cite: 51].
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** Incidence of vomiting: TEAS = 231 (25.1%); Sham = 246 (26.9%) (P = 0.38) [cite: 51].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** 
    * Anxiety and depression (HADS score, Median [IQR]): TEAS = 0 [0-1]; Sham = 0 [0-2] (P < 0.001) [cite: 51].
    * Early ambulation (Mobilization within 24 h): TEAS = 573 (62.1%); Sham = 517 (56.4%) (P = 0.013) [cite: 51].
* **Length of stay:** Hospitalization duration (days, Median [IQR]): TEAS = 7 (6-10); Sham = 8 (6-10) (P = 0.16) [cite: 51].
* **Hospitalization costs:** TEAS = 22,714.9 yuan; Sham = 23,013.5 yuan (P = 0.21) [cite: 51].
* **Other postoperative complications:** No serious adverse events reported. Treatment-related adverse events were rare (Redness: 2 in TEAS, 1 in Sham; Itching: 0 in TEAS, 1 in Sham) [cite: 51].
