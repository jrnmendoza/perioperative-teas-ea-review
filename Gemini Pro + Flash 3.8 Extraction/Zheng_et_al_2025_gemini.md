# Data Extraction: 109499.pdf (Zheng et al., 2025)

## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Supported by the Liaoning Provincial Joint Science and Technology Program (2024JH2/102600283) [cite: 43].
* **Country:** China (Shenyang, Changchun) [cite: 43].
* **Journal:** Journal of Pain Research [cite: 43].
* **DOI:** Not explicitly stated with standard prefix in the text, but the URL https://doi.org/10.3147/JPR.S543374 is present in the document footer [cite: 43].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** Chinese Clinical Trial Registry (Registration No. ChiCTR2200058283) [cite: 43]. Ethical approval was obtained from the Ethics Committee of the Northern Theater General Hospital of the People's Liberation Army of China (Approval No. Y(2021)138) [cite: 43].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Prospective, randomized, single-blind, controlled trial [cite: 43].
* **Randomization method:** A computerized random number generator via SPSS (using a fixed seed number) was used to generate random numbers in a 1:1 ratio [cite: 43]. Group assignments were concealed in sequentially numbered, opaque, sealed envelopes [cite: 43].
* **Blinding:** Single-blind [cite: 43]. All participants and outcome assessors remained blinded to group assignments [cite: 43]. The anesthesiologists performing the intervention were unblinded [cite: 43].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** TEAS group and Control group [cite: 43].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 112 assessed for eligibility; 88 randomized; 85 completed and analyzed via modified intention-to-treat (mITT) (TEAS group: n=42; Control group: n=43) [cite: 43].
* **Age:** TEAS group: 42.1 (16.3) years; Control group: 44.9 (12.6) years (mean (SD)) [cite: 43].
* **Sex distribution:** 100% Female (undergoing gynecological laparoscopic surgery) [cite: 43].
* **BMI:** TEAS group: 24.7 (2.2) kg/m²; Control group: 25.1 (2.3) kg/m² [cite: 43].
* **ASA classification:** ASA physical status classification I-II [cite: 43]. TEAS group (I/II): 30/12; Control group (I/II): 29/14 [cite: 43].
* **Comorbidities:** Included patients aged 18-65 years with BMI 18-30 kg/m² undergoing gynecological laparoscopic surgery [cite: 43]. Excluded patients with rash/infection at acupoints, long-term use of sedatives/analgesics, substance abuse, diabetes mellitus, impaired hepatic/renal function, cognitive impairment, severe cardiovascular disease, history of chronic pain, prior acupoint therapy, or pacemaker [cite: 43].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Gynecological laparoscopic surgery [cite: 43].
* **Specific procedures:** Ovarian cystectomy (TEAS: 20, Control: 19); Myomectomy surgery (TEAS: 16, Control: 16); Hysterectomy surgery (TEAS: 6, Control: 8) [cite: 43].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 43].
* **Induction:** Sufentanil 0.15 μg/kg, rocuronium 0.6 mg/kg, and propofol 2.0 mg/kg [cite: 43].
* **Maintenance:** Propofol infusion at 4-8 mg/kg/h, remifentanil at 0.6-1.0 μg/kg/h, and sevoflurane inhalation (1%-2%, MAC set at 0.3) [cite: 43]. Adjusted to ensure BIS of 40-60 [cite: 43].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Transcutaneous electrical acupoint stimulation (TEAS) [cite: 43].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 42 analyzed [cite: 43].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Bilateral Neiguan, Zusanli, and Hegu [cite: 43].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** 2/100 Hz for sparse and dense wave stimulation [cite: 43].
* **Treatment intensity:** Gradually increased from 6 mA to the maximum tolerable level (not exceeding 15 mA) to induce slight muscle twitching ("De Qi" sensation) [cite: 43].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** Commenced 30 minutes before anesthesia induction [cite: 43].
* **Duration of each treatment:** Maintained from 30 min prior to induction until the end of anesthesia [cite: 43].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** One single continuous session [cite: 43].
* **Total treatment period:** Single day (perioperative) [cite: 43].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Bilateral TAP blocks performed under ultrasound guidance using 15 mL of 0.375% ropivacaine per side [cite: 43]. Prophylactic intravenous ondansetron 8 mg and flurbiprofen ester 50 mg at the beginning of suturing [cite: 43]. Postoperative PCIA (sufentanil) [cite: 43].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Sham TEAS [cite: 43].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 43 analyzed [cite: 43].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Identical electrodes applied, but no electrical stimulation was delivered (sham procedure) [cite: 43].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Patient-controlled intravenous analgesia (PCIA) (sufentanil 2 μg/kg + 0.9% sodium chloride) [cite: 43].
* **Dosage parameters:** Flow rate was 2 mL/h, with a single additional dose of 0.5 mL and a lock time of 15 minutes [cite: 43].
* **Duration:** Postoperative period [cite: 43].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Numerical Rating Scale (NRS) (0-10) [cite: 43].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Not specifically distinguished [cite: 43].
* **Pain assessment time points:** 6 h, 24 h, and 48 h postoperatively [cite: 43].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group (Mean ± SD):**
    * 6 h: TEAS = 2.9 ± 1.1; Control = 3.4 ± 1.2 (P = 0.022) [cite: 43].
    * 24 h: TEAS = 1.7 ± 0.8; Control = 2.2 ± 1.1 (P = 0.422; note text says P=0.042) [cite: 43].
    * 48 h: TEAS = 0.9 ± 0.8; Control = 1.0 ± 0.9 [cite: 43].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** The TEAS group had lower NRS pain scores at 6 and 24 hours postoperatively [cite: 43].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Sufentanil (via PCIA) [cite: 43]. Remifentanil used intraoperatively [cite: 43].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous [cite: 43].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** Intraoperative Remifentanil = 233.1 (29.6) μg [cite: 43]. Propofol = 495.1 (151.5) mg [cite: 43].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** Intraoperative Remifentanil = 289.5 (37.9) μg [cite: 43]. Propofol = 511.8 (162.1) mg [cite: 43].
* **Measurement time point(s):** Intraoperative [cite: 43].
* **Between-group difference:** Remifentanil consumption during surgery was significantly reduced in the TEAS group (P = 0.031) [cite: 43]. Propofol and sevoflurane usage showed no significant differences [cite: 43].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Times of total PCIA use: TEAS = 6.3 (2.2); Control = 10.7 (3.9) (P = 0.004) [cite: 43]. Times of effective PCIA use: TEAS = 3.8 (1.0); Control = 4.8 (1.6) (P = 0.039) [cite: 43].
* **Between-group difference:** The TEAS group had significantly fewer total and effective PCIA demands [cite: 43]. No significant differences in emergency rescue analgesia events were observed [cite: 43].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting (Primary Outcome):** 
    * PONV incidence within 24 hours: TEAS group = 42.9%; Control group = 67.4% (P = 0.023) [cite: 43].
    * Incidence of Nausea (0-2h): TEAS = 10 (23.8%); Control = 21 (48.8%) (P = 0.013) [cite: 43]. No significant differences for nausea at other time points or for vomiting at any time point [cite: 43].
* **Gastrointestinal recovery:** Time to first borborygmus (h): TEAS = 19.2 (3.3); Control = 27.6 (2.7) (P = 0.035) [cite: 43]. Time to first flatus (h): TEAS = 24.2 (2.5); Control = 31.6 (4.4) (P = 0.003) [cite: 43].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** 
    * QoR-40 total scores at 48 h: TEAS = 184.0; Control = 181.0 (P < 0.001) [cite: 43]. QoR-40 scores at 24 h were also significantly higher in the TEAS group (P < 0.0001) [cite: 43].
    * PSQI scores at 24 h: TEAS = 11.5 ± 6.9; Control = 15.7 ± 8.1 (P = 0.015) [cite: 43].
    * PSQI scores at 48 h: TEAS = 7.4 ± 5.2; Control = 10.6 ± 5.4 (P = 0.024) [cite: 43].
* **Length of stay:** Hospital stay duration (days): TEAS = 4 (3-5); Control = 4 (3-5) (P = 0.722) [cite: 43].
* **Other relevant physiological/biochemical outcomes (Biomarkers):** Blood glucose levels 30 minutes after skin incision were lower in the TEAS group (5.5 ± 0.5 mmol/L) compared to the control group (5.9 ± 0.4 mmol/L) (P = 0.045) [cite: 43]. MAP decreased significantly more in the control group at multiple time points [cite: 43].
