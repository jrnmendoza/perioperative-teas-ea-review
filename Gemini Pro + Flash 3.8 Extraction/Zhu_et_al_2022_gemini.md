# Data Extraction: covidence_381_full_article.pdf (Zhu et al., 2022)

## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Supported by the funding of Jiangsu Province hospital of TCM (grant no. H2018107, Y16053), China, and an NHMRC TRIP fellowship (APP1110446), Australia [cite: 41].
* **Country:** China / Australia [cite: 41].
* **Journal:** Acupuncture in Medicine [cite: 41].
* **DOI:** 10.1177/09645284221076517 [cite: 41].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** Chinese Clinical Trial Registry (ChiCTR-INR-16010035) [cite: 41]. Approved by the Human Research Ethics Committee of the Affiliated Hospital of Nanjing University of Traditional Chinese Medicine (2016NL-019-02) [cite: 41].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Single-center, four-arm randomized, controlled study [cite: 41].
* **Randomization method:** Computer-generated randomization sequence and secure code, with allocations printed and stored in sequentially numbered, opaque, sealed envelopes [cite: 41].
* **Blinding:** Single/assessor-blind [cite: 41]. The anesthetists, surgeons, and research nurse performing outcome assessments were blinded to group allocation [cite: 41]. The acupuncturist was unblinded, and patients were instructed not to disclose their treatment [cite: 41].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** Group Pre (EA the day before surgery), Group 30 (EA 30 min before surgery), Group Comb (EA both the day before and 30 min before surgery), and Group Usual (usual care alone) [cite: 41].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 420 screened; 413 randomized; 400 completed and analyzed (Group Pre: n=101; Group 30: n=98; Group Comb: n=100; Group Usual: n=101) [cite: 41].
* **Age:** Group Pre: 35.6 ± 8.1 years; Group 30: 34.7 ± 7.6 years; Group Comb: 35.3 ± 8.2 years; Group Usual: 35.7 ± 8.0 years (mean ± SD) [cite: 41].
* **Sex distribution:** 100% Female (undergoing gynecological laparoscopic surgery) [cite: 41].
* **BMI:** Group Pre: 22.9 ± 3.13 kg/m²; Group 30: 22.7 ± 3.32 kg/m²; Group Comb: 22.7 ± 2.96 kg/m²; Group Usual: 22.97 ± 3.43 kg/m² [cite: 41].
* **ASA classification:** ASA class I or II [cite: 41].
* **Comorbidities:** Included females aged 18-60 years undergoing elective laparoscopic gynecological surgery [cite: 41]. Excluded patients with pregnancy/breastfeeding, psychiatric disorders, substance abuse, chronic pain syndromes, use of antiemetics/opioids/glucocorticoids within 3 days, nausea/vomiting within 24 h of surgery, cardiac pacemaker, or infection near needling sites [cite: 41].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Laparoscopic gynecological surgery [cite: 41].
* **Specific procedures:** Hysterectomy or myomectomy (Group Pre: 45, Group 30: 41, Group Comb: 46, Group Usual: 45); Salpingoopherectomy, oophorectomy, or ovarian cystectomy (Group Pre: 56, Group 30: 57, Group Comb: 54, Group Usual: 56) [cite: 41].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 41].
* **Induction:** Intravenous midazolam 0.05 mg/kg, propofol 2-3 mg/kg, sufentanil 0.2-0.4 μg/kg, vecuronium 0.1 mg/kg, and lidocaine 1 mg/kg [cite: 41].
* **Maintenance:** Total intravenous anesthesia with continuous propofol and remifentanil [cite: 41]. BIS ranged from 40 to 65 [cite: 41].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Electroacupuncture (EA) [cite: 41].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** Group Pre: 101; Group 30: 98; Group Comb: 100 (299 analyzed total in EA groups) [cite: 41].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Bilateral PC6 (Neiguan) and ST36 (Zusanli) [cite: 41].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** Dense-disperse mode (20-100 Hz) [cite: 41].
* **Treatment intensity:** Delivered at a level where participants reported a strong but not painful sensation [cite: 41].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** Group Pre: the day before surgery; Group 30: 30 min before surgery; Group Comb: both the day before and 30 min before surgery [cite: 41].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 30 minutes [cite: 41].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** 1 session for Group Pre and Group 30; 2 sessions for Group Comb [cite: 41].
* **Total treatment period:** Preoperative (day before to 30 min before surgery) [cite: 41].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Dexamethasone 5 mg and tropisetron 5 mg administered at induction and after skin closure, respectively [cite: 41]. Intravenous parecoxib 40 mg at the end of surgery [cite: 41].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Usual care (Group Usual) [cite: 41].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 101 analyzed [cite: 41].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Received no EA [cite: 41]. Received identical anesthesia, prophylactic antiemetics, and postoperative care [cite: 41].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Intravenous parecoxib 40 mg at the end of surgery [cite: 41]. Flurbiprofen 100 mg administered when NRS > 3 [cite: 41].
* **Dosage parameters:** Flurbiprofen 100 mg as rescue [cite: 41].
* **Duration:** Postoperative period [cite: 41].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Numerical rating scale (NRS) (0-10) [cite: 41].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Pain on movement [cite: 41].
* **Pain assessment time points:** 0-6 h, 6-24 h, 24-48 h, and 48-72 h after surgery [cite: 41].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group (Mean ± SD):**
    * 0-6 h: Pre = 1.89 ± 1.47; 30 = 2.01 ± 1.25; Comb = 1.97 ± 1.25; Usual = 2.45 ± 1.6 (P = 0.024) [cite: 41].
    * 6-24 h: Pre = 1.15 ± 1.02; 30 = 1.17 ± 1.17; Comb = 1.12 ± 1.03; Usual = 1.44 ± 1.30 (P = 0.179) [cite: 41].
    * 24-48 h: Pre = 0.4 ± 0.76; 30 = 0.5 ± 0.96; Comb = 0.54 ± 0.86; Usual = 0.73 ± 0.97 (P = 0.058) [cite: 41].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** The mean NRS pain score at 0-6 h was significantly different between groups (P = 0.024), with scores in Group Pre significantly lower than Group Usual [cite: 41]. No significant differences between the three EA-treated groups [cite: 41].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Sufentanil and Remifentanil [cite: 41].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous [cite: 41].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention groups:**
    * Group Pre: Sufentanil = 38.1 ± 4.97 μg; Remifentanil = 0.58 ± 0.23 mg [cite: 41].
    * Group 30: Sufentanil = 39.1 ± 4.83 μg; Remifentanil = 0.58 ± 0.23 mg [cite: 41].
    * Group Comb: Sufentanil = 39.0 ± 5.12 μg; Remifentanil = 0.50 ± 0.25 mg [cite: 41].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** Group Usual: Sufentanil = 37.8 ± 4.87 μg; Remifentanil = 0.57 ± 0.26 mg [cite: 41].
* **Measurement time point(s):** Intraoperative [cite: 41].
* **Between-group difference:** There were no significant differences between the groups in intraoperative sufentanil (P = 0.892) or remifentanil (P = 0.948) consumption [cite: 41].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Analgesics 0-48 h after surgery (n): Group Pre = 2; Group 30 = 1; Group Comb = 3; Group Usual = 3 [cite: 41].
* **Between-group difference:** No significant differences in incidence of postoperative analgesic use [cite: 41].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting (Primary Outcome):** 
    * Incidence of Nausea at 6-24 h: Pre = 11 (11%); 30 = 18 (18%); Comb = 11 (11%); Usual = 27 (28%) (P = 0.003) [cite: 41].
    * Incidence of Vomiting at 6-24 h: Pre = 5 (5%); 30 = 8 (8%); Comb = 9 (9%); Usual = 22 (23%) (P < 0.001) [cite: 41].
    * Antiemetic requirement at 6-24 h (n): Pre = 8; 30 = 16; Comb = 9; Usual = 28 (P < 0.001) [cite: 41].
* **Gastrointestinal recovery:** Time to passage of first flatus (h): Pre = 18.38 ± 6.11; 30 = 18.82 ± 7.50; Comb = 18.47 ± 6.47; Usual = 21.56 ± 8.07 (P = 0.003) [cite: 41].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** Overall QoR-15 score (mean ± SD): Pre = 142.94 ± 6.82; 30 = 144.61 ± 5.35; Comb = 144.74 ± 5.21; Usual = 144.23 ± 4.95 (P = 0.095) [cite: 41].
* **Length of stay:** Not reported [cite: 41].
* **Other postoperative complications:** Three patients in Group Comb experienced pain and itching at the needling sites [cite: 41].
