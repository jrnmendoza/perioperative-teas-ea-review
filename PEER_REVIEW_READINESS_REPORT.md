## Overall

The review's methodological defences are, for the most part, already built
and visible rather than needing to be improvised under review pressure. The
one item a careful reviewer would find that is not yet resolved is the
stratum-purity issue above — and it is already found, quantified, and flagged
on the dashboard, which is a materially different position than if it had
gone unnoticed until after submission.
