# 15. 24-hour opioid-sparing effect

## 2-Hz EA vs sham

- Sham: **56.54 ± 12.50 mL**
- 2 Hz: **39.31 ± 15.58 mL**
- Published difference: **17.24 mL (95% CI 11.40-23.07), P<0.001**
- Calculated relative reduction: **30.5%**

## 20/100-Hz EA vs sham

- Sham: **56.54 ± 12.50 mL**
- 20/100 Hz: **45.72 ± 16.92 mL**
- Published difference: **10.83 mL (95% CI 5.00-16.66), P=0.001**
- Calculated relative reduction: **19.1%**

## 2 Hz vs 20/100 Hz - publication inconsistency

### Table 3
- 2 Hz minus 20/100 Hz: **-6.41 mL**
- 95% CI: **-12.21 to 0.61**
- **P=0.031**

### Results prose / Discussion
The text states:
- difference **6.41**
- 95% CI **0.61-12.21**
- **P=0.0031**
- and describes 2-Hz EA as significantly better at 24 h.

### Why this matters
The table and prose cannot both be correct:
- The Table 3 CI crosses zero and P=0.031.
- Table 3 explicitly says the Bonferroni-adjusted significance threshold is **P<0.0167**.
- Thus **P=0.031 would not be significant after the paper's stated correction**.
- The prose value **P=0.0031** would be significant and its CI excludes zero.

### Extraction decision
Preserve the directly tabulated group means as primary quantitative data:
- **39.31 ± 15.58 vs 45.72 ± 16.92 mL**
and flag the **pairwise inferential-statistics discrepancy** for author clarification.

# 27. Major methodological / audit issues

1. **Analysis-population inconsistency:** 174 randomized and called ITT, but Tables 1-4 use only **158 completers**.
2. Figure 1 explicitly says **58/group included in ITT**, while Table 1 titled ITT uses **52/53/53**.
3. The study therefore appears to report **complete-case outcomes despite ITT terminology**.
4. **PCIA fentanyl concentration and pump settings are missing from the main article**, although they may be in Supplement 1.
5. Fentanyl consumption is reported in **mL**, not in fentanyl mass.
6. Consequently, exact **24-h MME cannot yet be calculated**.
7. There is a major **24-h 2-Hz vs 20/100-Hz inferential-statistics inconsistency**:
   - Table: difference -6.41, CI -12.21 to 0.61, P=0.031.
   - Prose: difference 6.41, CI 0.61-12.21, P=0.0031.
8. Under the table's Bonferroni threshold **P<0.0167**, Table P=0.031 is **not significant**.
9. The authors nevertheless conclude that 2-Hz EA produced significantly lower 24-h fentanyl consumption than 20/100-Hz EA.
10. Exact cesarean anesthetic technique is **not reported in the main article**.
11. Detailed inclusion/exclusion criteria are not in the main text and require Supplement 1.
12. Rescue anti-inflammatory analgesic use was said to be recorded, but exact rescue data are not in the main article.
13. Follow-up ended at 48 h, limiting assessment of chronic pain and longer-term maternal outcomes.
14. Single-center design limits generalizability.
15. PONV and most opioid adverse-effect outcomes were not reported quantitatively despite fentanyl use.

# 29. Concise Covidence-ready summary

**Jin et al. 2023 (Study #9)** was a three-arm randomized, single-blinded, sham-controlled trial in China evaluating **postoperative electroacupuncture combined with fentanyl PCIA after cesarean delivery**. A total of **174 primigravida women were randomized (58/group)**, although the published outcome tables contain only **158 completers: 52 sham, 53 2-Hz EA, and 53 20/100-Hz EA**.

Active EA was applied bilaterally at **ST36 and SP6** for **25 min at 6, 12, 24, and 48 h after surgery**. The 2-Hz group received continuous **2-Hz stimulation**; the second active group received **20/100 Hz alternating every 3 s**. Intensity was **0.1-5.0 mA** with de qi elicited. Sham participants received nonpenetrating placebo needles at nonmeridian points **20 mm away** from ST36/SP6, with no electrical output.

Both active EA regimens substantially reduced postoperative pain, PCA button presses, and the reported fentanyl/PCIA solution consumption. At **24 h**, consumption was **56.54 ± 12.50 mL in sham, 39.31 ± 15.58 mL with 2-Hz EA, and 45.72 ± 16.92 mL with 20/100-Hz EA**. This corresponds to calculated relative reductions of **30.5%** and **19.1%**, respectively, versus sham. At **48 h**, values were **94.88 ± 9.91, 82.41 ± 21.18, and 83.76 ± 17.91 mL**.
