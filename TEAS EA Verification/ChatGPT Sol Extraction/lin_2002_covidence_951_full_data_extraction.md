# 23. Major methodological / audit issues

1. **No trial registration** reported.
2. Allocation concealment beyond the computer-generated sequence is **not described**.
3. Untreated control could not be treatment-blinded.
4. The study did not explicitly designate one primary outcome.
5. No exact numeric serial VAS values are tabulated; pain data are shown graphically.
6. The first postoperative hour allowed **IM pethidine**, but group-specific pethidine use is not reported.
7. Therefore the reported 24-h morphine values are exact **PCA morphine**, but may not equal total all-opioid exposure over the entire first 24 h.
8. No acupuncture-specific adverse-event table.
9. Sample size is modest at **25/group**, though complete follow-up is a strength.
10. The paper reports both **0.5 mA constant current** and “maximum tolerable intensity,” creating some ambiguity in exact delivered current.
11. The sham group itself showed substantial morphine reduction vs untreated control, consistent with a placebo/needling effect.
12. The high-frequency group had the strongest opioid-sparing effect, but mechanistic frequency claims should remain separate from the clinical outcome extraction.

# 25. Concise Covidence-ready summary

**Lin et al. 2002 (Study #951)** randomized **100 women undergoing abdominal hysterectomy** to untreated control, sham EA, **2-Hz EA**, or **100-Hz EA** (**25/group**). All participants completed the study.

EA was applied **preoperatively only**, for **20 min immediately before general anesthesia**, at **bilateral ST36 (Zusanli)**. Active stimulation used a **1-ms square pulse**, with the paper reporting **0.5 mA constant current** at the maximal tolerable intensity. Sham participants received needle insertion at the same point with the device indicator light on but **no current**.

No intraoperative opioids were used. During the first postoperative hour, patients could receive **pethidine 1 mg/kg IM once**. At 1 h, all patients started **IV morphine PCA**, programmed for **2-mg boluses with a 10-min lockout**, continuing for the next 23 h.

The exact 24-h PCA morphine totals were:

- **Control: 38.1 ± 16.0 mg**
- **Sham EA: 30.2 ± 14.4 mg**
- **Low EA 2 Hz: 21.8 ± 14.7 mg**
- **High EA 100 Hz: 15.0 ± 10.7 mg**

This corresponds to morphine reductions versus control of approximately **21% with sham EA**, **43% with low-frequency EA**, and **61% with high-frequency EA**. High-frequency EA also reduced morphine by about **31% relative to low-frequency EA**.

Pain scores were reported as **not significantly different among groups**, meaning the opioid reduction occurred without worse pain. Both active EA groups also had significantly lower **nausea and dizziness** than control and sham.

For your opioid-sparing review, this is a **high-value quantitative study** because it provides exact **24-h IV morphine means and SDs**. The only caveat is that the separate first-hour pethidine exposure is not quantified by group, so the published morphine values represent exact **PCA morphine**, not necessarily total all-opioid exposure over the full first 24 h.
