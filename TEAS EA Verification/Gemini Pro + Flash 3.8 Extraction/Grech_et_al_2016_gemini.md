## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** The Faculty Program of the Department of Surgery of the New Jersey Medical School, the New Jersey Health Foundation, and the US National Institute of Health (R01GM114180) [cite: 17].
* **Country:** United States [cite: 17].
* **Journal:** Journal of Acupuncture and Meridian Studies (J Acupunct Meridian Stud) [cite: 17].
* **DOI:** 10.1016/j.jams.2016.03.009 [cite: 17].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** clinicaltrials.gov (NCT01937520) [cite: 17].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Prospective, double-blinded, randomized pilot study [cite: 17].
* **Randomization method:** Randomized into two groups, though the specific generation method is not detailed [cite: 17].
* **Blinding:** Double-blinded; all patients were under general anesthesia during the EA and blood collection, making them blinded to the treatment to avoid any placebo effect [cite: 17].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** EA group and sham (control) group [cite: 17].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 20 patients randomized (EA: n=11; Sham: n=9); 1 patient was excluded, resulting in 10 patients analyzed in the EA group and 9 in the control group [cite: 17].
* **Age:** All patients: 48.15 ± 2.45 years. Control group: 52.33 ± 4.08 years; EA group: 44.73 ± 2.68 years [cite: 17].
* **Sex distribution:** Control group: 9 Females; EA group: 9 Females, 2 Males (one male was likely the excluded patient) [cite: 17].
* **BMI:** Weight was reported as: All patients: 81.16 ± 4.27 kg; Control group: 85.00 ± 6.22 kg; EA group: 77.70 ± 5.97 kg. BMI was not reported [cite: 17].
* **ASA classification:** Not reported [cite: 17].
* **Comorbidities:** Patients were excluded if they had pre-existing diabetes, cardiovascular conditions, or elevated levels of blood glucose, insulin, or TNF prior to surgery [cite: 17].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Thyroid and parathyroid surgery [cite: 17].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 17].
* **Induction:** Midazolam 1-2.5 mg, propofol 1.0-2.0 mg/kg, fentanyl 1-3 μg/kg, and lidocaine 1 mg/kg [cite: 17].
* **Maintenance:** Sevoflurane at a minimal alveolar concentration of 1.0-3.0 mixed with 50% oxygen and 50% air [cite: 17].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Intraoperative low-frequency electroacupuncture (EA) [cite: 17].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 10 analyzed [cite: 17].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Bilateral He Gu (LI-4), Qu Chi (LI-11), and Zu San Li (ST-36) acupoints [cite: 17].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** 10-Hz frequency with a continuous electrical current of wave, delivering a symmetrical biphasic wave alternately [cite: 17].
* **Treatment intensity:** Induced mild muscle twitching [cite: 17].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** During the maintenance phase of general anesthesia (starting ~15 minutes after induction) [cite: 17].
* **Duration of each treatment:** Two 30-minute treatments, separated by 30 minutes [cite: 17].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** Two intraoperative sessions [cite: 17].
* **Total treatment period:** Single day (intraoperative) [cite: 17].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Postoperative analgesia (converted to morphine equivalents) [cite: 17].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Sham [cite: 17].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 9 [cite: 17].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Control patients were maintained under general anesthesia without EA, but further specific details of the sham intervention (e.g., whether needles were placed without current) are not provided in the text [cite: 17].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Postoperative analgesic treatments were converted to morphine equivalents [cite: 17]. The exact analgesic drugs used were not specified [cite: 17].
* **Dosage parameters:** Not detailed beyond conversion to morphine equivalents [cite: 17].
* **Duration:** Measured at the Postanesthesia Care Unit (PACU) and during the 3 days after surgery [cite: 17].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Visual analog scale (VAS), score 0-10 [cite: 17].
* **Pain assessment time points:** At the PACU and the 3 days after surgery [cite: 17].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group:** Visualized in Figure 1. Pain scores were stated to be similar between control and EA groups [cite: 17].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** Both groups had similar pain scores at the PACU and after hospital discharge [cite: 17].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Reported as morphine equivalents (mg) [cite: 17].
* **Route of administration:** Not specified [cite: 17].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** Not numerically tabulated, but depicted in Figure 1. EA-treated women required significantly less [cite: 17].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** Not numerically tabulated, but depicted in Figure 1 [cite: 17].
* **Measurement time point(s):** At the PACU and 1, 2, and 3 days post-surgery [cite: 17].
* **Between-group difference:** EA-treated women required significantly less analgesics than control women at the PACU (p < 0.1) [cite: 17]. EA also significantly reduced the request for analgesics in younger (< 45 years) patients at the PACU [cite: 17].
* **Percentage/relative reduction:** EA-treated women required 60% less analgesics than control women at the PACU [cite: 17].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Not specifically reported; all analgesic use was analyzed via morphine equivalents [cite: 17].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** Not reported [cite: 17].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** The Quality of Recovery (QoR) score (maximum 18) was assessed. Patients with or without EA had similar quality of recovery during the 3 days after surgery [cite: 17].
* **Length of stay:** Not explicitly reported, though outcomes were assessed up to 3 days post-surgery [cite: 17].
* **Other postoperative complications:** None explicitly reported [cite: 17].
* **Other relevant physiological/biochemical outcomes (Biomarkers):** 
    * EA prevented postoperative hyperglycemia without affecting serum insulin levels [cite: 17].
    * EA reduced mean serum ACTH levels at the PACU by over 70% without affecting cortisol [cite: 17].
    * EA did not affect the induction of IL-6 and IL-10 or the levels of TNF, IL-2, and IL-4 [cite: 17]. 
    * EA increased serum TGFβ1 levels post-surgery by three-fold compared to controls [cite: 17].
