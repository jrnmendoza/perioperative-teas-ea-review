## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China (No. Grant number 81971225) and the Clinical Research Project of the Air Force Medical University (No. 2021LC2207) [cite: 19].
* **Country:** China (Xi'an) [cite: 19].
* **Journal:** Heliyon [cite: 19].
* **DOI:** 10.1016/j.heliyon.2023.e19386 [cite: 19].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** Chinese Clinical Trial Registry (ChiCTR2000035552) and the Chinese Medicine Clinical Trial Registry (ChiMCTR2000003560) [cite: 19].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Prospective, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study [cite: 19].
* **Randomization method:** Randomization sequence was generated by SPSS 25.0 software, and the allocation code was performed by an independent technician [cite: 19].
* **Blinding:** Double-blind; the patients, anesthesiologists, surgeons, and observers were blinded to group assignment [cite: 19].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** TEAS group and sham-TEAS group [cite: 19].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 128 randomized; 110 completed the 12-month follow-up and were analyzed (TEAS group: n=55; Sham-TEAS group: n=55) [cite: 19].
* **Age:** TEAS group: 70.09 ± 3.95 years; Sham-TEAS group: 70.56 ± 4.59 years [cite: 19].
* **Sex distribution:** TEAS group: 35 Male / 20 Female; Sham-TEAS group: 28 Male / 27 Female [cite: 19].
* **BMI:** TEAS group: 24.09 ± 2.78 kg/m²; Sham-TEAS group: 23.63 ± 2.85 kg/m² [cite: 19].
* **ASA classification:** ASA status I to III [cite: 19].
* **Comorbidities:** Patients with severe comorbidities in major organ systems, including pre-existing coagulopathy, hypertension, hepatic or renal insufficiency, cardiovascular disease, diabetes, and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, were excluded [cite: 19].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Thoracoscopic pulmonary resection for suspected lung cancer (lasting more than 2 hours) [cite: 19].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia with pulmonary isolation using a double-lumen tube (DLT) [cite: 19].
* **Induction:** Midazolam (0.015-0.03 mg/kg), sufentanil (0.4-0.8 μg/kg), propofol (1.5-2.5 mg/kg), and rocuronium bromide (0.6 mg/kg) [cite: 19].
* **Maintenance:** Target-controlled infusion (TCI) system of propofol, rocuronium, and remifentanil (adjusting the BIS to 40-60) [cite: 19].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Transcutaneous electrical acupoint stimulation (TEAS) [cite: 19].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 55 analyzed [cite: 19].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Baihui (DU20) and bilateral Neiguan (PC6), Hegu (LI4), and Zusanli (ST36) [cite: 19].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** Disperse-dense waves; 2/100 Hz [cite: 19].
* **Treatment intensity:** 10-30 mA [cite: 19].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** From 30 min before anesthesia induction until the end of the surgery [cite: 19].
* **Duration of each treatment:** Maintained continuously throughout the operation (mean duration of anesthesia was 159.36 ± 39.68 min) [cite: 19].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** One continuous intraoperative session [cite: 19].
* **Total treatment period:** Single day (intraoperative) [cite: 19].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Patient-controlled intravenous analgesia (PCIA) postoperatively [cite: 19].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Sham TEAS [cite: 19].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 55 analyzed [cite: 19].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Stimulators were connected to the same acupoints, but the power was turned off [cite: 19].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** PCIA pump with sufentanil [cite: 19].
* **Dosage parameters:** Sufentanil 0.04 μg/kg/h, bolus 0.01 μg/kg, background dose 2 ml/h, lockout interval 15 min [cite: 19].
* **Duration:** 48 hours postoperatively [cite: 19].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Numerical Rating Scale (NRS) on pain [cite: 19].
* **Pain assessment time points:** Postoperative day 1, day 7, and day 30 [cite: 19].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group:** Visualized in Figure 3B; actual numbers not tabulated, but shown graphically [cite: 19]. 
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** On day 1, day 7, and day 30 after surgery, the pain scores in the TEAS group were significantly lower than those in the sham-TEAS group (P < 0.001) [cite: 19]. 

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Sufentanil (postoperatively via PCIA) and Remifentanil (intraoperatively) [cite: 19].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous via PCIA and TCI [cite: 19].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** Intraoperative remifentanil = 0.87 ± 0.30 mg; Intraoperative sufentanil = 29.42 ± 7.62 μg [cite: 19]. Postoperative PCIA consumption was not explicitly quantified [cite: 19].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** Intraoperative remifentanil = 1.01 ± 0.39 mg; Intraoperative sufentanil = 30.55 ± 7.94 μg [cite: 19].
* **Measurement time point(s):** Intraoperative consumption measured [cite: 19].
* **Between-group difference:** The consumption of remifentanil in the sham-TEAS group was significantly higher than in the TEAS group (P = 0.040) [cite: 19]. There was no significant difference for intraoperative sufentanil (P = 0.433) [cite: 19]. Frequency of remedial analgesic in the hospital showed no significant difference (P = 0.361) [cite: 19].
* **Percentage/relative reduction:** Not explicitly calculated [cite: 19].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Addressed via frequency of remedial analgesic in the hospital (times): TEAS = 2.29 ± 1.76; Sham = 1.96 ± 1.97 (P = 0.361) [cite: 19].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** Dexamethasone (4-5 mg) was given preventatively [cite: 19]. Adverse events of nausea and vomiting were reported in 2 (3.63%) TEAS patients and 5 (9.09%) sham-TEAS patients [cite: 19].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes (Primary Outcome - Cognition):** 
    * The incidence of POCD in the sham-TEAS group was significantly higher than in the TEAS group on Day 1 (65.4% vs 20%), Day 7 (43.6% vs 7.3%), and Day 30 (40% vs 3.6%) (all P < 0.001) [cite: 19]. 
    * MMSE scores were significantly higher in the TEAS group on Days 1, 7, and 30 (P < 0.001) [cite: 19]. 
    * NRS sleep scores were significantly higher (better sleep) in the TEAS group on Days 1, 7, and 30 (P < 0.001) [cite: 19]. 
    * EORTC-QLQ-C30 scores at 6 and 12 months showed the TEAS group had significantly higher global health scores (P = 0.007 and P = 0.029, respectively) and emotional function scores [cite: 19].
* **Length of stay:** Not reported [cite: 19].
* **Other postoperative complications:** Chronic pain at 12 months was significantly lower in the TEAS group (current pain: 7.3% vs 23.6%, P = 0.018; pain duration >2 months: 14.5% vs 32.7%, P = 0.025) [cite: 19].
