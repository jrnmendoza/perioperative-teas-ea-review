## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** National Basic Research Program of Ministry of Science and Technology of China (2013CB531900) and Peking University People's Hospital R&D fund (RDC2013-09) [cite: 33].
* **Country:** China (Beijing) [cite: 33].
* **Journal:** Journal of Anesthesia (J Anesth) [cite: 33].
* **DOI:** 10.1007/s00540-015-2057-1 [cite: 33].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** www.chictr.org (ChiCTR-TRC-14004207) [cite: 33].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blinded study with a pre- and post-test design [cite: 33].
* **Randomization method:** Depending on enrollment sequence according to a series of random numbers generated by Excel [cite: 33].
* **Blinding:** Double-blinded; participants were unaware of group allocation, a single investigator applied interventions, and another investigator unaware of group allocation collected the data [cite: 33].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** Control (con), 2/100 Hz, 2 Hz, and 100 Hz groups [cite: 33].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 80 patients met inclusion criteria and were randomized (20 per group) [cite: 33]. All 80 completed the trial and were analyzed [cite: 33].
* **Age:** con: 57.2 ± 15.7 years; 2/100 Hz: 57.3 ± 11.6 years; 2 Hz: 58.2 ± 10.6 years; 100 Hz: 56.5 ± 11.2 years [cite: 33].
* **Sex distribution:** con: 9 Male / 11 Female; 2/100 Hz: 10 Male / 10 Female; 2 Hz: 9 Male / 11 Female; 100 Hz: 9 Male / 11 Female [cite: 33].
* **BMI:** con: 22.8 ± 2.6 kg/m²; 2/100 Hz: 24.7 ± 2.2 kg/m²; 2 Hz: 25.0 ± 3.5 kg/m²; 100 Hz: 23.6 ± 2.2 kg/m² [cite: 33].
* **ASA classification:** ASA I/II/III distribution was con: 7/12/1; 2/100 Hz: 6/14/0; 2 Hz: 8/12/0; 100 Hz: 10/7/3 [cite: 33]. Patients were excluded if they had severe co-existing disease with ASA status IV [cite: 33].
* **Comorbidities:** Not explicitly tabulated beyond ASA. Patients were excluded if they had recent use of TEAS/acupuncture, or neural damage or infection along the meridian [cite: 33].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Selective Video-assisted thoracic surgical (VATS) lobectomy (single/two lobes) [cite: 33].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 33].
* **Induction:** 1.5-2 mg/kg propofol, 0.2-0.4 μg/kg sufentanil, and 0.2 mg/kg cisatracurium [cite: 33].
* **Maintenance:** Continuous infusion of cisatracurium (0.06 mg kg-1 h-1) and target controlled infusion (TCI) of propofol and remifentanil [cite: 33]. Bi-spectrum index was maintained between 40-60, and analgesia nociception index (ANI) was maintained between 50-70 [cite: 33].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Transcutaneous electrical acupoint stimulation (TEAS) [cite: 33].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 60 total (20 in 2/100 Hz group; 20 in 2 Hz group; 20 in 100 Hz group) [cite: 33].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Neiguan (PC6), Hegu (LI4), Lieque (LU7), and Quchi (LI11) on the surgery-lateral side [cite: 33].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** Frequencies were set as 2/100 Hz (alternating 2 and 100 Hz every 3 s), 2 Hz, or 100 Hz [cite: 33].
* **Treatment intensity:** Highest tolerable level that caused no discomfort [cite: 33].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** 30 min before induction, during the entire intraoperative period, and 30 min at 24 and 48 h after surgery [cite: 33].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 30 min preoperatively, continuously during the operation, 30 min at 24h, and 30 min at 48h [cite: 33].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** 4 sessions (pre-op, intra-op, 24h post-op, 48h post-op) [cite: 33].
* **Total treatment period:** Up to 48 hours post-surgery [cite: 33].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Patient-controlled intravenous analgesia (PCIA) pump connected 30 min before the end of surgery [cite: 33]. The surgeon administered an intercostal nerve block with 0.5% ropivacaine (10 ml) upon skin closure [cite: 33].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Placebo-TEAS (control) [cite: 33].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 20 [cite: 33].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Electrodes with no central pore were placed on the skin [cite: 33]. Participants could see output light flashing but no current was transmitted [cite: 33]. Patients were told this was a type of stimulus without perception [cite: 33].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** PCIA pump containing 1 μg/mL sufentanil and 1.2 mg/mL flurbiprofen [cite: 33].
* **Dosage parameters:** Flow rate 2 mL/h; bolus 3 mL; lockout time 15 min [cite: 33].
* **Duration:** Administered and evaluated over 48 hours postoperatively [cite: 33].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Numerical rating scale (NRS; 0 = no pain, 10 = worst pain) [cite: 33].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Not specifically distinguished [cite: 33].
* **Pain assessment time points:** Immediately after extubation (in PACU) [cite: 33]. Postoperatively at 6, 12, 24, and 48 h, dosages were adjusted to maintain NRS < 4 (explicit NRS scores were not directly compared at these later time points) [cite: 33].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group:**
    * NRS score right after extubation: con = 2.4 ± 0.5; 2/100 Hz = 1.3 ± 0.7; 2 Hz = 2.2 ± 1.9; 100 Hz = 2.4 ± 1.3 [cite: 33].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** The NRS score immediately after extubation was significantly lower in the 2/100 Hz group compared to the control group (P = 0.047) [cite: 33].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Remifentanil (intraoperative), Sufentanil (intraoperative and postoperative) [cite: 33].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous (TCI intraoperatively, PCIA postoperatively) [cite: 33].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** 
    * Intraoperative total opioid dose (remifentanil equivalent, μg kg-1 min-1): 2/100 Hz = 0.1128 ± 0.034; 2 Hz = 0.1718 ± 0.066; 100 Hz = 0.1451 ± 0.036 [cite: 33].
    * Postoperative Sufentanil (μg/kg): 2/100 Hz = 1.67 ± 0.59; 2 Hz = 1.41 ± 0.52; 100 Hz = 1.69 ± 0.50 [cite: 33].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** 
    * Intraoperative total opioid dose: con = 0.1617 ± 0.037 μg kg-1 min-1 [cite: 33].
    * Postoperative Sufentanil: con = 1.32 ± 0.48 μg/kg [cite: 33].
* **Between-group difference:** Intraoperative total opioid dosage was significantly lower in the 2/100 Hz group than in the control group (P ≤ 0.001) and other experimental groups [cite: 33]. Postoperative dosages of sufentanil and flurbiprofen required at 6, 12, 24, and 48 h did not differ significantly among groups [cite: 33].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Not explicitly reported; patients controlled their own analgesia via the PCIA pump to maintain NRS < 4 [cite: 33].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** PONV morbidity was 35% in control, 25% in 2/100 Hz, 26% in 2 Hz, and 5% in 100 Hz [cite: 33]. The incidence of PONV in the 100 Hz group was significantly lower than the control group (P = 0.044) [cite: 33]. No patient experienced PONV in the PACU [cite: 33]. PONV scores at 6, 12, 24, and 48 h did not differ significantly among groups [cite: 33]. Tropisetron (5 mg) was given 30 min before the end of surgery for prevention [cite: 33].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** 
    * Extubation time: con = 14.7 ± 4.0 min; 2/100 Hz = 9.9 ± 3.5 min (P = 0.028 vs con); 2 Hz = 12.4 ± 4.6 min; 100 Hz = 12.8 ± 6.2 min [cite: 33].
    * Duration of PACU stay: con = 29.3 ± 8.0 min; 2/100 Hz = 19.8 ± 7.0 min (P = 0.043 vs con); 2 Hz = 24.9 ± 9.2 min; 100 Hz = 25.6 ± 12.4 min [cite: 33].
    * Quality of life (SF-12): The mean postoperative physical component scores were significantly lower than the mean preoperative scores in all groups; mental component scores did not differ significantly [cite: 33].
    * Pulmonary function: PaO2 change rate (mmHg/min) decreased more slowly in the 2/100 Hz group (0.76 ± 0.95) compared to the control group (1.28 ± 0.78), P = 0.042 [cite: 33]. FEV1 and FVC change rates did not differ significantly among groups [cite: 33].
* **Length of stay:** Not reported [cite: 33].
* **Other postoperative complications:** None of the patients reported adverse effects such as intraoperative awareness or dizziness [cite: 33].
