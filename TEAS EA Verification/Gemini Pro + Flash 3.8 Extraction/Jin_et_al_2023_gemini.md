## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Funded by Natural Science Foundation of Zhejiang Province (grant number LQ21H270008 and LY19H040014), Youth Innovative Talents Support Program of Zhejiang Medical and Health Science and Technology Program (grant number 2022492789), and Key Laboratory of Pulsed Power Translational Medicine of Zhejiang Province (grant number ZMY-KF-210002) [cite: 39].
* **Country:** China [cite: 39].
* **Journal:** American Journal of Obstetrics & Gynecology MFM (AJOG MFM) [cite: 39].
* **DOI:** 10.1016/j.ajogmf.2022.100826 [cite: 39].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** ClinicalTrials.gov (NCT04879212) [cite: 39].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Single-center, randomized, single-blinded, sham acupuncture controlled clinical trial [cite: 39].
* **Randomization method:** The randomization sequence was generated at the hospital, and participants were randomized using the sealed envelope method in a 1:1:1 ratio [cite: 39].
* **Blinding:** Single-blinded; participants, outcome assessors, data analysts, and statisticians were blinded to treatment allocation, while acupuncturists were not [cite: 39]. The sham device had power indicator lights covered and the battery compartment sealed to assist in blinding [cite: 39].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** PCIA + 2-Hz EA group, PCIA + 20/100-Hz EA group, and PCIA + sham EA group [cite: 39].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 174 eligible patients were enrolled and randomized; 158 finished the allocated intervention and were included in the intention-to-treat (ITT) analysis (sham EA: n=52; 2-Hz EA: n=53; 20/100-Hz EA: n=53) [cite: 39].
* **Age:** sham EA: 31.19 ± 3.37 years; 2-Hz EA: 30.83 ± 3.59 years; 20/100-Hz EA: 30.13 ± 3.31 years [cite: 39].
* **Sex distribution:** 100% Female (primigravida women who underwent cesarean delivery) [cite: 39].
* **BMI:** sham EA: 26.65 ± 3.22 kg/m²; 2-Hz EA: 27.16 ± 3.47 kg/m²; 20/100-Hz EA: 27.82 ± 3.54 kg/m² [cite: 39].
* **ASA classification:** Not reported [cite: 39].
* **Comorbidities:** Not reported [cite: 39].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Cesarean delivery (CD) [cite: 39].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** Not explicitly stated (assumed regional/general based on standard CD, but details missing in text) [cite: 39].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Electroacupuncture (EA) [cite: 39].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 2-Hz EA group: 53; 20/100-Hz EA group: 53 [cite: 39].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Bilateral Zusanli (ST36) and Sanyinjiao (SP6) [cite: 39].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** Continuous wave at 2 Hz or alternating administration of 20 Hz and 100 Hz at 3-second intervals [cite: 39].
* **Treatment intensity:** 0.1-5.0 mA, appropriate for patient tolerance [cite: 39]. Inserted vertically to a depth of 25 to 30 mm; acupuncturists twirled the needle handles back and forth to achieve the sensation of "de qi" [cite: 39].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** 6, 12, 24, and 48 hours after surgery [cite: 39].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 25 minutes [cite: 39].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** 4 treatments [cite: 39].
* **Total treatment period:** Up to 48 hours after surgery [cite: 39].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Patient-controlled intravenous analgesia (PCIA) [cite: 39].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Sham EA [cite: 39].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 52 [cite: 39].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Received sham acupoints (20 mm aside from the ST36 and SP6 points) using pragmatic placebo needles (with a blunt tip) [cite: 39]. Similar procedures, but with no skin penetration, electricity output, or needle manipulation for "de qi" [cite: 39].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Patient-controlled intravenous analgesia (PCIA) using fentanyl [cite: 39]. Patients were also allowed to take anti-inflammatory analgesics as rescue medication for unbearable pain [cite: 39].
* **Dosage parameters:** Fentanyl consumption (mL) was measured [cite: 39]. (Exact mg/ml or PCIA pump settings were not detailed).
* **Duration:** Measured up to 48 hours postoperatively [cite: 39].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Pain score (scale not specified but graphs imply 0-10) [cite: 39].
* **Pain assessment time points:** 6, 12, 24, and 48 hours after surgery [cite: 39].
* **Mean pain score for each group (Mean ± SD):**
    * 6 h: sham EA = 7.23 ± 1.13; 2-Hz EA = 7.17 ± 1.21; 20/100-Hz EA = 6.87 ± 1.32 [cite: 39].
    * 12 h: sham EA = 4.71 ± 0.92; 2-Hz EA = 2.62 ± 0.84; 20/100-Hz EA = 2.42 ± 0.77 [cite: 39].
    * 24 h: sham EA = 3.54 ± 0.75; 2-Hz EA = 1.15 ± 0.69; 20/100-Hz EA = 1.15 ± 0.69 [cite: 39].
    * 48 h: sham EA = 2.48 ± 0.67; 2-Hz EA = 0.58 ± 0.49; 20/100-Hz EA = 0.62 ± 0.48 [cite: 39].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** Pain scores at 12, 24, and 48 hours were significantly lower in the two EA groups than in the sham EA group (P < 0.001) [cite: 39]. There was little difference between the 2-Hz and 20/100-Hz groups (P > .05) [cite: 39]. No statistical difference was seen at 6 hours (P > .05) [cite: 39].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Fentanyl [cite: 39].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous via PCIA [cite: 39].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** 
    * 48 h cumulative Fentanyl consumption (mL): 2-Hz EA = 83.76 ± 17.91; 20/100-Hz EA = 82.41 ± 21.18 [cite: 39].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** 
    * 48 h cumulative Fentanyl consumption (mL): sham EA = 94.88 ± 9.91 [cite: 39].
* **Measurement time point(s):** 6, 12, 24, and 48 hours after surgery [cite: 39].
* **Between-group difference:** Fentanyl consumption was significantly lower in the 2 EA groups than in the sham EA group at all time points (P < .05) [cite: 39].
* **Percentage/relative reduction:** Not explicitly reported in percentages [cite: 39].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Rescue analgesia details:** Assessed via the number of analgesic pump compressions (NAPC) [cite: 39].
* **Number of analgesic pump compressions (Median [IQR]):** 
    * 48 h: sham EA = 9.0 (4.3-12.0); 2-Hz EA = 1.0 (0.0-4.5); 20/100-Hz EA = 1.0 (0.0-3.0) [cite: 39].
    * The NAPC was significantly lower in the EA groups than in the sham EA group at 6, 12, 24, and 48 hours (P < .001) [cite: 39].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **Gastrointestinal recovery:** Time to first exhaust after surgery (hours, Mean ± SD): sham EA = 32.19 ± 12.93; 2-Hz EA = 30.77 ± 13.10; 20/100-Hz EA = 29.88 ± 14.07 [cite: 39]. There was no significant difference (P > .05) [cite: 39].
* **Other relevant physiological/biochemical outcomes (Biomarkers):** Interleukin 6 (IL-6) and procalcitonin (PCT) levels at 12 and 48 hours after surgery were not significantly different among the 3 groups (P > .05) [cite: 39].
* **Other postoperative complications:** 5 patients reported adverse events: skin bruising at ST36 (1 in 2-Hz EA), subcutaneous hematoma at the puncture site (3 in 20/100-Hz EA), and subcutaneous hematoma in the burrow area (1 in sham EA) [cite: 39]. No severe adverse events were reported [cite: 39].
