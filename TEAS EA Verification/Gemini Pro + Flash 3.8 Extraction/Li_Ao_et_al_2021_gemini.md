## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** No funding was received [cite: 4].
* **Country:** P.R. China (Tangshan, Hebei and Shijiazhuang, Hebei) [cite: 4].
* **Journal:** EXPERIMENTAL AND THERAPEUTIC MEDICINE [cite: 4].
* **DOI:** 10.3892/etm.2021.9615 [cite: 4].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** www.chictr.org.cn (Clinical Trial no. ChiCTR1800017768) [cite: 4].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Randomized controlled trial [cite: 4].
* **Randomization method:** Sequentially-numbered sealed envelopes and a random number generator [cite: 4].
* **Blinding:** The anesthesiologist who provided anesthesia and the anesthesiologist who conducted follow-ups were blinded [cite: 4]. The operators administering the intervention could not be blinded [cite: 4].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** TEAS group and sham TEAS group [cite: 4].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 70 randomized (65 analyzed: TEAS n=32, Sham n=33) [cite: 4].
* **Age:** TEAS group: 45.6 ± 9.8 years; Sham TEAS group: 46.9 ± 8.6 years [cite: 4].
* **Sex distribution:** 100% Female (adult female patients) [cite: 4].
* **BMI:** TEAS group: 23.4 ± 4.2 kg/m²; Sham TEAS group: 22.6 ± 3.3 kg/m² [cite: 4]. Patients with BMI > 30 kg/m² were excluded [cite: 4].
* **ASA classification:** ASA I or II [cite: 4]. TEAS group: 26 (ASA I), 6 (ASA II); Sham TEAS group: 24 (ASA I), 9 (ASA II) [cite: 4].
* **Comorbidities:** Excluded patients with severe cardiac or respiratory diseases, significant renal or hepatic impairment, immune disorders, history of immunosuppressive therapy, chronic pain, or systemic infection [cite: 4].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Elective radical mastectomy of breast cancer [cite: 4].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 4].
* **Induction:** Intravenous midazolam (1-2 mg), sufentanil (0.3-0.5 μg/kg), propofol (2-3 mg/kg), and cisatracurium (0.2 mg/kg) [cite: 4].
* **Maintenance:** Propofol and remifentanil via target-controlled infusion [cite: 4].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Transcutaneous electrical acupoint stimulation (TEAS) [cite: 4].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 32 analyzed [cite: 4].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Bilateral Hegu (LI-4), Neiguan (PC-6), and Zusanli (ST-36) [cite: 4].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** Dense-and-disperse frequency of 2/100 Hz [cite: 4].
* **Treatment intensity:** Optimal intensity set to mild twitching of the surrounding muscle and individual maximum tolerance (5-10 mA for upper limbs; 10-30 mA for lower limbs) [cite: 4].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** 30 minutes before induction of anesthesia, 4 and 12 hours post-surgery on the day of surgery, and three times daily (8 a.m., 2 p.m., 8 p.m.) at postoperative days 1 and 2 [cite: 4].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 30 minutes each time [cite: 4].
* **Total treatment period:** Preoperative phase to 48 hours post-surgery [cite: 4].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Patient-controlled analgesia (PCA) pump for 48 hours post-surgery [cite: 4].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Sham TEAS [cite: 4].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 33 analyzed [cite: 4].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Electrode attachment on the target acupoints without electronic stimulation [cite: 4].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** PCA consisting of 1.5 μg/kg sufentanil, diluted to 150 ml with normal saline [cite: 4].
* **PCA parameters:** Basal infusion rate 2 ml/h, bolus dose 0.5 ml, lockout interval 15 minutes [cite: 4].
* **Duration:** Maintained for 48 hours post-surgery [cite: 4].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Visual analogue scale (VAS), scores 0-10 [cite: 4].
* **Pain assessment time points:** 4 h (T1), 12 h (T2), 24 h (T3), and 48 h (T4) post-surgery [cite: 4].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group:** Presented graphically in the study [cite: 4]. Exact numeric values for pain scores are not tabulated in the text [cite: 4].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** VAS scores at 12 hours (T2) and 24 hours (T3) were significantly lower in the TEAS group compared to the sham TEAS group (P < 0.05) [cite: 4]. There were no statistical differences at 4 h (T1) and 48 h (T4) [cite: 4].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Sufentanil [cite: 4].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous via PCA [cite: 4].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group (volume):** 102.8 ± 7.4 ml within 48 h [cite: 4].
* **Opioid consumption for control group (volume):** 120.6 ± 9.2 ml within 48 h [cite: 4].
* **Measurement time point(s):** Total consumption within 48 h post-surgery [cite: 4].
* **Between-group difference:** Total consumption of opioids in the PCA pump was significantly lower in the TEAS group compared with the sham TEAS group (P < 0.001) [cite: 4].
* **Conversion to MME / Absolute reduction / MCID:** Not reported [cite: 4].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Rescue analgesia requirement:** Measured via the "number of effective press" of the PCA pump [cite: 4].
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia (Effective press counts):** TEAS group: 6.2 ± 3.7; Sham TEAS group: 12.3 ± 4.6 (P < 0.001) [cite: 4].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** Incidence of PONV was significantly lower in the TEAS group (4 cases) compared to the Sham TEAS group (12 cases) (P = 0.042) [cite: 4].
* **Other postoperative complications:** Incidence of headache was significantly lower in the TEAS group (2 cases vs 9 cases, P = 0.044) [cite: 4].
* **Dizziness/Pruritus:** No significant differences were detected regarding the incidence of dizziness (P = 0.614) or pruritus (P = 0.672) [cite: 4].
* **Other relevant physiological/biochemical outcomes (Immune function):** 
    * Serum levels of IFN-γ at T3 and T4 were higher in the TEAS group [cite: 4]. 
    * Serum levels of IL-2 and the IL-2/IL-4 ratio at T2, T3, and T4 were higher in the TEAS group [cite: 4]. 
    * Serum levels of IL-4 were lower at T2, T3, and T4 in the TEAS group [cite: 4].
