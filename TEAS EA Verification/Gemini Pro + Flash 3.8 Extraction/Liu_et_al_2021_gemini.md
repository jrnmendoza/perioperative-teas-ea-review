## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China (81771134), Hebei Province technology Innovation guide Project Science and Technology Winter Olympics special project (19977790D), and the Hebei Provincial government [cite: 53].
* **Country:** China (Cangzhou, Hebei) [cite: 53].
* **Journal:** Clinical Interventions in Aging [cite: 53].
* **DOI:** 10.2147/CIA.S309982 [cite: 53].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** China Clinical Trial Registry (ChiCTR2000040397) [cite: 53]. Approved by the ethics committee of Cangzhou Central Hospital (2020-153-02) [cite: 53].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Prospective, single-center, parallel-group, randomized trial [cite: 53].
* **Randomization method:** Randomly divided into two groups according to a random number table using an online randomization tool [cite: 53].
* **Blinding:** The trained researchers who performed the MMSE score were blinded to the grouping of patients [cite: 53].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** TEAS group (Group T) and control group (Group C) [cite: 53].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 105 initially selected; 100 randomized and analyzed (Group T: n=50; Group C: n=50) [cite: 53].
* **Age:** Group T: 70.80 ± 5.41 years; Group C: 69.68 ± 4.85 years (mean ± SD) [cite: 53].
* **Sex distribution:** Group T: 22 Female (44%); Group C: 27 Female (54%) [cite: 53].
* **BMI:** Group T: 24.75 ± 2.57 kg/m²; Group C: 24.82 ± 3.47 kg/m² [cite: 53].
* **ASA classification:** ASA grade I-II [cite: 53]. (Group T: ASA I = 25 [50%]; Group C: ASA I = 27 [54%]) [cite: 53].
* **Comorbidities:** Baseline characteristics included hypertension, diabetes mellitus, coronary heart disease, and hypoproteinemia [cite: 53]. Excluded if history of neurological/psychiatric disorders, severe cardiac/pulmonary/hepatic/renal dysfunction, or MMSE ≤ 23 [cite: 53].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Laparoscopic radical resection of colon cancer [cite: 53].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 53].
* **Induction:** 2-5 μg/kg fentanyl, 2-5 mg/kg propofol, and 0.2-0.3 mg/kg cis-atracurium [cite: 53].
* **Maintenance:** Remifentanil (0.25-0.4 μg/kg/min) plus propofol (TIVA, 4-12 mg/kg/h) [cite: 53]. BIS maintained between 40-60 [cite: 53].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Transcutaneous electrical acupoint stimulation (TEAS) [cite: 53].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 50 analyzed [cite: 53].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Bilateral Neiguan (PC6), Hegu (LI4), and Zusanli (ST36) [cite: 53].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** Density wave, frequency 2-100 Hz [cite: 53].
* **Treatment intensity:** Maximum current intensity the patient could tolerate [cite: 53].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** From 30 min before the induction of anesthesia to the end of surgery [cite: 53].
* **Duration of each treatment:** Maintained continuously throughout surgery (mean surgical duration in Group T was 225.6 ± 53.28 min) plus 30 min pre-induction [cite: 53].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** One single continuous intraoperative session [cite: 53].
* **Total treatment period:** Single day (intraoperative) [cite: 53].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Electronic pump controlled intravenous analgesia postoperatively [cite: 53].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Control group (sham) [cite: 53].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 50 analyzed [cite: 53].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Electrodes were placed in the same position as Group T, but no electric current stimulation was performed [cite: 53].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Electronic pump controlled intravenous analgesia containing 0.15 μg/kg sufentanil in 100 mL saline [cite: 53].
* **Dosage parameters:** Flow rate 2 mL/h; bolus 0.5 mL; lockout time 15 min [cite: 53].
* **Duration:** Tracked over 24 h postoperatively [cite: 53].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Visual Analogue Score (VAS) [cite: 53].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Recorded at rest and during coughing [cite: 53].
* **Pain assessment time points:** Evaluated within 24 h after surgery [cite: 53].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group:**
    * VAS rest: Group T = 2.52 ± 0.505; Group C = 2.66 ± 0.626 [cite: 53].
    * VAS coughing: Group T = 3.20 ± 0.571; Group C = 3.16 ± 0.681 [cite: 53].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** There were no differences in VAS scores 24h after surgery between Group T and C [cite: 53].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Fentanyl (induction), Remifentanil (maintenance), Sufentanil (postoperative) [cite: 53].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous [cite: 53].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** Total consumption not explicitly reported [cite: 53].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** Total consumption not explicitly reported [cite: 53].
* **Measurement time point(s):** N/A [cite: 53].
* **Between-group difference:** Not reported [cite: 53].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Addressed via the number of analgesic pump compressions [cite: 53].
* **Number of compressions (times, mean ± SD):** Group T = 17.62 ± 2.687; Group C = 17.38 ± 3.801 (P = 0.716) [cite: 53]. There were no differences between groups [cite: 53].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **Postoperative Cognitive Decline (POCD) (Primary Outcome):** 
    * Measured via Z-score based on MMSE [cite: 53].
    * Incidence of POCD on POD1-3 was not statistically significant between groups (e.g., POD1: Group T = 10%, Group C = 25%, P = 0.102) [cite: 53].
    * The cumulative duration of POCD on POD2 and POD3 in Group T was significantly lower than that in Group C (P = 0.026 and P = 0.006, respectively) [cite: 53].
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** Not reported [cite: 53].
* **Length of stay:** Length of stays (days): Group T = 16.10 ± 2.30; Group C = 16.46 ± 2.06 (P = 0.412) [cite: 53].
* **Other relevant physiological/biochemical outcomes (Biomarkers):** 
    * At 1h after surgery (T1) and end of surgery (T2), serum levels of IL-6 and hs-CRP were significantly decreased in Group T compared to Group C (P < 0.001) [cite: 53]. 
    * The level of CGRP was significantly increased in Group T compared to Group C at T1 and T2 (P < 0.001) [cite: 53].
