# Data Extraction: pdf.pdf (Liu et al., 2025)

## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Funded by National Natural Science Foundation of China (82374451) [cite: 49].
* **Country:** China (Guangzhou) [cite: 49].
* **Journal:** Frontiers in Neurology [cite: 49].
* **DOI:** 10.3389/fneur.2025.1580759 [cite: 49].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** Chinese Clinical Trial Registry (ChiCTR2300077984) [cite: 49].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Randomized controlled trial [cite: 49].
* **Randomization method:** Randomization sequences generated using SPSS in a 1:1 ratio, and group allocation was concealed in serially numbered, sealed opaque envelopes [cite: 49].
* **Blinding:** Double-blind; the surgeons, anesthesiologist, patients, data collectors, ECG physicians, sleep physicians and statisticians were unaware of the groups throughout the study [cite: 49]. The EA provider documented the group allocation in a secluded place and a consistent tone [cite: 49]. Sterile sheets covered the areas to ensure blinding [cite: 49].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** Electroacupuncture (EA) group and control group [cite: 49].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 132 assessed for eligibility; 80 randomized (EA: n=40; control: n=40) [cite: 49]. 77 finished the study and were analyzed (EA: n=38; control: n=39) [cite: 49].
* **Age:** EA group: 60.3 ± 11.9 years; control group: 61.3 ± 10.5 years (mean ± SD) [cite: 49].
* **Sex distribution:** EA group: 15 Male / 23 Female; control group: 20 Male / 19 Female [cite: 49].
* **BMI:** Weight reported: EA group = 60.4 ± 6.0 kg; control group = 60.2 ± 10.0 kg [cite: 49]. BMI not explicitly reported [cite: 49].
* **ASA classification:** ASA physical status 1 to 3 [cite: 49]. ASA I/II/III: EA group = 8 (21.1%) / 29 (76.3%) / 1 (2.6%); control group = 13 (33.3%) / 22 (56.4%) / 4 (10.3%) [cite: 49].
* **Comorbidities:** Patients with cardiac, pulmonary, hepatic or renal dysfunction, pre-existed cardiac arrhythmia, history of sleep disturbances, abnormal thyroid function, or mental impairment were excluded [cite: 49]. History of smoking was present in 11 (28.9%) EA patients and 14 (35.9%) control patients [cite: 49].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Single-port thoracoscopic lung cancer surgery [cite: 49].
* **Specific procedures:** Segmentectomy (EA: n=22, control: n=25) and Lobectomy (EA: n=16, control: n=14) [cite: 49].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia with one-lung ventilation [cite: 49].
* **Induction:** 0.3 μg/kg sufentanil, 1 to 2.5 mg/kg propofol and 0.1 mg/kg vecuronium bromide [cite: 49].
* **Maintenance:** Remifentanil 0.1-0.2 μg/kg/min and sevoflurane 1-3% were used in combination for anesthesia maintenance [cite: 49]. Bispectral index values were kept between 40 and 60 [cite: 49].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Electroacupuncture (EA) [cite: 49].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 38 analyzed [cite: 49].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Neiguan (PC6) and Gongsun (SP4) acupoints on the surgical side [cite: 49].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** 2/100 Hz [cite: 49].
* **Treatment intensity:** Maximum current the patient can tolerate, usually between 8 and 12 mA, to achieve a "de qi" sensation [cite: 49].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** Started 30 min before anesthetic induction, lasted until the end of the surgery, and was repeated at the 24th hour after the surgery [cite: 49].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 30 min pre-induction + intraoperative period (surgery duration: 146 ± 46 min), and 30 min at 24 hours postoperatively [cite: 49].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** Two sessions (one perioperative, one at 24 hours postoperatively) [cite: 49].
* **Total treatment period:** Up to 24 hours postoperatively [cite: 49].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Parecoxib sodium 50 mg was injected IV before surgical skin incision [cite: 49]. At 5 min before surgery end, 0.1 μg/kg sufentanil was given and 0.5% (0.2 mL/kg) ropivacaine was infiltrated into the wound [cite: 49]. Patient-controlled intravenous analgesia (PCIA) pump was connected for postoperative analgesia [cite: 49].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Control group (Sham EA) [cite: 49].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 39 analyzed [cite: 49].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Needles were inserted to the same depth at non-specific acupoints near PC6 and SP4 on the surgical side [cite: 49].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Patient-controlled intravenous analgesia (PCIA) containing sufentanil (1.5 μg/mL) [cite: 49].
* **Dosage parameters:** PCIA dose: 3 mL, background dose: 2 mL/h, interval: 20 min [cite: 49].
* **Duration:** Maintained for 2 days postoperatively [cite: 49].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Visual analog scores (VAS) (0-10) [cite: 49].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Not specified [cite: 49].
* **Pain assessment time points:** 1 h, 6 h, 12 h, 24 h, and 48 h postoperatively [cite: 49].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group:** Depicted in Figure 2; visual data implies pain scores ranged from roughly 1 to 3 [cite: 49].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** From 1 h to 48 h after surgery, the VAS scores were all comparable between the two groups and postoperative pain was well controlled [cite: 49]. VAS scores did not differ between groups [cite: 49].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Sufentanil and Remifentanil [cite: 49].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous [cite: 49].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** Intraoperative dose of sufentanil = 24.0 ± 2.8 μg; Intraoperative dose of remifentanil = 911.1 ± 233.8 μg [cite: 49].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** Intraoperative dose of sufentanil = 24.1 ± 4.0 μg; Intraoperative dose of remifentanil = 1095.9 ± 362.2 μg [cite: 49].
* **Measurement time point(s):** Intraoperative period [cite: 49].
* **Between-group difference:** The dose of remifentanil was significantly reduced in the EA group (P = 0.01) [cite: 49]. There was no significant difference in the dose of sufentanil (P = 0.98) [cite: 49].
* **Percentage/relative reduction:** EA treatment reduced intraoperative remifentanil consumption by almost 17% [cite: 49].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Not reported [cite: 49].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **Postoperative Arrhythmia (Primary Outcome):** 
    * Incidence of new-onset supraventricular tachycardia (SVT) during the first 24 h: EA group = 4 (10.5%); Control group = 13 (33.3%) (P = 0.02) [cite: 49]. 
    * Use of β-blockers during the first 24 h: EA group = 3 (7.9%); Control group = 11 (28.2%) (P = 0.04) [cite: 49].
    * There were no significant differences in the incidence of supraventricular ectopic beat (SVEB) and ventricular premature beat (VPB) between groups [cite: 49].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes (Sleep Quality):** 
    * Total sleep time (TST) during the first postoperative night (min): EA group = 209.5 [73.8 to 261.5]; Control group = 119 [48 to 193] (P = 0.02) [cite: 49].
    * Awake time (min): EA group = 134.5 [72.8 to 248.3]; Control group = 225.0 [153.0 to 316.0] (P = 0.01) [cite: 49].
    * Sleep duration of stage N1 and stage N2 were significantly longer in the EA group [cite: 49].
* **Length of stay:** Not reported [cite: 49].
* **Other intraoperative/postoperative complications:** Incidence of intraoperative hypotension was significantly lower in the EA group (6 [15.8%]) than in the control group (15 [38.5%]) (P = 0.05) [cite: 49]. Adverse effects such as redness, swelling, and inflammatory reactions were not observed at the acupuncture site [cite: 49].
