## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Supported by the Bureau of Science and Technology, China, ZY2023051 [cite: 51].
* **Country:** China [cite: 51].
* **Journal:** Perioperative Medicine [cite: 51].
* **DOI:** 10.1186/s13741-026-00656-7 [cite: 51].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** Chinese Clinical Trials Registry (ChiCTR2300078615) [cite: 51]. The protocol was approved by the Science and Technology Ethics Committee of the First Affiliated Hospital of Shihezi University (reference: KJ2023-385-01) [cite: 51].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Randomized, double-blind, controlled trial [cite: 51].
* **Randomization method:** Computer-generated design in a 1:1 ratio, with allocations concealed in sequentially numbered, opaque, sealed envelopes [cite: 51].
* **Blinding:** Double-blind; all individuals involved in the study (patients, surgeons, anesthesiologists, data collectors, data analysts) were blinded to the randomization and stimulation [cite: 51]. The TEAS operator, who was independent of the study, conducted the stimulation or false stimulation [cite: 51].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** TEAS group (T group) and control group (C group) [cite: 51].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 102 assessed for eligibility, 88 randomized, 86 enrolled, completed, and analyzed (T group: n=43; C group: n=43) [cite: 51].
* **Age:** T group: 42.0 ± 9.8 years; C group: 39.5 ± 11.3 years (mean ± SD) [cite: 51].
* **Sex distribution:** T group: 34 Male / 9 Female; C group: 33 Male / 10 Female [cite: 51].
* **BMI:** T group: 24.6 ± 3.5 kg/m²; C group: 24.9 ± 3.2 kg/m² [cite: 51].
* **ASA classification:** ASA physical status I or II [cite: 51]. T group: 11 (25.6%) ASA I, 32 (74.4%) ASA II; C group: 9 (20.9%) ASA I, 34 (79.1%) ASA II [cite: 51].
* **Comorbidities:** Baseline characteristics included hypertension, diabetes mellitus, coronary heart disease, and obesity [cite: 51]. Patients were excluded if they had major systemic diseases, alcohol or substance abuse, chronic pain, or long-term analgesic medication [cite: 51].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Burn wound debridement [cite: 51].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia (total intravenous anesthesia) [cite: 51].
* **Induction:** Midazolam 0.05 mg/kg, sufentanil 0.5 μg/kg, etomidate 0.2 mg/kg, and rocuronium 0.7 mg/kg [cite: 51].
* **Maintenance:** Propofol 4-12 mg/kg·h, remifentanil 0.1-1 μg/kg·min intravenous pumping, and rocuronium 0.2 mg/kg intravenous injection as needed [cite: 51]. Propofol and remifentanil dosage was adjusted to maintain a BIS value between 40-60 and SPI value between 20-50 [cite: 51]. Thirty minutes before the end of surgery, intravenous sufentanil 0.1 μg/kg was pushed [cite: 51].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Preoperative transcutaneous electrical acupoint stimulation (TEAS) [cite: 51].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 43 [cite: 51].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Bilateral LI4 (Hegu), PC6 (Neiguan), ST36 (Zusanli), and SP6 (Sanyinjiao) [cite: 51]. Note: The text lists "PC6 (Sanyinjiao)" which is likely a typo for SP6 (Sanyinjiao), but states four main acupoints [cite: 51].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** 2/100 Hz [cite: 51].
* **Treatment intensity:** <10 mA (gradually adjusted to the maximum intensity acceptable to the patient) [cite: 51].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** 30 min before the induction of anesthesia [cite: 51].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 30 minutes [cite: 51].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** One single preoperative session [cite: 51].
* **Total treatment period:** Single day (preoperative) [cite: 51].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Patient-controlled intravenous analgesia (PCIA) postoperatively [cite: 51].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** False stimulation (Sham TEAS) [cite: 51].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 43 [cite: 51].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Stimulated by a placebo-type stimulator that had no therapeutic effect [cite: 51]. The intervention caused numbness at acupoints but without real electrical stimulation [cite: 51].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Postoperative patient-controlled intravenous analgesia (PCIA) containing sufentanil 100 μg + butorphanol 4 mg + dexamethasone 5 mg + ondansetron 8 mg + 0.9% sodium chloride (total 100 ml) [cite: 51]. 
* **Dosage parameters:** Pressed the PCIA pump when F-VAS was above 40 mm, with an interval of 15 min [cite: 51].
* **Duration:** Measured and tracked until the first postoperative day (POD1) and 48 hours [cite: 51].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Facial-visual analogue scale (F-VAS), 0-100 mm [cite: 51].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Resting F-VAS score [cite: 51].
* **Pain assessment time points:** Before stimulation (F1), after stimulation (F2), 24 h after surgery (F3), and 48 h after surgery (F4) [cite: 51].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group:** Visualized in Figure 5 [cite: 51]. F2 and F3 in the T group were significantly lower than those in the C group [cite: 51].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** Before intervention, F1 was comparable (P = 0.336) [cite: 51]. F2 was significantly reduced in the T group compared with the C group (P = 0.030) [cite: 51]. At 24 h after surgery, F3 was significantly lower in the T group compared to the C group (P = 0.039) [cite: 51]. At 48 h after surgery, F4 was similar between the two groups (P = 0.061) [cite: 51].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Remifentanil, Sufentanil, and Propofol [cite: 51].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous [cite: 51].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** Intraoperative remifentanil dosage: 0.65 (0.45, 1.00) μg/kg/min (median [IQR]) (Note: text states 0.70(0.50,1.10) in results section, but abstract states 0.65) [cite: 51]. Intraoperative sufentanil consumption = 21.4 (3.1) μg; Propofol consumption = 400.0 (305.0, 625.0) mg [cite: 51].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** Intraoperative remifentanil dosage: 0.90 (0.67, 1.23) μg/kg/min (median [IQR]) [cite: 51]. Intraoperative sufentanil consumption = 20.0 (2.9) μg; Propofol consumption = 450.0 (365.0, 720.0) mg [cite: 51].
* **Measurement time point(s):** Intraoperative [cite: 51].
* **Between-group difference:** The dosage of remifentanil showed a significant difference between the two groups (P < 0.05) [cite: 51]. The consumption of intraoperative propofol was not significantly different between the two groups (P = 0.296) [cite: 51].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Use of rescue analgesics until POD1 (dezocine 5 mg IM): T group = 3 (7.0%); C group = 8 (18.6%) [cite: 51].
* **Between-group difference:** The use of rescue analgesics was similar between the two groups (P = 0.106) [cite: 51].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** Incidence of nausea-vomiting until POD1 was significantly lower in the T group (7 [16.3%]) than in the C group (16 [37.2%]) (P = 0.028) [cite: 51].
* **Gastrointestinal recovery:** Incidence of constipation until POD1 was significantly lower in the T group (8 [18.6%]) than in the C group (17 [39.5%]) (P = 0.033) [cite: 51].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** Incidence of drowsiness (T group: 7.0%; C group: 16.3%, P = 0.178) and dizziness (T group: 14.0%; C group: 25.6%, P = 0.176) showed no significant differences [cite: 51].
* **Length of stay:** Not reported [cite: 51].
* **Other postoperative complications:** No SAEs were reported in the study groups [cite: 51]. One patient in each group withdrew before analysis due to redness and itching on the skin while applying electrodes [cite: 51]. Hemodynamic indicators (MAP and HR) showed no significant differences between the two groups [cite: 51].
