## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Supported by the Zhejiang Traditional Chinese Medicine Scientific Research Fund Project [2020ZB102] and Jiangxi Provincial Health Commission Technology Plan [202510679] [cite: 43].
* **Country:** China (Nanchang, Hangzhou) [cite: 43].
* **Journal:** Injury [cite: 43].
* **DOI:** 10.1016/j.injury.2025.112660 [cite: 43].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** China Clinical Trial Registration Center (registration number: ChiCTR2100053050) [cite: 43]. Approved by the Ethics Committee of the Second Affiliated Hospital of Zhejiang Chinese Medicine University (IRB: 2019-KL-129-01) [cite: 43].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Randomized, prospective, double-blind, intervention-controlled clinical trial [cite: 43].
* **Randomization method:** Randomly assigned to either the control group (Group C) or the electroacupuncture group (Group A) using a random number table method [cite: 43].
* **Blinding:** Double-blind; specific blinding procedures for outcome assessors or patients are not fully detailed, but "double-masked" and "double-blind" are explicitly stated [cite: 43].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** Control group (Group C) and electroacupuncture group (Group A) [cite: 43].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 60 enrolled and randomized (Group C: n=30; Group A: n=30); 53 completed and were analyzed (Group C: n=26; Group A: n=27) [cite: 43].
* **Age:** Group C: 72.35 ± 15.15 years; Group A: 75.70 ± 10.95 years (mean ± SD) [cite: 43].
* **Sex distribution:** Group C: 15 Male / 11 Female; Group A: 14 Male / 13 Female [cite: 43].
* **BMI:** Group C: 22.22 ± 2.53 kg/m²; Group A: 21.61 ± 2.58 kg/m² [cite: 43].
* **ASA classification:** ASA classification I-III [cite: 43]. Group C: I=1 (3.8%), II=17 (65.4%), III=8 (30.8%); Group A: I=1 (3.7%), II=17 (63.0%), III=9 (33.3%) [cite: 43].
* **Comorbidities:** Patients with hip fracture (femoral neck, intertrochanteric, acetabular) [cite: 43]. Baseline comorbidities included hypertension, diabetes, cardiopathy, and cerebrovascular disease [cite: 43]. Excluded if they had severe visual/hearing impairment, senile dementia, history of epilepsy, alcoholism, neurological diseases, mental illness, or infected skin at the acupoints [cite: 43].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Surgical repair of fragility hip fractures [cite: 43].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General endotracheal anesthesia (GETA) with total intravenous anesthesia (TIVA) [cite: 43].
* **Induction:** Etomidate 0.3 mg/kg, sufentanil 0.3-0.5 μg/kg, and cisatracurium 0.15-0.2 mg/kg [cite: 43].
* **Maintenance:** Intravenous propofol (4-10 mg/kg/h), remifentanil (0.2-2 ug/kg/min), and cisatracurium (intermittently) [cite: 43]. Bispectral index was maintained between 40 and 60 [cite: 43].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Electroacupuncture (EA) / Transcutaneous electrical acupoint stimulation (TEAS) [cite: 43]. (The text uses both terms, but states "electronic pulse therapeutic device... used to stimulate acupoints with a millimeter needle or electrode patch") [cite: 43].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 27 analyzed [cite: 43].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Baihui (GV20), bilateral Neiguan (PC6), Zusanli (ST36), and Sanyinjiao (SP6) [cite: 43].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** 2/100 Hz density wave [cite: 43].
* **Treatment intensity:** Adjusted to patient tolerance [cite: 43].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** 30 min before anesthesia on the day of surgery and 30 min post-anesthesia in the PACU when the recovery score was ≥ 9 [cite: 43].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 30 minutes [cite: 43].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** Two sessions (one pre-anesthesia, one post-anesthesia in PACU) [cite: 43].
* **Total treatment period:** Single day (perioperative) [cite: 43].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Postoperative patient-controlled intravenous analgesia (PCIA) [cite: 43].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Non-stimulated control group [cite: 43].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 26 analyzed [cite: 43].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Electrodes were placed at the acupuncture points without electrical stimulation [cite: 43].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Postoperative patient-controlled intravenous analgesia (PCIA) consisting of 2 μg/kg sufentanil diluted in 100 ml of saline [cite: 43].
* **Dosage parameters:** Flow rate 1.5 ml/h; bolus 2 ml; lockout time 15 min [cite: 43].
* **Duration:** Tracked up to 48 hours [cite: 43].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Visual Analog Scale (VAS) pain score (0-10) [cite: 43].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Not specifically distinguished [cite: 43].
* **Pain assessment time points:** 24 and 48 hours post-surgery [cite: 43].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group (Mean ± SD):**
    * 24 h post-operation: Group A = 2.65 ± 0.94; Group C = 3.96 ± 0.96 [cite: 43].
    * 48 h post-operation: Group A = 1.85 ± 0.72; Group C = 2.65 ± 0.94 (Note: Text states C group is 2.65±0.94, which is the same as the 24h value in text, but Figure 9 shows a clear decrease) [cite: 43].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** VAS pain score at 24 h post-operation was significantly lower in Group A compared to Group C (P < 0.001) [cite: 43]. At 48 h post-operation, the VAS score remained significantly lower in Group A compared to Group C (P = 0.001) [cite: 43].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Sufentanil and Remifentanil [cite: 43].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous [cite: 43].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** PCIA consumption at 48 h post-surgery: 86.22 ± 4.27 ml [cite: 43]. Intraoperative consumption not explicitly reported [cite: 43].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** PCIA consumption at 48 h post-surgery: 89.23 ± 3.67 ml [cite: 43]. Intraoperative consumption not explicitly reported [cite: 43].
* **Measurement time point(s):** 48 h post-surgery [cite: 43].
* **Between-group difference:** The mean consumption of PCIA at 48 h post-surgery was significantly lower in Group A compared to Group C (P = 0.008) [cite: 43].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Addressed via mean number of PCIA compressions at 48 h post-surgery [cite: 43].
* **Mean PCIA compressions at 48 h:** Group A = 7.11 ± 2.14; Group C = 8.62 ± 1.84 [cite: 43]. The groups had significantly different mean PCIA compressions (P = 0.008) [cite: 43].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **Perioperative Neurocognitive Disorders (PND) (Primary Outcome):** 
    * PND was assessed by a drop of two or more points in the MMSE score post-surgery [cite: 43].
    * Incidence on post-op day 1: Group A = 7 (25.0%); Group C = 14 (56.0%) (P < 0.05) [cite: 43].
    * Incidence on post-op day 3: Group A = 4 (14.3%); Group C = 12 (48.0%) (P < 0.05) [cite: 43].
    * Incidence on post-op day 7: Group A = 1 (3.6%); Group C = 2 (8.0%) (P > 0.05) [cite: 43].
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** The incidence of postoperative nausea and vomiting (PONV) was significantly lower in Group A (1 case, 3.7%) compared to Group C (8 cases, 30.8%) (P = 0.024) [cite: 43].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** The proportion of patients with postoperative recovery room stays exceeding 30 min was higher in Group C (42.3%) than in Group A (29.6%), though not statistically significant (P = 0.336) [cite: 43]. Mean extubation time was Group C: 10.85 ± 8.44 min vs Group A: 7.44 ± 6.03 min (P = 0.097) [cite: 43].
* **Length of stay:** Average hospital stay was 14.77 ± 3.57 days for Group C and 13.37 ± 3.03 days for Group A (P = 0.130) [cite: 43].
* **Other relevant physiological/biochemical outcomes (Biomarkers):** Time-group interactions were significant for IL-1β (P=0.002) and IL-6 (P=0.001) [cite: 43]. At 30 min after surgery (t1), IL-6 levels were significantly lower in Group A compared to Group C (P < 0.05) [cite: 43]. No significant differences in S-100β levels were observed between the groups at any time point [cite: 43]. Group A exhibited a significantly lower MAP value compared to Group C during intubation (T2) (P < 0.05) [cite: 43].
