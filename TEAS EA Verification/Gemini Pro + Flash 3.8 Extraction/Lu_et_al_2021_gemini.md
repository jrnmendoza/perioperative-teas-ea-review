## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Supported by the National Key Basic Research Program of China (No. 2014CB543200), the Overseas, Hong Kong & Macao Scholars Collaborative Researching Fund (No. 81228022), Major Program of National Natural Science Foundation of China (No. 81590954), the National Natural Science Foundation of China (No. 81730032, 81871028) and the Natural Science Foundation of Shaanxi Province (No. 2018SF-277) [cite: 49].
* **Country:** China [cite: 49].
* **Journal:** Journal of Clinical Anesthesia [cite: 49].
* **DOI:** 10.1016/j.jclinane.2021.110453 [cite: 49].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** Clinicaltrials.gov identifier: NCT02741726 [cite: 49].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Multicenter randomized clinical trial [cite: 49].
* **Randomization method:** Randomized 1:1:1 using a centralized computer-generated randomization system (secured web-based system) that was stratified according to permuted blocks (random block sizes; maximum of 12) by the treatment center [cite: 49].
* **Blinding:** Anesthesiologists, surgeons, and outcome assessors were blinded to the interventions [cite: 49]. The patients and investigators who participated in the intervention were not masked [cite: 49].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** Sham control group, single acupoint group, and combined acupoints group [cite: 49].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 1593 assessed for eligibility; 576 randomized; 568 completed the trial and were analyzed in the ITT population (Sham group: n=188; Single acupoint group: n=198; Combined acupoint group: n=190) [cite: 49].
* **Age:** Sham group: 48.2 ± 8.2 years; Single acupoint: 48.0 ± 9.1 years; Combined acupoint: 48.2 ± 9.0 years [cite: 49].
* **Sex distribution:** 100% Female (women scheduled for radical mastectomy) [cite: 49].
* **BMI:** Sham group: 23.3 ± 2.8 kg/m²; Single acupoint: 23.7 ± 2.6 kg/m²; Combined acupoint: 23.6 ± 2.5 kg/m² [cite: 49].
* **ASA classification:** ASA status I/II [cite: 49]. Sham group: 135/49; Single acupoint: 144/52; Combined acupoint: 135/53 [cite: 49].
* **Comorbidities:** Baseline characteristics included Charlson score and pre-existing pain of the surgical site [cite: 49]. Exclusions: contraindications to TEAS, communication difficulties, history of breast surgery, drug/alcohol abuse, cardiac dysfunction, severe hypertension, liver/renal dysfunction [cite: 49].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Radical mastectomy [cite: 49].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** Total intravenous anesthesia with endotracheal intubation (general anesthesia) [cite: 49].
* **Induction:** Midazolam, propofol, and fentanyl [cite: 49].
* **Maintenance:** Continuous infusion of remifentanil and propofol titrated to maintain a Narcotrend index of 40-60 and stable hemodynamics [cite: 49].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Transcutaneous electrical acupoint stimulation (TEAS) [cite: 49].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** Single acupoint group: 198 analyzed; Combined acupoint group: 190 analyzed [cite: 49].
* **Acupuncture points used:** 
    * Single acupoint group: Bilateral PC6 (Neiguan) [cite: 49].
    * Combined acupoint group: Bilateral PC6 (Neiguan) and CV17 (Danzhong) [cite: 49].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** "Disperse-dense" waves with alternating frequencies of 2 Hz and 10 Hz for two cycles [cite: 49].
* **Treatment intensity:** Maximal tolerance to the "Teh Chi" sensations [cite: 49].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** 30 min before anesthesia induction [cite: 49].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 30 min [cite: 49].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** One single preoperative session [cite: 49].
* **Total treatment period:** Single day (preoperative) [cite: 49].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** 40 mg of parecoxib sodium was administered before the incision [cite: 49].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Sham-operated control [cite: 49].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 188 analyzed [cite: 49].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Received electrode attachment but without stimulation [cite: 49].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Sufentanil was used for patient-controlled analgesia after surgery [cite: 49]. Parecoxib sodium (40 mg) was administered as rescue analgesia for those whose NRS was higher than 4 [cite: 49].
* **Dosage parameters:** Not specifically detailed for PCA [cite: 49].
* **Duration:** Analgesia satisfaction and NRS were evaluated up to 24 h post-surgery; chronic pain assessed at 3 and 6 months [cite: 49].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Numeric rating scale (NRS) [cite: 49].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** NRS assessed at rest and at cough [cite: 49].
* **Pain assessment time points:** 24 h (acute pain); 3 months and 6 months (chronic pain) [cite: 49].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group:**
    * NRS at rest at 24 h (median (IQR)): Sham = 1 (0, 1.6); Single = 0.9 (0, 1.3); Combined = 1 (0, 1) [cite: 49].
    * NRS at cough at 24 h (median (IQR)): Sham = 1.1 (0.1, 2.8); Single = 1.3 (0.3, 2.9); Combined = 1 (0, 2.3) [cite: 49].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** The NRS scores at rest and cough at 24h did not differ among the three groups (P = 0.12 and 0.49) [cite: 49].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Remifentanil (intraoperative), Fentanyl (induction), Sufentanil (postoperative PCA) [cite: 49].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous [cite: 49].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** Intraoperative Remifentanil consumption: Single acupoint = 1.3 ± 0.6 mg; Combined acupoint = 1.2 ± 0.5 mg [cite: 49].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** Intraoperative Remifentanil consumption: Sham group = 1.4 ± 0.7 mg [cite: 49].
* **Measurement time point(s):** Intraoperative [cite: 49].
* **Between-group difference:** Remifentanil consumption during surgery was significantly lower in the combined acupoints group than in the sham group (P < 0.001); however, it was not different between the single acupoint and sham groups (P = 0.04; note: P-value threshold for significance was corrected to 0.0125 for multiple comparisons) [cite: 49].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Rescue analgesia by 24 h after surgery: Sham group = 7 (3.7%); Single acupoint = 3 (1.5%); Combined acupoint = 4 (2.1%) [cite: 49].
* **Between-group difference:** The demand for rescue analgesics was similar among the groups (P = 0.35) [cite: 49].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **Chronic Pain (Primary Outcome):** 
    * Post-mastectomy chronic pain at 6 months: Combined acupoints = 42/190 (22.1%); Sham = 65/188 (34.6%); Single acupoint = 72/198 (36.4%) [cite: 49].
    * The incidence of chronic pain at 6 months was significantly lower in the combined acupoints group compared to the sham group (P = 0.007) and the single acupoint group (P = 0.002) [cite: 49].
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** 
    * Incidence of PONV by 24 h after surgery: Combined acupoints = 35 (18.4%); Single acupoint = 59 (29.8%); Sham = 68 (36.2%) [cite: 49].
    * The incidence of PONV in the combined acupoints group was significantly lower than that in the single acupoint group (P = 0.009) and sham group (P < 0.001) [cite: 49].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** Time to verbal response and time to extubation did not differ among the groups (P = 0.80 and 0.82) [cite: 49]. Patient's satisfaction score with analgesia was similar among groups [cite: 49].
* **Length of stay:** Not reported [cite: 49].
* **Other postoperative complications:** One patient in the single acupoint group reported discomfort in the skin area attached to the electrodes [cite: 49].
