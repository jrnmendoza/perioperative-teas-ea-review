## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Supported by the National Key Basic Research Program of China (No. 2014CB543200), the National Natural Science Foundation of China (No. 81871028), the National Cultivation and Development Program (2021ZY019) and the Aeromedical Flow Principal Investigator Program of Air Force Medical University (2022HYP102) [cite: 47].
* **Country:** China (Xi'an, Wuhan, Chongqing) [cite: 47].
* **Journal:** Journal of Multidisciplinary Healthcare [cite: 47].
* **DOI:** 10.2147/JMDH.S391262 [cite: 47].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** ClinicalTrials.gov (NCT02921529) [cite: 47]. Approved by the Institutional Ethics Committee of each participating hospital (Xijing Hospital, Tongji Hospital, and First Affiliated Hospital of Chongqing Medical University) [cite: 47].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Prospective multicenter randomized controlled trial (sham-controlled is mentioned in text, but Control group received "no stimulation") [cite: 47].
* **Randomization method:** Randomly assigned in a ratio of 1:1 using a computer-generated random allocation sequence; the randomization code for each patient was put in a sealed envelope [cite: 47].
* **Blinding:** Interventions were performed by a designated investigator who was not involved in the anaesthesia or the follow-up [cite: 47]. Investigators involved in the follow-up were blinded to the group allocation [cite: 47]. The stimulator was placed in an opaque box [cite: 47]. Patients were told they may or may not feel the electrical stimulation [cite: 47].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** TEAS group and Control group [cite: 47].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 210 assessed for eligibility; 100 randomized; 94 completed the study and were analyzed (TEAS: n=47; Control: n=47) [cite: 47].
* **Age:** TEAS group: 56.2 ± 9.0 years; Control group: 55.6 ± 9.9 years (mean ± SD) [cite: 47].
* **Sex distribution:** TEAS group: 26 Male / 21 Female; Control group: 23 Male / 24 Female [cite: 47].
* **BMI:** TEAS group: 22.5 ± 2.6 kg/m²; Control group: 22.8 ± 2.8 kg/m² [cite: 47].
* **ASA classification:** ASA status I-III [cite: 47]. TEAS group: 0 ASA I / 45 ASA II / 2 ASA III; Control group: 1 ASA I / 43 ASA II / 3 ASA III [cite: 47].
* **Comorbidities:** Included patients scheduled for elective colon surgery; excluded those with contraindications for electrical stimulation, difficulty communicating, drug/alcohol abuse/addiction, or severe hepatic or renal dysfunction [cite: 47].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Laparoscopic colon surgery (Left, Right, or Sigmoid) [cite: 47]. TEAS group: 6 Left / 18 Right / 23 Sigmoid; Control group: 5 Left / 18 Right / 24 Sigmoid [cite: 47].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 47].
* **Induction:** Midazolam, propofol, and fentanyl; rocuronium given to facilitate tracheal intubation [cite: 47].
* **Maintenance:** Remifentanil infusion and sevoflurane [cite: 47].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Transcutaneous electrical acupoint stimulation (TEAS) [cite: 47].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 47 analyzed [cite: 47].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Bilateral PC6, LI4, ST36, and SP6 (electrodes placed at all, but text states stimulation received at bilateral PC6 and ST36) [cite: 47].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** "Disperse-dense" waves of alternating frequencies of 2 Hz and 10 Hz for 2 cycles [cite: 47].
* **Treatment intensity:** Increasing electrical stimulation intensity (4 mA to 11 mA) was applied to identify the threshold intensity [cite: 47].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** 30 min before induction, and then in the morning once a day for 3 days after surgery [cite: 47].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 30 minutes [cite: 47].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** 4 sessions (one preoperative, and one per day for 3 days postoperatively) [cite: 47].
* **Total treatment period:** Up to 3 days postoperatively [cite: 47].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** ERAS protocol [cite: 47].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Control group (no stimulation) [cite: 47].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 47 analyzed [cite: 47].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Electrodes were placed, but patients received no stimulation [cite: 47].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Patient-controlled analgesia (PCA) [cite: 47].
* **Dosage parameters:** Not specifically detailed [cite: 47].
* **Duration:** Tracked at 24 h, 48 h, and 72 h after surgery [cite: 47].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Visual Analogue Scale (VAS) (0-10) [cite: 47].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Assessed at rest and at cough [cite: 47].
* **Pain assessment time points:** 24 h, 48 h, and 72 h after surgery [cite: 47].
* **Median pain score for each group (median (min, max)):**
    * 24 h after surgery (at rest): TEAS = 2.2 (0.0, 10.0); Control = 1.2 (0.0, 8.1) (P = 0.41) [cite: 47].
    * 24 h after surgery (at cough): TEAS = 5.8 (0.4, 10.0); Control = 5.5 (1.4, 10.0) (P = 0.94) [cite: 47].
    * 48 h after surgery (at rest): TEAS = 1.2 (0.0, 6.7); Control = 1.6 (0.0, 6.0) (P = 0.80) [cite: 47].
    * 48 h after surgery (at cough): TEAS = 5.0 (0.3, 10.0); Control = 5.0 (0.7, 10.0) (P = 0.98) [cite: 47].
    * 72 h after surgery (at rest): TEAS = 0.6 (0.0, 5.0); Control = 1.0 (0.0, 6.2) (P = 0.05) [cite: 47].
    * 72 h after surgery (at cough): TEAS = 3.5 (0.1, 10.0); Control = 4.0 (0.3, 10.0) (P = 0.33) [cite: 47].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** VAS at rest and at cough in 3 days after surgery were not significantly different between the groups (all P > 0.05) [cite: 47].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** PCA (drug not explicitly named in results, but likely opioid-based) [cite: 47].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous via PCA [cite: 47].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention/control groups:** Actual mg consumption not reported [cite: 47].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Evaluated via PCA deliveries and attempts [cite: 47].
* **PCA attempts/deliveries (median (min, max)):**
    * 24 h: TEAS attempts = 8.0 (3.0, 18.0) / deliveries = 5.0 (2.0, 12.0); Control attempts = 16.5 (8.0, 38.0) / deliveries = 12.0 (6.0, 22.0) [cite: 47].
    * 48 h: TEAS attempts = 13.0 (5.0, 22.0) / deliveries = 7.0 (3.0, 14.0); Control attempts = 26.0 (11.5, 45.5) / deliveries = 16.5 (8.0, 35.0) [cite: 47].
    * 72 h: TEAS attempts = 13.0 (5.0, 24.0) / deliveries = 7.0 (3.0, 20.0); Control attempts = 24.5 (13.0, 46.0) / deliveries = 16.5 (10.0, 36.0) [cite: 47].
* **Between-group difference:** Median PCA deliveries and PCA attempts at 24 h, 48 h, and 72 h after surgery were significantly less in the TEAS group than in the Control group (all P < 0.01) [cite: 47].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **Gastrointestinal recovery:** 
    * Time to flatus (mean ± SD): TEAS = 34.5 ± 16.7 h; Control = 42.4 ± 22.9 h (P = 0.03) [cite: 47].
    * Time to first defecation (mean ± SD): TEAS = 42.0 ± 25.0 h; Control = 55.4 ± 29.7 h (P = 0.03) [cite: 47].
    * Time to first oral intake of water: TEAS = 33.5 ± 22.3 h; Control = 28.9 ± 17.5 h (P = 0.28) [cite: 47].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** 
    * Time to meet discharge criteria (Primary Outcome): TEAS = 6.0 ± 1.8 days; Control = 7.0 ± 2.4 days (P = 0.02) [cite: 47].
    * QoR-15 score at 24 h: TEAS = 54.5 (15.0, 92.0); Control = 59.0 (27.0, 136.0) (P = 0.01) (Note: Results text states TEAS lower at 24h: 50.5 vs 44.5, P=0.04, but Table 3 reports 54.5 vs 59.0) [cite: 47]. 
    * QoR-15 scores at 48 h and 72 h, and sleeping quality (VSS and RCSQ scores) were not significantly different between groups [cite: 47].
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** Incidence of PONV at 24 h (TEAS: 63.83%; Control: 46.81%, P = 0.17), 48 h (TEAS: 21.28%; Control: 19.15%, P = 0.76), and 72 h (TEAS: 17.02%; Control: 8.7%, P = 0.23) showed no significant differences between groups [cite: 47].
* **Length of stay/Expenses:** Post-surgery expense: TEAS = 62,962 ± 28,001 CNY; Control = 84,218 ± 115,027 CNY (P = 0.21) [cite: 47].
* **Other postoperative complications:** Major complications were 0 (0%) in both groups [cite: 47].
