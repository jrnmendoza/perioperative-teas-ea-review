# Data Extraction: 117210.pdf (Ma et al., 2026)

## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** The research received no specific grant from any funding agency [cite: 51].
* **Country:** China [cite: 51].
* **Journal:** Journal of Pain Research [cite: 51].
* **DOI:** 10.2147/JPR.S619125 [cite: 51].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** Chinese Clinical Trial Registry (ChiCTR2200062959) [cite: 51]. Approved by the Medical Ethics Committee of the Second Affiliated Hospital of Harbin Medical University (approval number: KY2022-193) [cite: 51].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Prospective, partially randomized controlled trial [cite: 51].
* **Randomization method:** Patients with PSQI < 6 were assigned to a non-randomized control group (Group C); patients with PSQI ≥ 6 were randomly allocated in a 1:1 ratio to Group S or Group T using a computer-generated random number table [cite: 51].
* **Blinding:** Outcome assessors were blinded to group assignment [cite: 51]. Allocation concealment and participant blinding were not feasible due to the physical nature of the TEAS intervention [cite: 51].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** Group C (PSQI < 6, non-randomized control), Group S (PSQI ≥ 6, sleep disturbance control), and Group T (PSQI ≥ 6, TEAS intervention) [cite: 51].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 72 recruited; 61 stratified by PSQI scores (Group C: n=26; Group S: n=17; Group T: n=18); 59 completed and included in analysis (Group C: n=26; Group S: n=16; Group T: n=17) [cite: 51].
* **Age:** Group C: 54.62 ± 9.41 years; Group S: 59.00 ± 9.216 years; Group T: 55.12 ± 8.558 years [cite: 51].
* **Sex distribution:** Female (breast cancer patients) [cite: 51].
* **BMI:** Group C: 23.37 ± 3.30 kg/m²; Group S: 23.44 ± 2.91 kg/m²; Group T: 22.36 ± 1.92 kg/m² [cite: 51].
* **ASA classification:** ASA physical status I-III [cite: 51].
* **Comorbidities:** Included patients scheduled for elective breast cancer surgery [cite: 51]. Excluded patients with severe cardiovascular, cerebrovascular, hepatic, renal, or hematopoietic diseases; cardiac pacemaker; or history of severe neurological or psychiatric disorders [cite: 51].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Breast cancer surgery [cite: 51].
* **Specific procedures:** Breast-conserving surgery with sentinel lymph node biopsy (BCS + SLNB), simple mastectomy with SLNB, and modified radical mastectomy (MRM) [cite: 51].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 51].
* **Induction:** Intravenous propofol (1.5-2.0 mg/kg), sufentanil (0.3-0.5 μg/kg), and cis-atracurium (0.1 mg/kg) [cite: 51].
* **Maintenance:** Sevoflurane inhalation (1.0-2.0% end-tidal concentration) and continuous intravenous remifentanil infusion (0.1-0.3 μg/kg/min) [cite: 51]. BIS was maintained between 45 and 60 [cite: 51].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Transcutaneous electrical acupoint stimulation (TEAS) [cite: 51].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 17 analyzed (Group T) [cite: 51].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Hegu (LI4) and Shenmen (HT7) on the left upper limb, and Hegu (LI4) and Neiguan (PC6) on the right upper limb [cite: 51].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** 2/100 Hz (dense-disperse mode) [cite: 51].
* **Treatment intensity:** 8-12 mA based on patient tolerance [cite: 51].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** Prior to anesthesia induction [cite: 51].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 30 minutes [cite: 51].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** One single preoperative session [cite: 51].
* **Total treatment period:** Single day (preoperative) [cite: 51].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Flurbiprofen axetil (100 mg) was administered intravenously 30 minutes before the end of surgery and once daily for three consecutive days; prophylactic antiemetics (dexamethasone and granisetron) were administered [cite: 51].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Non-randomized reference control (Group C) and randomized sleep disturbance control (Group S) [cite: 51].
* **Number of participants in control group:** Group C: 26 analyzed; Group S: 16 analyzed [cite: 51].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Patients in Groups C and S did not receive TEAS or sham stimulation [cite: 51].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Flurbiprofen axetil [cite: 51].
* **Dosage parameters:** 100 mg intravenously [cite: 51].
* **Duration:** Administered 30 minutes before the end of surgery and subsequently once daily for three consecutive days [cite: 51].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** 11-point Numerical Rating Scale (NRS) [cite: 51].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Recorded at rest [cite: 51].
* **Pain assessment time points:** 4, 24, 48, and 72 hours after surgery [cite: 51].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group:** Depicted in Figure 3. Group S exhibited significantly higher NRS scores at 24, 48, and 72 hours postoperatively compared with Group C (p < 0.05) [cite: 51].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** TEAS intervention (Group T) significantly reduced NRS pain scores compared with Group S at all postoperative time points (p < 0.05) [cite: 51]. Pain scores in Group T were comparable to those in Group C across all time points [cite: 51].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Sufentanil (induction) and Remifentanil (maintenance) [cite: 51].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous [cite: 51].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention/control groups:** Explicit dosages not detailed beyond the standard protocol ranges [cite: 51].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Not explicitly reported, though intraoperative ephedrine consumption was recorded as an indicator of hemodynamic instability [cite: 51]. Group C: 10.73 ± 6.01 mg; Group S: 16.31 ± 7.26 mg; Group T: 11.29 ± 5.15 mg (p = 0.016) [cite: 51].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **Sleep Quality:** 
    * Athens Insomnia Scale (AIS) scores: On the day of surgery (T1), sleep quality was poor across all groups [cite: 51]. On postoperative days 1 (T2) and 2 (T3), both Group C and Group T demonstrated significantly greater improvements in AIS scores compared with Group S (p < 0.05) [cite: 51].
* **Intraoperative Hemodynamics:** 
    * Standard deviation of systolic blood pressure (sdSBP): Group C = 12.09 ± 3.56; Group S = 18.85 ± 6.74; Group T = 9.31 ± 2.73 (p < 0.001) [cite: 51].
    * Coefficient of variation of systolic blood pressure (cvSBP): Group C = 0.10 ± 0.03; Group S = 0.16 ± 0.05; Group T = 0.08 ± 0.02 (p < 0.001) [cite: 51].
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** Incidence of nausea within 24 hours: Group C = 50%; Group S = 50%; Group T = 35.3% (p = 0.590) [cite: 51]. Incidence of vomiting: Group C = 42.3%; Group S = 43.8%; Group T = 35.3% (p = 0.863) [cite: 51].
* **Length of stay:** Hospital stay (days): Group C = 7.27 ± 2.41; Group S = 8.06 ± 2.29; Group T = 6.82 ± 1.68 (p = 0.266) [cite: 51].
* **Other postoperative complications:** No serious adverse events attributable to TEAS or study procedures were reported [cite: 51].
