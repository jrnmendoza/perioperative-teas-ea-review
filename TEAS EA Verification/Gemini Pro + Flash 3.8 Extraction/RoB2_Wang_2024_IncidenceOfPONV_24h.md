# Cochrane Risk of Bias 2 (RoB 2) Assessment

## Study & Result Information
- **Study Citation:** Wang H, Sun M, Zhang Y, et al. Transcutaneous electrical acupoint stimulation reduces the incidence of postoperative nausea and vomiting in breast cancer patients with a history of severe pregnancy-induced nausea: A randomised controlled trial. Breast Cancer. 2024;31(5):926–934.
- **Source Document in Google Drive:** `covidence_212_full_article.pdf`
- **Trial Registration:** Chinese Clinical Trial Registry ChiCTR2100053752
- **Population:** 140 female breast cancer patients (aged 25–65 years, ASA I–II) with a documented history of severe nausea and vomiting during pregnancy undergoing modified radical mastectomy under general anesthesia.
- **Intervention:** TEAS applied to bilateral Neiguan (PC6) and Zusanli (ST36) 30 min preoperatively and once daily for 30 min on POD 1 (dense-disperse 2/100 Hz, current 10–20 mA; n = 70 analyzed).
- **Comparator:** Sham TEAS with identical appearance electrodes placed at identical acupoints with 0 mA current delivered (n = 70 analyzed).
- **Assessed Outcome:** Cumulative Incidence of Postoperative Nausea and Vomiting (PONV)
- **Assessed Timepoint:** 0 to 24 hours postoperatively
- **Numerical Result:**
  - Incidence of PONV within 24 hours was significantly lower in the TEAS group compared with the Sham group (19/70 [27.1%] vs. 38/70 [54.3%]; relative risk 0.50, 95% CI 0.33–0.77, P = 0.001). Rescue antiemetic requirement was also significantly lower in the TEAS group (11.4% vs. 30.0%, P = 0.006).

### Step 1: Study Design Verification
- **Experimental Design:** Prospective, randomized, double-blind, sham-controlled parallel-group trial (1:1 ratio).
- **Unit of Randomization:** Individual patient.

---

### Step 2: Signaling Questions & Domain Evaluations

#### Domain 1: Risk of bias arising from the randomization process
- **1.1 Random sequence generation:** Yes (Y)
  - **Evidence:** "Random numbers were generated using a computer-based random sequence generated by SPSS software." (p. 927, col. 2).
- **1.2 Allocation concealment:** Yes (Y)
  - **Evidence:** "Allocation was concealed in sequentially numbered, opaque, sealed envelopes opened by a dedicated research nurse." (p. 927, col. 2).
- **1.3 Baseline balance:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** Age, BMI, Apfel PONV risk score, chemotherapy history, anesthesia duration, and intraoperative opioid doses were balanced (P > 0.05; Table 1).
- **Domain 1 Judgment:** **Low Risk of Bias**

#### Domain 2: Risk of bias due to deviations from the intended interventions
- **2.1 Participant awareness:** Probably Yes (PY)
  - **Evidence:** Pre-induction stimulation delivered awake; active tingling (10–20 mA) vs. turned-off sham device (0 mA) creates discernible awake sensation contrast.
- **2.2 Carers awareness:** Probably No (PN)
  - **Evidence:** "Surgeons, anesthesiologists, and ward nursing staff were blinded to group assignments throughout the trial." (p. 928, col. 1).
- **2.3 Contextual deviations:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** Standardized general anesthesia regimen and standardized rescue antiemetic (ondansetron 4 mg) protocol.
- **2.5 Appropriate analysis:** Yes (Y)
  - **Evidence:** 140 randomized (70 TEAS, 70 Sham); all 140 analyzed in assigned groups (100% ITT).
- **Domain 2 Judgment:** **Some concerns Risk of Bias**

#### Domain 3: Risk of bias due to missing outcome data
- **3.1 Data completeness:** Yes (Y)
  - **Evidence:** Complete 24-hour outcome records available for all 140 randomized patients (100% complete follow-up; Fig. 1).
- **Domain 3 Judgment:** **Low Risk of Bias**

#### Domain 4: Risk of bias in measurement of the outcome
- **4.1 Inappropriate measurement:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** Validated PONV scoring criteria (4-point scale: 0 = none, 1 = nausea, 2 = retching, 3 = vomiting).
- **4.2 Differ between groups:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** Identical evaluation timing (0–2, 2–6, 6–24 h postop).
- **4.3 Assessors aware:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** "Outcome assessors were independent and blinded to treatment allocation." (p. 928, col. 1).
- **Domain 4 Judgment:** **Low Risk of Bias**

#### Domain 5: Risk of bias in selection of the reported result
- **5.1 Pre-specified analysis plan:** Yes (Y)
  - **Evidence:** Prospectively registered in Chinese Clinical Trial Registry (ChiCTR2100053752) on November 29, 2021.
- **5.2 Result selected:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** PONV incidence and rescue antiemetic rates reported as prespecified.
- **5.3 Multiple analyses:** No (N)
  - **Evidence:** Standard pre-specified dichotomous event analysis.
- **Domain 5 Judgment:** **Low Risk of Bias**
