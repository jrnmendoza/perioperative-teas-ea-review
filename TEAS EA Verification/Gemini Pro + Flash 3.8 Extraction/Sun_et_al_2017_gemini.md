# Data Extraction: sun2017-2.pdf (Sun et al., 2017)

## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Funded by the Scientific Research Program of Chinese Medicine (2010ZA074) and the Department of Anesthesiology, the second affiliated hospital, Zhejiang University School of Medicine, Hangzhou, China [cite: 39].
* **Country:** China [cite: 39].
* **Journal:** Clinical Journal of Pain [cite: 39].
* **DOI:** 10.1097/AJP.0000000000000400 [cite: 39].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** ClinicalTrials.gov (ChiCTR-IOR-15006032) [cite: 39]. Approved by the Institutional Review Board [cite: 39].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Randomized controlled trial [cite: 39].
* **Randomization method:** A SPSS-generated random number table was used to allocate the patients into 1 of the 4 groups in a 1:1:1:1 ratio [cite: 39].
* **Blinding:** The TEAS-operators were the only individuals aware of the treatment allocation, while patients, anesthesiologists, and evaluators were blinded to the treatment allocation [cite: 39].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** Group SSS (sham TEAS preoperatively, intraoperatively, and postoperatively), group TSS (true TEAS preoperatively, sham intra/post), group TTS (true TEAS preoperatively and intraoperatively, sham post), and group TST (true TEAS preoperatively and postoperatively, sham intra) [cite: 39].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 435 assessed for eligibility; 380 randomized (n=95 each group); 361 analyzed (Group SSS: n=90; Group TSS: n=91; Group TTS: n=91; Group TST: n=89) [cite: 39].
* **Age:** Group SSS: 46.5 ± 13.6 years; Group TSS: 45.9 ± 10.2 years; Group TTS: 42.4 ± 10.4 years; Group TST: 44.8 ± 11.5 years [cite: 39].
* **Sex distribution:** Overall, 82.3% of study participants were female [cite: 39]. Male percentage by group: Group SSS = 20.0%; Group TSS = 14.3%; Group TTS = 16.5%; Group TST = 20.2% [cite: 39].
* **BMI:** Group SSS: 22.7 ± 3.1 kg/m²; Group TSS: 22.8 ± 2.3 kg/m²; Group TTS: 22.6 ± 2.8 kg/m²; Group TST: 23.3 ± 3.2 kg/m² [cite: 39].
* **ASA classification:** ASA class I or II [cite: 39]. Percentage ASA I: Group SSS = 31.1%; Group TSS = 36.3%; Group TTS = 23.1%; Group TST = 32.6% [cite: 39].
* **Comorbidities:** Included patients scheduled for laparoscopic cholecystectomy or laparoscopic gynecological surgery [cite: 39]. Excluded patients with cardiac pacemakers, history of using acupuncture, history of chronic pain/daily use of analgesics, excess/long-term alcohol use, renal/hepatic insufficiency, asthma, GI ulcer, hemorrhage tendency, or pregnancy [cite: 39].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Laparoscopic surgery [cite: 39].
* **Specific procedures:** Laparoscopic cholecystectomy (176 patients, 48.8%) and laparoscopic gynecological surgery (185 patients, 51.2%) [cite: 39].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 39].
* **Induction:** Propofol 2.0 to 4.0 mg/kg, sufentanil 0.5 to 1.0 μg/kg, and rocuronium 0.6 mg/kg [cite: 39].
* **Maintenance:** Adjusted propofol and remifentanil infusion to maintain the bispectral index 40 to 50 [cite: 39].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Transcutaneous electrical acupoint stimulation (TEAS) [cite: 39].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** Group TSS: n=91; Group TTS: n=91; Group TST: n=89 [cite: 39].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Bilateral Hegu (LI4) and Neiguan (P6) [cite: 39].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** Alternating dense and disperse stimulation: 2 Hz (0.6ms pulse width) alternated with 100 Hz (0.2 ms pulse width) automatically every 3 seconds [cite: 39].
* **Treatment intensity:** Maximum current tolerated but subnoxious to the patient [cite: 39].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** Preoperative, intraoperative, and postoperative depending on group allocation [cite: 39].
* **Duration of each treatment:** Each treatment lasted 30 minutes in each perioperative period [cite: 39].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** 
    * Group TSS: 1 session (preoperative) [cite: 39].
    * Group TTS: 2 sessions (preoperative and intraoperative) [cite: 39].
    * Group TST: 2 sessions (preoperative and postoperative) [cite: 39].
* **Total treatment period:** Single day (perioperative) [cite: 39].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Intravenous flurbiprofenaxetil 50 mg at the end of surgery, and tropisetron 5 mg at the end of surgery [cite: 39].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Sham TEAS [cite: 39].
* **Number of participants in control group:** Group SSS (sham pre, intra, and post): 90 analyzed [cite: 39].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Stimulated with the minimal current that the patient could feel, with the same alternating frequency for 5 minutes, and electrodes retained in situ for 30 minutes [cite: 39].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Intravenous flurbiprofenaxetil 50 mg at the end of surgery [cite: 39].
* **Dosage parameters:** Additional flurbiprofenaxetil 50 mg was supplemented if analgesia was inadequate (VAS ≥ 4) or if patients required analgesics within 48 hours [cite: 39].
* **Duration:** Tracked over 48 hours postoperatively [cite: 39].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Visual analog scale (VAS) (0-10) [cite: 39].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Resting and activity (during coughing) pain intensity [cite: 39].
* **Pain assessment time points:** 1, 6, 24, and 48 hours after surgery [cite: 39].
* **Median pain score for each group (median (IQR)):**
    * Resting pain intensity at 1, 6, 24, and 48 hours: No significant differences among the 4 groups (visualized in Figure 2) [cite: 39].
    * Activity pain intensity at 6 h: Group SSS = 5.0 (4.0 to 6.0); Group TSS = 5.0 (5.0 to 6.0); Group TTS = 3.0 (3.0 to 4.0); Group TST = 3.0 (2.5 to 5.0) [cite: 39].
    * Activity pain intensity at 24 h: Group SSS = 4.0 (3.8 to 5.0); Group TSS = 4.0 (4.0 to 5.0); Group TTS = 4.0 (3.0 to 4.0); Group TST = 3.0 (3.0 to 4.0) [cite: 39].
    * Activity pain intensity at 48 h: Group SSS = 3.0 (3.0 to 4.0); Group TSS = 3.0 (3.0 to 4.0); Group TST = 3.0 (2.0 to 3.0) [cite: 39].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** 
    * At 6 hours, activity pain intensity was significantly lower in groups TTS and TST compared with groups SSS and TSS (P < 0.001) [cite: 39].
    * At 24 hours, activity pain intensity significantly decreased in group TST compared with groups SSS and TSS (P < 0.001), and in group TTS compared with group TSS (P < 0.001) [cite: 39].
    * At 48 hours, activity pain intensity was significantly reduced in group TST compared with groups SSS and TSS (P < 0.001) [cite: 39].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Sufentanil (induction) and Remifentanil (maintenance) [cite: 39]. Converted to morphine equivalents (0.01 mg sufentanil = 0.1 mg remifentanil = 10 mg morphine) [cite: 39]. Propofol also tracked [cite: 39].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous [cite: 39].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention groups (mean ± SD):** 
    * Group TSS: Propofol = 10.0 ± 4.8 mg/kg; Morphine = 1.6 ± 0.4 mg/kg [cite: 39].
    * Group TTS: Propofol = 10.5 ± 3.4 mg/kg; Morphine = 1.6 ± 0.4 mg/kg [cite: 39].
    * Group TST: Propofol = 10.0 ± 4.0 mg/kg; Morphine = 1.5 ± 0.4 mg/kg [cite: 39].
* **Opioid consumption for control group (mean ± SD):**
    * Group SSS: Propofol = 9.3 ± 4.4 mg/kg; Morphine = 1.5 ± 0.4 mg/kg [cite: 39].
* **Measurement time point(s):** Intraoperative [cite: 39].
* **Between-group difference:** There was no significant difference in intraoperative propofol (P=0.340) and opioid consumption (P=0.303) among the 4 groups [cite: 39].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Supplemental analgesics required: Group SSS = 23.3%; Group TSS = 11.0%; Group TTS = 15.4%; Group TST = 7.9% [cite: 39].
* **Between-group difference:** The supplemental analgesic requirements were significantly less in group TST compared with group SSS (P = 0.020) [cite: 39].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** Incidence of nausea: Group SSS = 25.6%; Group TSS = 26.4%; Group TTS = 34.1%; Group TST = 34.8% (P = 0.376) [cite: 39]. Incidence of vomiting: Group SSS = 22.2%; Group TSS = 25.3%; Group TTS = 26.4%; Group TST = 29.2% (P = 0.759) [cite: 39].
* **Gastrointestinal recovery:** First postoperative passed flatus (h): Group SSS = 41.5 ± 19.6; Group TSS = 44.7 ± 16.6; Group TTS = 45.2 ± 19.9; Group TST = 41.6 ± 18.1 (P = 0.391) [cite: 39]. First postoperative defecation (h): Group SSS = 63.4 ± 22.9; Group TSS = 63.6 ± 19.8; Group TTS = 61.8 ± 15.7; Group TST = 57.3 ± 20.6 (P = 0.127) [cite: 39].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** Patient satisfaction was significantly higher in groups TSS (100%), TTS (98.9%), and TST (100%) compared with group SSS (88.9%) (P < 0.001) [cite: 39].
* **Length of stay:** Length of stay in PACU (min): Group SSS = 66.0 ± 16.0; Group TSS = 62.2 ± 19.4; Group TTS = 62.7 ± 16.3; Group TST = 64.8 ± 15.5 (P = 0.379) [cite: 39].
* **Other postoperative complications:** No side effect related to TEAS was observed during the postoperative 48 hours [cite: 39].
