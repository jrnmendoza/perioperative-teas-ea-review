# Data Extraction: covidence_912_wong_2006.pdf (Wong et al., 2006)

## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Not explicitly stated [cite: 47].
* **Country:** Hong Kong SAR, China [cite: 47].
* **Journal:** Annals of Thoracic Surgery [cite: 47].
* **DOI:** 10.1016/j.athoracsur.2005.12.064 [cite: 47].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** Not reported [cite: 47]. Study design was approved by the Ethics Committees of the Chinese University of Hong Kong and of the Hospital Authority of Hong Kong [cite: 47].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Prospective, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (pilot study) [cite: 47].
* **Randomization method:** Randomization lists were generated by a computer program and sealed in envelopes containing individual group allocation and consecutive number [cite: 47]. The envelopes were opened and the results disclosed in the immediate postoperative period by the acupuncturist [cite: 47].
* **Blinding:** Double-blind; both the patients and all surgeons in charge of postoperative patient care were blinded to the nature of the acupuncture [cite: 47]. The data was collected by a dedicated assessor who was blinded to the results of randomization [cite: 47].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** Electroacupuncture (EA) group and sham acupuncture (SA) group [cite: 47].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 32 consecutive patients assessed for eligibility; 27 randomized; 25 completed and were analyzed (EA: n=13; SA: n=12) [cite: 47].
* **Age:** EA group: 64.6 ± 8.0 years; SA group: 64.5 ± 8.5 years (mean ± SD) [cite: 47].
* **Sex distribution:** EA group: 9 Male (62.9%), 4 Female (30.8%) [Note: Percentages do not sum to 100%, 9/13 is 69.2%]; SA group: 10 Male (83.3%), 2 Female (16.7%) [cite: 47].
* **BMI:** Not reported [cite: 47].
* **ASA classification:** Not reported [cite: 47].
* **Comorbidities:** Included patients diagnosed to have operable non-small cell lung carcinoma, whose primary tumor was equal or larger than 4 cm in diameter precluding a VATS approach [cite: 47].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Anatomic lung resection through a thoracotomy approach (posterolateral thoracotomy with controlled rib-fracture technique) [cite: 47].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 47].
* **Induction:** Morphine 0.1 mg/kg and fentanyl 2 μg/kg [cite: 47].
* **Maintenance:** Morphine 0.05 mg/(kg·h) [cite: 47].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Electroacupuncture (EA) [cite: 47].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 13 analyzed [cite: 47].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Hegu (LI 4), Yanglingquan (GB 34), Waiqiu (GB 36), and Sanyangluo (TE 8) ipsilateral to the side of the thoracotomy [cite: 47].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** Alternating current at 60 Hz [cite: 47].
* **Treatment intensity:** Adjusted to reach a state of De-Qi (sensation of heaviness, fullness, and numbness) [cite: 47].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** Commenced immediately on return of the patient to the ward after surgery [cite: 47].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 30 minutes per session [cite: 47].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** Two sessions each day for the first 7 postoperative days [cite: 47].
* **Total treatment period:** Up to 7 days postoperatively [cite: 47].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Patient-controlled analgesia (PCA) morphine for the first 3 postoperative days [cite: 47]. Oral analgesics (640 mg acetaminophen and 65 mg dextropropoxyphene) four times per day [cite: 47].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Sham acupuncture (SA) / placebo control [cite: 47].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 12 analyzed [cite: 47].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Used a special blunt-tip needle pressed to the same acupoints, mimicking pinprick sensation without skin piercing, fixed with an opaque fixator to blind the assessor [cite: 47]. Pseudostimulation given by connecting needle to incorrect output socket (no current flow, De-Qi state not reached) [cite: 47]. Same frequency and schedule as the EA group [cite: 47].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Intravenous patient-controlled analgesia (PCA) morphine [cite: 47].
* **Dosage parameters:** 1 mg of morphine per bolus with a lockout time of 5 minutes [cite: 47].
* **Duration:** First 3 postoperative days [cite: 47].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Visual analog scale (VAS) (0-100 mm scale or 0-10 based on table values) [cite: 47].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Not specifically distinguished [cite: 47].
* **Pain assessment time points:** Daily from postoperative day 0 to day 7 [cite: 47].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group (Mean ± SD):**
    * Day 0: EA = 3.7 ± 1.6; SA = 3.0 ± 2.3 (P = 0.42) [cite: 47].
    * Day 1: EA = 3.9 ± 1.3; SA = 3.9 ± 1.4 (P = 0.96) [cite: 47].
    * Day 2: EA = 3.1 ± 1.4; SA = 3.8 ± 1.6 (P = 0.92) [cite: 47].
    * Day 3: EA = 2.9 ± 1.1; SA = 3.8 ± 1.9 (P = 0.15) [cite: 47].
    * Day 4: EA = 3.0 ± 1.5; SA = 4.4 ± 1.9 (P = 0.06) [cite: 47].
    * Day 5: EA = 2.5 ± 1.8; SA = 4.0 ± 1.1 (P = 0.02) [cite: 47].
    * Day 6: EA = 3.2 ± 1.9; SA = 4.8 ± 2.1 (P = 0.15) [cite: 47].
    * Day 7: EA = 3.0 ± 3.6; SA = 3.0 ± 1.4 (P = 1.00) [cite: 47].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** There was a trend for lower VAS pain scores in the EA group between postoperative days 2 and 6, although this did not reach statistical significance except for Day 5 (P = 0.02) [cite: 47].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Morphine [cite: 47].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous via PCA [cite: 47].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** 
    * Day 0: 8.3 ± 4.9 mg [cite: 47].
    * Day 1: 18.0 ± 8.8 mg [cite: 47].
    * Day 2: 7.5 ± 5.0 mg [cite: 47].
    * Total PCA usage: 33.9 ± 12.8 mg [cite: 47].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** 
    * Day 0: 7.8 ± 6.4 mg [cite: 47].
    * Day 1: 18.8 ± 9.8 mg [cite: 47].
    * Day 2: 15.7 ± 12.0 mg [cite: 47].
    * Total PCA usage: 42.3 ± 21.3 mg [cite: 47].
* **Measurement time point(s):** Postoperative days 0, 1, and 2 [cite: 47].
* **Between-group difference:** The cumulative dose of PCA morphine used on postoperative day 2 was significantly lower in the EA group (P = 0.035) [cite: 47]. No significant difference on days 0, 1, or for total PCA usage (P = 0.25) [cite: 47].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** All patients were on PCA morphine [cite: 47].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** Not reported [cite: 47].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** Peak flow rate was tracked daily from Day 0 to Day 7; there was no significant difference between the two groups [cite: 47]. Chest drain duration (days): EA = 4.5 ± 1.8; SA = 5.4 ± 2.1 (P = 0.27) [cite: 47].
* **Length of stay:** Not reported [cite: 47].
* **Other postoperative complications:** Two patients were excluded due to postoperative complications unrelated to acupuncture (one had respiratory failure, one had gastrointestinal bleeding) [cite: 47]. No mortality, complication, or adverse reaction related to acupuncture occurred in both groups [cite: 47].
