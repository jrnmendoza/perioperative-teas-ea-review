# Data Extraction: 103940.pdf (Wu et al., 2025)

## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Supported by the Fundamental Research Funds for the Central Universities (YG2024LC10), Shanghai Hospital Association Hospital Management Research Fund (x2022179), and National Natural Science Foundation of China (82373226) [cite: 49].
* **Country:** China (Shanghai) [cite: 49].
* **Journal:** Journal of Pain Research [cite: 49].
* **DOI:** Not explicitly stated with standard prefix in the text, but the URL https://doi.org/10.3147/JPR.S52193 is present in the document footer [cite: 49].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** Chinese Clinical Trial Registry (No. ChiCTR2200066600) [cite: 49]. Approved by the Institutional Review Board of Shanghai Chest Hospital (IRB #KS (Y) 23025) [cite: 49].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Randomized, sham-controlled trial [cite: 49].
* **Randomization method:** Randomly assigned (1:1) using a computer-generated list of random numbers created by an independent third party [cite: 49].
* **Blinding:** Double-blind; all clinicians and researchers collecting or analyzing the data were blinded to the group assignments [cite: 49]. The sham TEAS group received electrode patches without electrical stimulation to simulate the sensory experience [cite: 49].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** TEAS group and Control (placebo/sham) group [cite: 49].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 132 assessed for eligibility; 100 randomized; 97 finished the trial; 100 included in the intention-to-treat (ITT) analysis (TEAS group: n=50; Control group: n=50) [cite: 49].
* **Age:** TEAS group: 49.3 (11.5) years; Control group: 50.1 (11.4) years (mean (SD)) [cite: 49].
* **Sex distribution:** TEAS group: 35 Female (70.0%); Control group: 35 Female (70.0%) [cite: 49].
* **BMI:** TEAS group: 23.8 (3.1) kg/m²; Control group: 22.9 (3.2) kg/m² [cite: 49].
* **ASA classification:** ASA grade I or II [cite: 49]. TEAS group: 30 ASA I / 20 ASA II; Control group: 26 ASA I / 24 ASA II [cite: 49].
* **Comorbidities:** Included patients undergoing day lung cancer surgery with normal liver and kidney functions [cite: 49]. Excluded patients with previous surgical intervention/scarring along the meridian, upper/lower limb nerve damage, long history of sedation, use of analgesics/antidepressants, history of chronic incision pain, or pregnancy/breastfeeding [cite: 49].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Day lung cancer surgery (ambulatory video-assisted thoracoscopic pulmonary wedge resection) [cite: 49].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 49].
* **Induction:** Sufentanil, propofol, and rocuronium bromide [cite: 49].
* **Maintenance:** Propofol, remifentanil, rocuronium bromide, and dexmedetomidine continuously administered [cite: 49]. BIS was maintained between 40-60 [cite: 49].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Transcutaneous Electrical Acupoint Stimulation (TEAS) [cite: 49].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 50 analyzed (ITT) [cite: 49].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Bilateral Hegu (LI4) and Neiguan (PC6) [cite: 49].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** Frequencies of 2 Hz and 10 Hz [cite: 49].
* **Treatment intensity:** 12-20 mA [cite: 49].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** Administered immediately upon arrival in the Post-Anesthesia Care Unit (PACU), while the patients were still under residual anesthesia [cite: 49].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 30 minutes [cite: 49].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** One single postoperative session [cite: 49].
* **Total treatment period:** Single day (postoperative) [cite: 49].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Ultrasound-guided thoracic paravertebral nerve blocks (TPVB) at T4-7 levels with 150 mg ropivacaine [cite: 49]. 200 mg of sugammadex sodium administered postoperatively [cite: 49].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Sham TEAS / Placebo [cite: 49].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 50 analyzed (ITT) [cite: 49].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Electrode patches were affixed to the same acupoints (LI4 and PC6) without electrical stimulation for 30 minutes [cite: 49].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** No additional postoperative analgesics were administered unless the postoperative VAS score exceeded 4 [cite: 49]. Rescue included paracetamol and oxycodone with acetaminophen tablets or intravenous parecoxib sodium (40 mg) [cite: 49].
* **Dosage parameters:** 40 mg parecoxib sodium (i.v.); compound paracetamol / oxycodone with acetaminophen (P.O.) [cite: 49].
* **Duration:** Tracked up to 24 hours postoperatively [cite: 49].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Visual analog scale (VAS) (0-10) [cite: 49].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Not specifically distinguished [cite: 49].
* **Pain assessment time points:** 1 h, 12 h, and 24 h post-surgery [cite: 49].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group (Mean (SD)):**
    * At 1 h: TEAS = 0.9 (1.2); Control = 1.8 (1.6) (P = 0.006) [cite: 49].
    * At 12 h: TEAS = 2.0 (1.1); Control = 5.4 (1.6) (P < 0.001) [cite: 49].
    * At 24 h (Primary Outcome): TEAS = 1.7 (0.9); Control = 4.0 (1.4) (P < 0.001) [cite: 49].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** The TEAS group had significantly lower VAS scores at 1, 12, and 24 hours post-surgery [cite: 49]. The incidence of moderate-to-severe pain (VAS score >4) within 12 hours was significantly reduced in the TEAS group (12.0% vs 74.0%, P < 0.001) [cite: 49].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Sufentanil (induction) and Remifentanil (maintenance) [cite: 49].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous [cite: 49].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** Sufentanil (i.v.) = 24.2 (9.5) μg; Remifentanil (i.v.) = 489.4 (226.0) μg [cite: 49].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** Sufentanil (i.v.) = 24.3 (10.9) μg; Remifentanil (i.v.) = 533.8 (263.6) μg [cite: 49].
* **Measurement time point(s):** Intraoperative [cite: 49].
* **Between-group difference:** There were no significant differences in intraoperative anesthetic consumption (sufentanil, remifentanil, propofol, dexmedetomidine, rocuronium) between the groups [cite: 49].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** 
    * Compound paracetamol (P.O.): TEAS = 0.3 (0.5) tablets; Control = 0.8 (0.9) tablets (P = 0.001) [cite: 49].
    * Oxycodone with acetaminophen (P.O.): TEAS = 0.3 (0.5) tablets; Control = 0.3 (0.6) tablets (P = 0.864) [cite: 49].
    * Parecoxib sodium (i.v.): TEAS = 4.8 (13.1) mg; Control = 11.2 (18.1) mg (P = 0.046) [cite: 49].
* **Between-group difference:** Analgesic consumption (compound paracetamol and parecoxib sodium) was significantly lower in the TEAS group [cite: 49].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** Frequency of vomiting within 1 h (TEAS: 0%, Control: 2.0%), 1-12 h (TEAS: 30.0%, Control: 42.0%), and 12-24 h (TEAS: 4.0%, Control: 8.0%) showed no significant differences between the two groups [cite: 49]. Metoclopramide use was equal (10.0% in both) [cite: 49].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** The QoR-15 total score at 24 h was significantly higher in the TEAS group (130.8 (9.0)) than in the control group (115.9 (12.2)) (P < 0.001) [cite: 49]. Sleep quality, postoperative activity, and psychological assessment scores on the QoR-15 were also significantly better in the TEAS group (P < 0.001) [cite: 49].
* **Length of stay:** The length of hospital stay (LOS) showed no significant differences, with 80% of patients in both groups staying 36-48 hours [cite: 49].
* **Hospitalization costs:** Expense of hospitalization was not significantly different: TEAS = 44,978 CNY; Control = 45,885 CNY (P = 0.637) [cite: 49].
* **Other postoperative complications:** The most common adverse event besides nausea and vomiting was dizziness, with comparable incidence [cite: 49]. No serious adverse events or adverse skin reactions to TEAS were observed [cite: 49].
