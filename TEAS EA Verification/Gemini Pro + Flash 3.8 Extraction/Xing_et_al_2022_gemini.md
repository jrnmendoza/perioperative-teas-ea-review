# Data Extraction: s40122-022-00429-2.pdf (Xing et al., 2022)

## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** No funding or sponsorship was received for this study or publication of this article [cite: 39].
* **Country:** China (Hefei) [cite: 39].
* **Journal:** Pain Therapy [cite: 39].
* **DOI:** 10.1007/s40122-022-00429-2 [cite: 39].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** Chinese Clinical Trial Registry (ChiCTR2100042119) [cite: 39]. The trial protocol was approved by the ethics committee of the First Affiliated Hospital of USTC (approval no. KY2019-108) [cite: 39].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Double-blind randomized controlled trial [cite: 39].
* **Randomization method:** Computer-generated random numbers were used to randomize patients with an allocation ratio of 1:1:1 [cite: 39]. Randomization codes were kept in a sealed envelope and relayed to an independent nurse [cite: 39].
* **Blinding:** Double-blind [cite: 39]. The allocation was blinded for all patients, surgeons, the leading anesthesiologist, physician in the post-anesthesia care unit, and follow-up observers [cite: 39]. The TEAS stimulator was obscured by an opaque cloth throughout the experiment [cite: 39]. 
* **Group allocation / study groups:** General anesthesia group (Group G), transversus abdominis plane block group (Group NG), and TEAS combined with TAPB group (Group NTG) [cite: 39].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 90 patients randomized; 87 patients analyzed (Group G: n=29; Group NG: n=29; Group NTG: n=29) [cite: 39].
* **Age:** Group G: 70.41 ± 5.62 years; Group NG: 69.45 ± 5.89 years; Group NTG: 68.48 ± 5.37 years (mean ± SD) [cite: 39].
* **Sex distribution:** Group G: 20 Male / 9 Female; Group NG: 22 Male / 7 Female; Group NTG: 22 Male / 7 Female [cite: 39].
* **BMI:** Group G: 22.71 ± 3.03 kg/m²; Group NG: 23.05 ± 2.48 kg/m²; Group NTG: 22.99 ± 1.81 kg/m² [cite: 39].
* **ASA classification:** ASA grades I-III [cite: 39]. Group G (II/III): 7/22; Group NG (II/III): 5/24; Group NTG (II/III): 6/23 [cite: 39].
* **Comorbidities:** Included patients aged ≥ 60 years undergoing elective laparoscopic gastric cancer surgery [cite: 39]. Excluded patients with central nervous system and mental diseases, history of allergic reaction to local anesthetics, and swelling, infection, or ulceration at the site of TEAS [cite: 39].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Laparoscopic gastric cancer surgery [cite: 39].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 39].
* **Induction:** Midazolam (0.04 mg/kg), etomidate (0.3 mg/kg), sufentanil (0.3 μg/kg), and rocuronium (1 mg/kg) administered intravenously [cite: 39].
* **Maintenance:** Target-controlled infusion of propofol and remifentanil [cite: 39]. The concentrations were adjusted to hemodynamic index and BIS 40-60 [cite: 39].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Transcutaneous electrical acupoint stimulation (TEAS) combined with Transversus Abdominis Plane Block (TAPB) [cite: 39].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 29 analyzed (Group NTG) [cite: 39].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Bilateral Hegu (LI4), Neiguan (PC6), and Zusanli (ST36) [cite: 39].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** 2/100 Hz density wave [cite: 39].
* **Treatment intensity:** Optimal intensity was set to mild twitching of the surrounding muscle up to the maximum tolerated by the individual [cite: 39].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** 30 minutes before anesthesia until the end of the operation [cite: 39].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 30 minutes preoperative plus the duration of surgery [cite: 39].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** One single continuous session [cite: 39].
* **Total treatment period:** Single day (perioperative) [cite: 39].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Bilateral TAPB using 40 mL of 0.375% ropivacaine (20 mL per side) injected into the fascial plane between the internal oblique and transversus abdominis muscles [cite: 39]. Prophylactic flurbiprofen axetil (50 mg) and azasetron (10 mg) were administered for postoperative pain and PONV [cite: 39]. Patient-controlled intravenous analgesia (PCIA) was used postoperatively [cite: 39].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Sham TEAS and Sham TAPB (Group G); Sham TEAS and True TAPB (Group NG) [cite: 39].
* **Number of participants in control group:** Group G: 29 analyzed; Group NG: 29 analyzed [cite: 39].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** 
    * Group G received sham TEAS (electrode pads pasted but without actual electric stimuli) and sham TAPB (an equal volume of saline injected in the same way) [cite: 39].
    * Group NG received sham TEAS (electrode pads pasted but without actual electric stimuli) and true TAPB (40 mL of 0.375% ropivacaine) [cite: 39].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Patient-controlled intravenous analgesia (PCIA) [cite: 39].
* **Dosage parameters:** PCIA contained 100 mL of 0.5 μg/mL sufentanil [cite: 39]. Settings: single-dose 2 mL, lock time 15 min, no background continuous infusion [cite: 39].
* **Duration:** Administered for 2 days postoperatively [cite: 39].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Visual analog scale (VAS) [cite: 39].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Recorded at rest and movement [cite: 39].
* **Pain assessment time points:** 4, 12, 24, and 48 h after the operation [cite: 39].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group (Mean ± SD):**
    * VAS scores at rest (4h / 12h / 24h / 48h): 
        * Group G = 3.44 ± 0.62 / 2.68 ± 0.55 / 1.83 ± 0.55 / 1.24 ± 0.40 [cite: 39].
        * Group NG = 2.90 ± 0.82 / 1.92 ± 0.67 / 1.40 ± 0.52 / 1.10 ± 0.49 [cite: 39].
        * Group NTG = 2.62 ± 0.73 / 1.73 ± 0.60 / 1.18 ± 0.42 / 0.72 ± 0.36 [cite: 39].
    * VAS scores at movement (4h / 12h / 24h / 48h):
        * Group G = 4.42 ± 0.67 / 3.60 ± 0.57 / 2.84 ± 0.56 / 2.03 ± 0.52 [cite: 39].
        * Group NG = 3.68 ± 0.73 / 2.85 ± 0.53 / 2.15 ± 0.50 / 1.74 ± 0.56 [cite: 39].
        * Group NTG = 3.50 ± 0.64 / 2.67 ± 0.55 / 1.89 ± 0.52 / 1.19 ± 0.42 [cite: 39].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** The VAS scores at rest and movement were significantly lower in the NG and NTG groups at 4, 12, and 24 h after surgery than in the G group (P < 0.05) [cite: 39]. The VAS scores at movement were significantly lower in the NTG group at 48 h after surgery than in the NG group (P < 0.05) [cite: 39].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Remifentanil (intraoperative), Sufentanil (postoperative PCIA) [cite: 39].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous [cite: 39].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** Intraoperative remifentanil usage = 1182.61 ± 253.61 μg (Group NTG) [cite: 39].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** Intraoperative remifentanil usage = 1619.13 ± 328.98 μg (Group G); 1415.41 ± 295.67 μg (Group NG) [cite: 39].
* **Measurement time point(s):** Intraoperative [cite: 39].
* **Between-group difference:** Remifentanil consumption was significantly lower in the NTG group than in the NG and G groups (P < 0.05) [cite: 39]. Propofol consumption did not show a significant difference among the three groups [cite: 39].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** PCIA pump press numbers (Mean ± SD): Group G = 12.45 ± 4.21; Group NG = 9.97 ± 3.80; Group NTG = 6.55 ± 3.19 [cite: 39].
* **Between-group difference:** PCIA press numbers were significantly lower in the NG and NTG groups than in the G group (P < 0.05), and significantly lower in the NTG group than in the NG group (P < 0.05) [cite: 39].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** PONV [n (%)]: Group G = 13 (44.82%); Group NG = 11 (37.93%); Group NTG = 5 (17.24%) [cite: 39]. The frequency of PONV was significantly lower in the NTG group compared with the G group (P < 0.05) [cite: 39].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** 
    * Quality of Recovery (QoR-15) scores on POD 3 and POD 7 were significantly higher in the NTG group than in the G and NG groups (P < 0.05) [cite: 39]. 
    * Extubation time (min): Group G = 17.72 ± 4.67; Group NG = 16.10 ± 3.39; Group NTG = 9.45 ± 3.98 (P < 0.05 for NTG vs G and NG) [cite: 39].
    * Postoperative time to first ambulation (h): Group G = 31.81 ± 7.38; Group NG = 28.50 ± 5.38; Group NTG = 27.64 ± 5.86 [cite: 39].
    * Postoperative time to first flatus (h): Group G = 43.03 ± 8.13; Group NG = 36.83 ± 6.19; Group NTG = 32.57 ± 6.94 (P < 0.05 for NTG vs NG) [cite: 39].
* **Length of stay:** Not reported [cite: 39].
* **Other relevant physiological/biochemical outcomes (Biomarkers):** 
    * T lymphocyte subsets: On POD 1 and POD 3, the percentages of CD3+ and CD4+ T cells and the CD4+/CD8+ ratio were higher and the percentage of CD8+ T cells was lower in the NTG group than in the G and NG groups (P < 0.05) [cite: 39].
    * Hemodynamics: MAPs and HRs at immediately after extubation (T5) were significantly lower in the NTG group than in the NG group (P < 0.05) [cite: 39].
