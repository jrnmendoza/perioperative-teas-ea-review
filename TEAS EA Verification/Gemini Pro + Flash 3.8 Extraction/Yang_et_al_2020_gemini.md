# Data Extraction: covidence_464_verified.pdf (Yang et al., 2020)

## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Supported by a research grant from the Affiliated Hospital of Nanjing University of Chinese Medicine (Y18021) [cite: 39].
* **Country:** China (Nanjing) [cite: 39].
* **Journal:** Medical Science Monitor [cite: 39].
* **DOI:** 10.12659/MSM.920648 [cite: 39].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** Chinese Clinical Trial Registry (ChiCTR1800014461) [cite: 39]. Approved by the Ethics Committee of the Affiliated Hospital of Nanjing University of TCM [cite: 39].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Single-center, randomized, open-label trial (Feasibility study) [cite: 39].
* **Randomization method:** Group allocation was concealed using a sealed envelope containing the allocation sequence generated by SPSS in a 1:1 ratio [cite: 39].
* **Blinding:** Open-label for participants and the acupuncture provider [cite: 39]. Assessors, anesthetists, and statisticians were blinded to study-group assignments [cite: 39].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** Electroacupuncture (EA) group (EA treatments plus usual care) and usual care (UC) group [cite: 39].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 60 approached and screened; 59 enrolled and randomized (EA group: n=30; UC group: n=29); 57 completed the study (EA group: n=29; UC group: n=28) [cite: 39].
* **Age:** EA group: 58.93 ± 11.77 years; UC group: 56.39 ± 9.34 years (mean ± SD) [cite: 39].
* **Sex distribution:** EA group: 13 Male (44.83%) / 16 Female (55.17%); UC group: 18 Male (64.29%) / 10 Female (35.71%) [cite: 39].
* **BMI:** Weight: EA group = 62.14 ± 7.67 kg; UC group = 65.5 ± 7.74 kg [cite: 39]. BMI not explicitly reported [cite: 39].
* **ASA classification:** ASA class I-III physical status [cite: 39].
* **Comorbidities:** Included patients diagnosed with a benign nodule or tumor without metastasis scheduled for lobectomy or segmentectomy by VATS [cite: 39]. Excluded poor pulmonary function, pre-existing severe cardiovascular/liver/kidney diseases, cognitive/psychological disorders, history of PONV, opioid addiction, cardiac pacemaker, gastrointestinal disorders, or previous history of EA use [cite: 39].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Thoracoscopic surgery (video-assisted thoracoscopic (VATS) segmentectomy/lobectomy) [cite: 39].
* **Specific procedures:** Lobectomy: 16 (55.17%) in EA group, 14 (50.00%) in UC group. Segmentectomy: 13 (44.83%) in EA group, 14 (50%) in UC group [cite: 39].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 39].
* **Induction:** Intravenous midazolam 0.5 mg/kg, 2% lidocaine 1 mg/kg, propofol 2 mg/kg, dexamethasone 5 mg, and rocuronium 0.6 mg/kg [cite: 39].
* **Maintenance:** 1-2% sevoflurane in oxygen [cite: 39].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Electroacupuncture (EA) [cite: 39].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 29 completed [cite: 39].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Bilateral PC6 (Neiguan), ST36 (Zusanli, positive electrode), and ST37 (Shangjuxu) [cite: 39].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** 15 Hz [cite: 39].
* **Treatment intensity:** 3-5 mA current (adjusted until the patient reported maximum comfort level) [cite: 39]. Needles manipulated until a de qi sensation was felt [cite: 39].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** 24 hours before surgery, and 4 hours and 24 hours after surgery [cite: 39].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 30 minutes [cite: 39].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** 3 sessions in total [cite: 39].
* **Total treatment period:** Up to 24 hours postoperatively [cite: 39].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Thoracic paravertebral block (PVB) between T4 and T5 with ultrasound guidance using 20 mL 0.375% ropivacaine [cite: 39]. Intravenous ondansetron 0.1 mg/kg at the end of surgery [cite: 39]. Intravenous PCA containing morphine, ketamine, and ondansetron [cite: 39]. Routine oral celecoxib 200 mg every 12 hours [cite: 39].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Usual care (UC) alone [cite: 39].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 28 completed [cite: 39].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Received standard care without EA [cite: 39]. Received same anesthesia, PVB, PCA, and oral analgesics [cite: 39].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Intravenous patient-controlled analgesia (PCA) and routine oral celecoxib [cite: 39].
* **Dosage parameters:** PCA: morphine 1 mg/kg, ketamine 1 mg/kg, and ondansetron 10 mg diluted with normal saline to 100 mL. 2 mL/hour background infusion, 1 mL on-demand bolus, 10-minute lockout time [cite: 39]. Oral celecoxib: 200 mg every 12 hours [cite: 39].
* **Duration:** Used postoperatively [cite: 39].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Visual Analogue Scale (VAS) (0-10) [cite: 39].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Static Pain VAS and Dynamic Pain VAS [cite: 39].
* **Pain assessment time points:** 24, 48, and 72 hours after surgery (reported in tables as 0-24 h, 24-48 h, and 48-72 h) [cite: 39].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group (median (quartile range)):**
    * Static Pain VAS (0-24h): EA = 2 (2-2); UC = 2 (2-2) [cite: 39].
    * Dynamic Pain VAS (0-24h): EA = 3 (3-3); UC = 3 (3-3) [cite: 39].
    * Static pain VAS (24-48h): EA = 2 (2-3); UC = 2 (2-3) [cite: 39].
    * Dynamic pain VAS (24-48h): EA = 3 (3-3); UC = 3 (3-3) [cite: 39].
    * Static pain VAS (48-72h): EA = 3 (2-3); UC = 3 (2-3) [cite: 39].
    * Dynamic pain VAS (48-72h): EA = 3 (3-3); UC = 3 (3-3) [cite: 39].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** Pain intensity was similar in both groups at the recorded time periods, with no significant differences [cite: 39].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Morphine (via PCA) [cite: 39].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous [cite: 39].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** Not reported separately [cite: 39].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** Not reported separately [cite: 39].
* **Measurement time point(s):** N/A.
* **Between-group difference:** Not reported.

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Additional intramuscular injection of bucinnazine 100 mg was administered if VAS exceeded 3/10 [cite: 39]. Actual rescue doses required were not reported [cite: 39].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **Gastrointestinal recovery (Primary Outcome):** 
    * Time to first flatus (h): EA = 20.8 ± 4.6; UC = 24.1 ± 6.2 (P = 0.026) [cite: 39].
    * Time to first defecation (h): EA = 53.9 ± 6.0; UC = 57.5 ± 7.2 (P = 0.046) [cite: 39].
    * Incidence of abdominal distension (AD): EA = 9 (31.03%); UC = 11 (39.28%) (P = 0.514) [cite: 39].
    * The EA group showed significantly improved outcomes compared to the UC group in terms of time to first flatus and defecation [cite: 39].
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** Incidence of nausea (0-24h): EA = 8 (27.59%); UC = 8 (28.57%) (P = 0.934) [cite: 39]. Incidence of vomiting (0-24h): EA = 5 (17.24%); UC = 7 (25.00%) (P = 0.473) [cite: 39]. PONV was similar in both groups [cite: 39]. Rescue medication given: EA = 5 (17.24%); UC = 7 (25.00%) (P = 0.473) [cite: 39].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** Acceptability questionnaires showed significant improvement in participants' acceptance of EA for alleviating postoperative gastrointestinal discomfort (P = 0.001) [cite: 39]. Time to out-of-bed activity (h): EA = 11.2 ± 1.0; UC = 13 ± 3.5 (P = 0.5) [cite: 39].
* **Length of stay:** Hospital stay (days): EA = 6.1 ± 2.15; UC = 6.5 ± 2.05 (P = 0.442) [cite: 39].
* **Other postoperative complications:** There were no serious EA-related adverse events. The most commonly reported adverse events were hematoma, sleeplessness, and mild or transient sharp pain [cite: 39].
