# Data Extraction: 015_PainATHM-2.pdf (Yeh et al., 2010)

## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Not explicitly stated [cite: 45].
* **Country:** Taiwan [cite: 45].
* **Journal:** Alternative Therapies in Health and Medicine [cite: 45].
* **DOI:** Not provided [cite: 45].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** Ethical approval was obtained from the institutional review board of the study hospital [cite: 45].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Randomized, controlled, repeated measures research design [cite: 45].
* **Randomization method:** Patients were randomly assigned to one of three groups [cite: 45].
* **Blinding:** Single-blind [cite: 45]. Only one of the researchers knew the randomization result [cite: 45]. Patients and their nurses and physicians were not informed of the group allocation [cite: 45].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** Experimental group 1 (EG1) received true acupoint intervention via electrical stimulation, experimental group 2 (EG2) received the sham acupoint manually, and the control group (CG) received no acupoint stimulation [cite: 45].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 99 participants met the criteria; 5 did not complete; 94 analyzed (EG1: n=33; EG2: n=30; CG: n=31) [cite: 45].
* **Age:** EG1: 60.7 ± 12.0 years; EG2: 57.2 ± 15.7 years; CG: 63.2 ± 14.0 years (mean ± SD) [cite: 45].
* **Sex distribution:** EG1: 11 Male / 22 Female; EG2: 9 Male / 21 Female; CG: 16 Male / 15 Female [cite: 45].
* **BMI:** Body weight and height provided (Height (cm): EG1 = 158.1 ± 7.7, EG2 = 157.1 ± 9.9, CG = 160.5 ± 9.0; Weight (kg): EG1 = 65.3 ± 10.1, EG2 = 63.9 ± 13.6, CG = 64.7 ± 13.1) [cite: 45].
* **ASA classification:** American Society of Anesthesiologists grades I to II [cite: 45].
* **Comorbidities:** Included patients scheduled for surgery to correct nontraumatic lumbar spine injuries [cite: 45]. Excluded patients with a pacemaker; a history of epilepsy or opiate dependence; and skin infection, damage, or hemorrhage at the site of acupoint application [cite: 45].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Lumbar spinal surgery [cite: 45].
* **Specific procedures:** Lumbar discectomy, Spinal decompression, Spongy bone transplantation, Lumbar laminectomy [cite: 45].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 45].
* **Induction:** Not explicitly detailed [cite: 45].
* **Maintenance:** Not explicitly detailed [cite: 45].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Acupoint electrical stimulation (AES) [cite: 45].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 33 analyzed (EG1) [cite: 45].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Bilateral Weizhong (BL40), Yanglingquan (GB34), Shenmen (HT7), and Neiguan (P6) [cite: 45].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** Frequency of 100 Hz, a brush frequency of 2 Hz (dense-dispersed waveform), and a pulse duration of 0.25 milliseconds [cite: 45].
* **Treatment intensity:** Titrated for each individual with an intensity in the range of 4 mA to 7 mA depending on body weight [cite: 45].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** 1 hour before surgery, and 1 hour and 2 hours after the patient returned from the operating room [cite: 45].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 20 minutes [cite: 45].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** Three times on the first operative day [cite: 45].
* **Total treatment period:** Single day (perioperative) [cite: 45].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Epidural PCA with a 1-mg bolus of morphine and a 10-minute lockout interval without basal infusion [cite: 45].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Sham acupoint manual stimulation (EG2) and Control group (CG - no acupoint stimulation) [cite: 45].
* **Number of participants in control group:** EG2: 30 analyzed; CG: 31 analyzed [cite: 45].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** 
    * EG2 received the same procedure but at sham acupoints that were 2 cm apart from a true acupoint and not on any meridian, applied manually [cite: 45]. Note: "received the sham acupoint manually" implies no electrical stimulation was applied [cite: 45].
    * CG received no acupoint stimulation [cite: 45].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Epidural PCA [cite: 45].
* **Dosage parameters:** 1-mg bolus of morphine and a 10-minute lockout interval without basal infusion [cite: 45].
* **Duration:** Tracked during the first postoperative 24 hours [cite: 45].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Visual analogue scale (VAS) (0-10) and Brief Pain Inventory (BPI) [cite: 45].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Not specifically distinguished [cite: 45].
* **Pain assessment time points:** 1 hour before surgery, 1 hour after surgery, and 2 hours after surgery [cite: 45].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group (Mean):**
    * Before surgery: EG1 = 1.5; EG2 = 1.3; CG = 0.9 [cite: 45].
    * After surgery 1 (1h): EG1 = 2.7; EG2 = 4.4; CG = 4.3 [cite: 45].
    * After surgery 2 (2h): EG1 = 1.6; EG2 = 3.1; CG = 3.5 [cite: 45].
    * Worst postoperative pain (24h): EG1 = 5.3 ± 1.5; EG2 = 6.5 ± 1.6; CG = 6.5 ± 1.6 [cite: 45].
    * Average postoperative pain (24h): EG1 = 2.1 ± 1.2; EG2 = 3.0 ± 1.4; CG = 3.1 ± 1.5 [cite: 45].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** Significant differences were observed in pain intensity (P < .001) and average pain (P = .01) among the groups [cite: 45]. Pain intensity was significantly lower in EG1 compared to EG2 and CG (P = .02 and P = .01 respectively) [cite: 45].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Morphine [cite: 45].
* **Route of administration:** Epidural via PCA [cite: 45].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** Dose of opiate used in 24 h = 18.6 ± 9.7 mg (EG1) [cite: 45].
* **Opioid consumption for control groups:** Dose of opiate used in 24 h = 21.6 ± 13.1 mg (EG2); 27.2 ± 12.5 mg (CG) [cite: 45].
* **Measurement time point(s):** First 24 postoperative hours [cite: 45].
* **Between-group difference:** The total dose was significantly different among the 3 groups (F=4.28, P=.02) and between EG1 and CG (P=.02) [cite: 45].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Occurrence of PCA button pushed (times): EG1 = 25.3 ± 28.5; EG2 = 47.4 ± 39.7; CG = 74.7 ± 85.5 [cite: 45].
* **Between-group difference:** The number of demands was significantly different among the 3 groups (P < .001) and significantly lower in EG1 vs CG [cite: 45]. Initial demand launched (min) was not significantly different among the groups (P = .88) [cite: 45].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** Measured using the Rhodes Index of Nausea and Vomiting (RINV) [cite: 45]. There was no significant difference overall (F=0.97, P=.33) or in any item of the PONV among the groups (P > .05) [cite: 45].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** The impact of postoperative pain on daily activities was significantly lower in EG1 compared to CG (F = 5.74, P < .001) [cite: 45]. Significant differences were found in respiratory rate and blood pressure among the groups, with better stability in EG1 compared to CG and EG2 (P < .001) [cite: 45]. Heart rates were similar among the groups [cite: 45].
* **Length of stay:** Not reported [cite: 45].
* **Other postoperative complications:** Not explicitly reported, beyond PONV and vital sign stability [cite: 45].
