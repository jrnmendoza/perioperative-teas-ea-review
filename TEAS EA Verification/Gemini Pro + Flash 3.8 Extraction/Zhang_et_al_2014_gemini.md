# Data Extraction: 043_zhang_2014.pdf (Zhang et al., 2014)

## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China (grant no. 81072888), the Major Program of National Natural Science Foundation of China (grant no. 30930091) and the Program for Changjiang Scholars and Innovative Research Team in University (grant no. IRT1053) [cite: 49].
* **Country:** China (Xi'an, Shaanxi) [cite: 49].
* **Journal:** Anaesthesia [cite: 49].
* **DOI:** 10.1111/anae.12639 [cite: 49].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** NCT01700855 at clinicaltrials.gov [cite: 49]. Approved by the Xijing Hospital Research Ethics Committee [cite: 49].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Prospective, randomised, placebo-controlled clinical trial [cite: 49].
* **Randomization method:** Assigned using a randomisation sequence based on a table of randomly generated numbers [cite: 49].
* **Blinding:** Double-blinded [cite: 49]. Patients were not informed of the allocation, and placebo gel electrodes were applied to the same acupressure points in the sham group to ensure blinding [cite: 49]. Both the randomisation and allocation lists were concealed from the anaesthetists and the persons who performed the final statistical analysis [cite: 49].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** TEAS group and Sham group [cite: 49].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 72 assessed for eligibility; 65 randomly assigned and completed the study to be analyzed (TEAS group: n=33; Sham group: n=32) [cite: 49].
* **Age:** TEAS group: 35.0 (7.6) years; Sham group: 34.3 (9.1) years (mean (SD)) [cite: 49].
* **Sex distribution:** 100% Female (women scheduled for elective cosmetic breast surgery) [cite: 49].
* **BMI:** TEAS group: 22.5 (1.2) kg/m²; Sham group: 22.2 (1.1) kg/m² [cite: 49].
* **ASA classification:** ASA physical status 1 or 2 [cite: 49].
* **Comorbidities:** Included patients scheduled for ambulatory elective cosmetic breast surgery [cite: 49]. Excluded patients with heart, lung, liver, kidney or endocrine system disease; mental disorder, sore throat; obesity; potentially difficult airway; or previous experience with acupuncture treatment [cite: 49].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Ambulatory cosmetic breast surgery [cite: 49].
* **Specific procedures:** Breast augmentation (TEAS: 11, Sham: 9); Reduction mammoplasty (TEAS: 12, Sham: 10); Ogilvy pull out (TEAS: 7, Sham: 9); Other (TEAS: 3, Sham: 4) [cite: 49].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anaesthesia [cite: 49].
* **Induction:** Midazolam 0.03 mg/kg, fentanyl 3.0 μg/kg, and propofol 2.0 mg/kg [cite: 49]. Rocuronium 0.5 mg/kg given to facilitate laryngeal mask airway (LMA) insertion [cite: 49].
* **Maintenance:** Target-controlled infusions of propofol and remifentanil, adjusted according to haemodynamics and bispectral index (BIS) [cite: 49].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Transcutaneous electric acupoint stimulation (TEAS) [cite: 49].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 33 analyzed [cite: 49].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Bilateral Hegu (LI4), Neiguan (PC6), and Zusanli (ST36) [cite: 49].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** Dense-disperse frequency of 2/10 Hz [cite: 49].
* **Treatment intensity:** 6-9 mA (optimal intensity adjusted to maintain a slight twitching of the regional muscle according to individual maximum tolerance) [cite: 49].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** 30 minutes before the induction of anaesthesia in the operating theatre [cite: 49].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 30 minutes [cite: 49].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** One single preoperative session [cite: 49].
* **Total treatment period:** Single day (preoperative) [cite: 49].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Parecoxib sodium (40 mg) and tropisetron hydrochloride (4 mg) were given 5 min before the end of surgery for prophylaxis of postoperative pain and nausea/vomiting [cite: 49].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Sham control [cite: 49].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 32 analyzed [cite: 49].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Placebo gel electrodes were applied to the same acupressure points; patients were connected to the instrument, but electronic stimulation was not applied [cite: 49].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Parecoxib sodium [cite: 49].
* **Dosage parameters:** Parecoxib sodium (40 mg) given 5 min before the end of surgery [cite: 49].
* **Duration:** Intraoperative administration for postoperative prophylaxis [cite: 49].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** 11-point verbal analogue scale (VAS) (0 = no pain to 10 = intolerable pain) [cite: 49].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Not specifically distinguished [cite: 49].
* **Pain assessment time points:** In the recovery room at 10-min intervals, and at 4, 8, and 24 h postoperatively (visualized at 0h, 4h, 8h, 24h) [cite: 49].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group:** Visualized as median (IQR) in Figure 3 [cite: 49].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** Pain scores were significantly lower at each time point (0 h, 4 h, 8 h, and 24 h) postoperatively in the TEAS group compared to the sham group (P < 0.05) [cite: 49].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Remifentanil [cite: 49]. (Propofol also tracked) [cite: 49].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous via Target-controlled infusion (TCI) [cite: 49].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** Intraoperative Remifentanil = 0.06 (0.02) μg/kg/min; Propofol = 109.1 (15.8) μg/kg/min [cite: 49].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** Intraoperative Remifentanil = 0.09 (0.03) μg/kg/min; Propofol = 108.9 (18.0) μg/kg/min [cite: 49].
* **Measurement time point(s):** Intraoperative [cite: 49].
* **Between-group difference:** Remifentanil consumption was significantly lower in the TEAS group compared to the sham group (P = 0.01) [cite: 49]. Propofol consumption showed no significant difference (P = 0.95) [cite: 49].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Not explicitly reported [cite: 49].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes (Primary Outcome):**
    * Length of recovery room stay: TEAS = 35.6 (12.9) min; Sham = 48.3 (16.3) min (P = 0.01) [cite: 49].
    * LMA removal time: TEAS = 10.2 (2.5) min; Sham = 17.8 (4.4) min (P = 0.01) [cite: 49].
    * Reorientation time: TEAS = 14.6 (3.2) min; Sham = 26.5 (5.0) min (P = 0.01) [cite: 49].
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting and other side effects:**
    * Nausea: TEAS = 7 (21.2%); Sham = 15 (46.9%) (P = 0.03) [cite: 49].
    * Vomiting: TEAS = 4 (12.1%); Sham = 11 (34.4%) (P = 0.03) [cite: 49].
    * Dizziness: TEAS = 6 (18.2%); Sham = 17 (53.1%) (P = 0.01) [cite: 49].
    * Pruritus: TEAS = 2 (6.1%); Sham = 8 (25.0%) (P = 0.03) [cite: 49].
    * Sedation scores were significantly different only at 4 h after operation [cite: 49].
* **Length of stay:** The length of recovery room stay was significantly reduced in the TEAS group [cite: 49]. Hospital length of stay not explicitly reported [cite: 49].
* **Other relevant physiological/biochemical outcomes (Biomarkers):** Concentrations of adrenaline, noradrenaline, ACTH, blood glucose, and cortisol measured at multiple time points showed no significant differences between the groups [cite: 49].
