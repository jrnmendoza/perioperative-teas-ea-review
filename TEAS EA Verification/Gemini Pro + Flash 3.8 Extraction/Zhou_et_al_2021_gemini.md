## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** Not explicitly stated in the text [cite: 45].
* **Country:** China (Qingdao, Shandong Province) [cite: 45].
* **Journal:** Cancer Management and Research [cite: 45].
* **DOI:** 10.2147/CMAR.S292325 (derived from the URL in the article footer) [cite: 45].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** Chinese Clinical Trial Registry (ChiCTR1900022692) [cite: 45]. Approved by the ethics committee of the Affiliated Hospital of Qingdao University (QYFYKYLL201812) [cite: 45].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Unblinded randomized controlled trial [cite: 45].
* **Randomization method:** Randomized according to the order of a random number table generated by SPSS 21.0 [cite: 45]. The allocation was based on the time when patients signed the informed consent [cite: 45].
* **Blinding:** Unblinded [cite: 45].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** TEAS group (TG) and control group (CG) at a 1:1 ratio [cite: 45].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 96 recruited; 82 enrolled and randomized; 81 completed and analyzed (TG: n=41; CG: n=40) [cite: 45].
* **Age:** TG: 58.71 ± 9.274 years; CG: 60.83 ± 9.137 years (mean ± SD) [cite: 45].
* **Sex distribution:** TG: 27 Male (65.9%) / 14 Female (34.1%); CG: 28 Male (70.0%) / 12 Female (30.0%) [cite: 45].
* **BMI:** TG: 23.93 ± 2.823 kg/m²; CG: 23.93 ± 2.696 kg/m² [cite: 45].
* **ASA classification:** Excluded if ASA grade > 3 [cite: 45]. TG: ASA 1 (0), 2 (35, 85.4%), 3 (6, 14.6%); CG: ASA 1 (0), 2 (37, 92.5%), 3 (3, 7.50%) [cite: 45].
* **Comorbidities:** Included patients 18-75 years old proposed for radical surgery for gastric cancer (laparoscopy/robot) [cite: 45]. Excluded patients receiving neoadjuvant therapy; cTNM stage IV; current or previous diabetes; systemic infection; chronic constipation/diarrhea; cardiovascular/cerebrovascular accident; mental illness; cardiac pacemaker; local infiltration anesthesia; use of PCA after surgery [cite: 45].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Radical gastrectomy for gastric cancer [cite: 45].
* **Specific procedures:** Laparoscopy (TG: 27, CG: 23) and Robot-assisted (TG: 14, CG: 17) [cite: 45]. Gastrectomy type: Distal (TG: 30, CG: 35) and Total (TG: 11, CG: 5) [cite: 45].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** Epidural anesthesia-assisted general anesthesia [cite: 45].
* **Induction:** Not explicitly detailed [cite: 45].
* **Maintenance:** Not explicitly detailed [cite: 45].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Transcutaneous electrical acupoint stimulation (TEAS) [cite: 45].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** 41 analyzed [cite: 45].
* **Acupuncture points used:** Hegu (LI4), Neiguan (PC6), Weishu (BL21), Xiaochangshu (BL27), Zusanli (ST36), and Shangjuxu (ST37) [cite: 45].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** 20/100Hz (20Hz 5s, 100Hz 10s) [cite: 45].
* **Treatment intensity:** Set according to the actual experience of the patients, taking the patient's discomfort as the upper limit [cite: 45].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** Postoperative day (POD) 1-3, at 8:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. [cite: 45].
* **Duration of each treatment:** 30 minutes [cite: 45].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** Twice a day for 3 days (6 sessions total) [cite: 45].
* **Total treatment period:** 3 days postoperatively [cite: 45].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Enhanced recovery after surgery (ERAS) protocol performed on both groups [cite: 45].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** Control group (Usual care) [cite: 45].
* **Number of participants in control group:** 40 analyzed [cite: 45].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** Received the same ERAS protocol without TEAS [cite: 45].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs, flurbiprofen axetil) for mild to moderate pain, and opioids for moderate to severe pain [cite: 45]. All patients enrolled did not use PCA [cite: 45].
* **Dosage parameters:** NSAIDs used once on POD 1 regularly, then prescribed according to condition [cite: 45]. Opioids measured by equivalent doses of morphine [cite: 45].
* **Duration:** Measured from POD 1 to 5 [cite: 45].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** Numerical rating scale (NRS) in 20 levels (0.5 as a unit) combined with the Wong-Baker faces pain rating scale [cite: 45].
* **Pain at rest / movement:** Not specifically distinguished [cite: 45].
* **Pain assessment time points:** 6-time points a day from POD 0 to POD 5 (awake time, 8:00, 12:00, 16:00, 20:00, time before sleep) [cite: 45].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group (Mean ± SD):**
    * POD 0: TG = 3.58 ± 0.60; CG = 3.83 ± 0.84 (P = 0.105) [cite: 45].
    * Average of POD 1-5 (Primary Outcome): TG = 2.55 ± 0.21; CG = 3.10 ± 0.42 (P < 0.001) [cite: 45].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** Pain scores in TG were significantly lower on POD 1-5 (P < 0.001) [cite: 45]. No significant difference was found on POD 0 [cite: 45].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Opioids (measured as equivalent doses of morphine) [cite: 45]. NSAIDs (flurbiprofen axetil) [cite: 45].
* **Route of administration:** Not explicitly detailed [cite: 45].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** Equivalent dose of morphine = 8.935 ± 3.660 mg; Dose of Flurbiprofen axetil = 120.0 ± 12.82 mg [cite: 45].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** Equivalent dose of morphine = 12.83 ± 8.097 mg; Dose of Flurbiprofen axetil = 145.9 ± 13.22 mg [cite: 45].
* **Measurement time point(s):** Postoperative period [cite: 45].
* **Between-group difference:** The use rate of opioids was significantly lower in the TG group (43.9% vs 75.0%, P = 0.004) [cite: 45]. However, for the equivalent dose of morphine and the consumption of NSAIDs, the differences were not statistically significant (P = 0.182 and P = 0.117, respectively) [cite: 45].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Use rate of opioids: TG = 18 (43.9%); CG = 30 (75.0%) (P = 0.004) [cite: 45].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **Gastrointestinal recovery:** Time of first postoperative flatus (h): TG = 55.63 ± 16.74; CG = 72.60 ± 20.92 (P < 0.001) [cite: 45]. Time of first postoperative defecation (h): TG = 72.20 ± 16.24; CG = 95.78 ± 17.75 (P < 0.001) [cite: 45]. Comfort of semi-fluid diet (0-10 score): TG = 7.63 ± 0.63; CG = 6.93 ± 0.69 (P < 0.001) [cite: 45].
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** Frequency of nausea: TG = 1.88 ± 1.09; CG = 2.58 ± 0.77 (P = 0.029) [cite: 45]. Number of patients with nausea: TG = 16 (39.0%); CG = 19 (47.5%) [cite: 45]. Frequency of vomiting: 0 in both groups [cite: 45]. Frequency of distension: TG = 2.60 ± 1.27; CG = 3.50 ± 2.01 (P = 0.213) [cite: 45].
* **Length of stay:** Length of postoperative stay (days): TG = 6.12 ± 1.29; CG = 6.45 ± 1.68 (P = 0.478) [cite: 45].
* **Other relevant physiological/biochemical outcomes (Biomarkers):** 
    * On POD 3 and POD 5, β-endorphin (β-End), interleukin-2 (IL-2), and motilin (MTL) were significantly higher in the TG group than in the CG group (P < 0.001) [cite: 45]. 
    * 5-hydroxytryptamine (5-HT), interleukin-6 (IL-6), and tumor necrosis factor-α (TNF-α) were significantly lower in the TG group (P < 0.001) [cite: 45].
