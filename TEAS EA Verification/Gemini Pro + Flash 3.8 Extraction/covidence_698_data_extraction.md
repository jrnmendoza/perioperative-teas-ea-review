## Study details
* **Sponsorship/funding source:** National Basic Research Program of China (973 Program), No. 2007CB512503 [cite: 1].
* **Country:** China [cite: 1].
* **Journal:** The American Journal of Chinese Medicine (Am. J. Chin. Med.) [cite: 1].
* **DOI:** 10.1142/S0192415X14500682 [cite: 1].
* **Clinical trial registration/registration number:** Not reported [cite: 1].

## Methodology
* **Study design:** Controlled, randomized, double-blind study [cite: 1].
* **Randomization method:** Computer-generated randomized number table [cite: 1].
* **Blinding:** Double-blind; all subjects involved were blinded, and the doctor who observed the postoperative pain and other outcomes was also blinded [cite: 1].
* **Group allocation / study groups:** Group C (Control Group) and Group A (Electro-Acupuncture Group) [cite: 1].

## Study population
* **Number of participants:** 88 patients enrolled, 81 completed the study (Group A: n=41; Group C: n=40) [cite: 1].
* **Age:** Group A: 40.7 ± 12.1 years; Group C: 39.1 ± 10.9 years [cite: 1].
* **Sex distribution:** Group A: 17 Male / 24 Female; Group C: 21 Male / 19 Female [cite: 1].
* **BMI:** Excluded if BMI > 30 kg/m² [cite: 1]. Mean body weight was 63.6 ± 9.5 kg (Group A) and 65.5 ± 9.6 kg (Group C) [cite: 1].
* **ASA classification:** ASA I-II [cite: 1].
* **Comorbidities:** Patients were excluded if they had cardiovascular or respiratory diseases [cite: 1].

## Type of surgery / surgical method
* **Type of surgery:** Supratentorial tumor resection / supratentorial craniotomy (Meningioma, Glioma, Metastasis or others) [cite: 1].

## Type of anesthesia
* **Type of anesthesia:** General anesthesia [cite: 1].
* **Induction:** IV infusion of sufentanil 0.3 μg/kg, propofol 2 mg/kg, and vecuronium 0.1 mg/kg [cite: 1]. 
* **Maintenance:** Sevoflurane (2%) in oxygen at 2 L/min [cite: 1].

## Intervention
* **Type of acupuncture/intervention:** Electro-acupuncture (EA) [cite: 1].
* **Number of participants in intervention group:** Initially 45, analyzed n=41 [cite: 1].
* **Acupuncture points used:** LI4 (Hegu) and SJ5 (WaiGuan), BL63 (JinMen) and LR3 (Tai-Chong), ST36 (ZuSanLi) and GB40 (QiuXu) on the same side as the craniotomy [cite: 1].
* **Electrical stimulation frequency/parameters:** Dense-dispersed wave with frequencies of 2 and 100 Hz alternating every 3 s, using a symmetric biphasic curve [cite: 1].
* **Treatment intensity:** Set to the level of maximum tolerance for each patient [cite: 1].
* **Timing relative to surgery:** From the induction of anesthesia until the end of the operation [cite: 1].
* **Duration of each treatment:** Continued throughout the entire operation (mean surgical duration was 201.2 ± 38.6 min) [cite: 1].
* **Number/frequency of treatment sessions:** One continuous intraoperative session [cite: 1].
* **Total treatment period:** Single day (intraoperative) [cite: 1].
* **Additional/co-interventions:** Patient-controlled intravenous analgesia (PCIA) postoperatively [cite: 1].

## Comparator/control
* **Type of control:** No treatment (the acupuncturist did not insert any needles) [cite: 1].
* **Number of participants in control group:** Initially 43, analyzed n=40 [cite: 1].
* **Details of sham/control procedure:** No acupuncture needles were inserted [cite: 1].

## Analgesic regimen/common perioperative treatment
* **Analgesic regimen:** PCIA programmed with 10 μg/kg fentanyl and 8 mg ondansetron with normal saline for a total of 100 mL without basal infusion [cite: 1]. 
* **Dosage parameters:** Supplemental bolus doses of 1 mL with a minimal lockout interval of 15 min and a maximum hourly dose of 4 mL [cite: 1]. 
* **Duration:** PCIA was discontinued at 48 hours [cite: 1].

## Postoperative pain
* **Pain measurement instrument:** 10-point visual analog scale (VAS, 0 = no pain, 10 = worst pain) [cite: 1].
* **Pain at rest:** VAS measured at rest [cite: 1].
* **Pain with movement:** Not reported [cite: 1].
* **Pain assessment time points:** 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 24, and 48 hours postoperatively [cite: 1].
* **Mean/median pain score for each group:** 
    * 1hr: Group A = 3.7; Group C = 4.7 [cite: 1].
    * 2hr: Group A = 3.67; Group C = 5.8 [cite: 1].
    * 3hr: Group A = 3.33; Group C = 5.43 [cite: 1].
    * 4hr: Group A = 3.4; Group C = 5.4 [cite: 1].
    * 6hr: Group A = 3.1; Group C = 4.6 [cite: 1].
    * 24hr: Group A = 2.6; Group C = 3.07 [cite: 1].
    * 48hr: Group A = 1.9; Group C = 2.2 [cite: 1].
* **SD/IQR or other dispersion measure:** Plotted as error bars (SD) in Figure 1, but numerical SDs for pain scores are not explicitly tabulated in the text [cite: 1].
* **Between-group difference/statistical significance:** During the first 6 hours, the VAS score of the EA group was significantly lower than the control group (p < 0.05) [cite: 1].

## Opioid/analgesic consumption
* **Opioid used:** Fentanyl [cite: 1].
* **Route of administration:** Intravenous via PCIA [cite: 1].
* **PCA regimen:** 10 μg/kg fentanyl in 100mL, 1 mL bolus, 15 min lockout, no basal infusion [cite: 1].
* **Opioid consumption for intervention group:** Total fentanyl dose was 0.67 ± 0.09 mg [cite: 1]. Total PCIA boluses (0-6h) = 14.43 ± 10.67; (6-48h) = 8.30 ± 2.91 [cite: 1].
* **Opioid consumption for control group:** Total fentanyl dose was 0.73 ± 0.12 mg [cite: 1]. Total PCIA boluses (0-6h) = 24.87 ± 14.88; (6-48h) = 9.63 ± 3.21 [cite: 1].
* **Measurement time point(s):** Evaluated across 0-6 hours and 6-48 hours [cite: 1].
* **Conversion to MME:** Not reported [cite: 1].
* **Between-group difference:** Mean total bolus administration within the first 6 hours was significantly lower in the EA group than in the control group (p < 0.05) [cite: 1]. There was no statistically significant difference in the total fentanyl dose [cite: 1].
* **Percentage/relative reduction:** Not reported [cite: 1].
* **MCID:** Not reported [cite: 1].

## Rescue analgesia requirement
* **Number/proportion requiring rescue analgesia:** Addressed via the "effective number of PCIA bolus administrations" [cite: 1].
* **Effective PCIA bolus administrations (0-6h):** Group A = 4.83 ± 2.95; Group C = 9.67 ± 3.35 (p < 0.05) [cite: 1].

## Other postoperative outcomes
* **PONV/nausea/vomiting:** First day incidence was 14.6% in Group A and 32.5% in Group C (p = 0.058); Second day incidence was 4.9% in Group A and 10.0% in Group C (p = 0.379) [cite: 1].
* **Gastrointestinal recovery:** Patients in Group A were able to eat a sufficient liquid diet significantly sooner (first 24h: 51.2% in Group A vs 27.5% in Group C, p = 0.03) [cite: 1].
* **Recovery/quality-of-recovery outcomes:** Incidence and severity of dizziness and feeling of fullness in the head were significantly lower in Group A during 0-6h and 6-24h (p < 0.05) [cite: 1].
* **Length of stay:** Not reported [cite: 1].
* **Other postoperative complications:** None specifically reported [cite: 1].
* **Adverse events related to acupuncture:** None reported [cite: 1].
* **Other relevant physiological/biochemical outcomes:** Fluid administration, urine output, and estimated blood loss showed no significant differences between the two groups [cite: 1].
