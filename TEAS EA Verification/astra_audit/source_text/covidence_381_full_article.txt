Abstract
Objective: We aimed to evaluate the effectiveness and safety of preoperative electroacupuncture (EA) on the inci-
dence of postoperative nausea and vomiting (PONV), and severity of postoperative pain, in gynecological patients
undergoing laparoscopic surgery. The effects of EA administered at different preoperative time points were compared.
Methods: A total of 413 patients undergoing elective laparoscopic gynecological surgery were randomly allocated into
4 groups receiving EA the day before surgery (Group Pre, n           =              103), 30                           min before (Group 30, n                                                             =              104) or both (Group
Comb, n                                                                                                =          103), or usual care alone (Group Usual, n                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   =          103). All acupuncture groups had usual care. The incidence of
PONV and pain at 24                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          h were primary outcomes. Secondary outcomes included the severity of postoperative nausea,
vomiting and pain, requirement for antiemetic medication and quality of recovery (QoR)-15 scores after surgery.

                                                                                                             Interpretation of findings: timing of EA
                                                                                                             To our knowledge, no study to date has investigated the
                                                                                                             effects of two different timing schedules of preoperative
                                                                                                             EA, or compared one versus two sessions. Although we
                                                                                                             were not powered to examine equivalence, we found no
                                                                                                             suggestion that two sessions of EA were superior to one
                                                                                                             session of EA at any time point, and results were not sig-
                                                                                                             nificantly influenced by the timing of EA. Therefore, a sin-
                                                                                                             gle preoperative EA treatment is recommended. Based on
                                                                                                             our data, 350 patients would have been required in each
                                                                                                             group to provide definitive answers regarding the effect of
                                                                                                             EA timing. Optimal timing is also likely to be influenced by
                                                                                                             local factors including cost, availability of clinicians, time
                                                                                                             constraints and cultural acceptance. These considerations
                                                                                                             should be explored in future studies.
                                                                                                                  Gastrointestinal function is an important parameter for
                                                                                                             postoperative recovery and may promote the prevention of
                                                                                                             PONV, while PONV reduction may accelerate the recovery
                                                                                                             of intestinal ileus. EA has been shown to reduce the dura-
                                                                                                             tion of postoperative ileus compared with sham after lapa-
                                                                                                             roscopic surgery for colorectal cancer.41 Therefore, EA may
                                                                                                             reduce PONV directly, or indirectly by regulating gastroin-
                                                                                                             testinal function. Our study also supported the analgesic
                                                                                                             effects of preoperative EA, which was similar to our previ-
                                                                                                             ous feasibility study and the conclusions of several system-
                                                                                                             atic reviews.42
                                                                                                                  The side effects attributable to EA in our study were
                                                                                                             minimal and resolved spontaneously. This is an important
                                                                                                             consideration, since the combination of different analge-
                                                                                                             sics may lead to an increase in adverse effects. Therefore,
                                                                                                             preoperative EA may be a safe adjunctive antiemetic
                                                                                                             method.

                                                                                                             Limitations
                                                                                                             This study was limited to one hospital and patients under-
                                                                                                             going a short duration surgery, and our findings may not
                                                                                                             extend to other procedures or longer durations. However,
                                                                                                             the adoption of standard anesthesia and EA procedures to
                                                                                                             patients undergoing a common surgery enhances the repli-
                                                                                                             cability of this study. In addition, since this study focused
                                                                                                             on patients who were at high risk of PONV, the results are
                                                                                                             clinically relevant.

                                                                                                             differences between groups. The sample size was sufficient
