# Aggressive abstract screening: postoperative opioid-use outcome

Date: 2026-08-17

Scope: the 25 unresolved citations previously grouped as 18 Lund order-available records and 7 CNKI/Chinese-database records.

## Decision rule

Retain for full-text review only when the abstract or verified complete publisher abstract explicitly reports postoperative opioid consumption, opioid PCIA use/delivery/demand, or postoperative rescue use of a named opioid.

Pain scores alone, generic analgesic use, an unidentified PCA/rescue drug, intraoperative opioid/anesthetic consumption, PONV, cognition, biomarkers, and recovery outcomes do not satisfy the rule. Ambiguity is resolved toward exclusion because this is an aggressive screen.
